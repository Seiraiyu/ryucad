## Base and edge flanges (decided in ryucad phase 16)

The design is document 346 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 16.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **Files.** `base_flange` (`sketch`, `loops`, `thickness`, `reverse`, a bent section's `depth` and `symmetric`, and the solid's settings: `radius`, `allowance` `{k_factor}` or `{deduction}`, `relief` `{kind, width?, depth?}`, `none` written as nothing) and `flange` (`target`, `edges` each a reference or `{edge, side}`, `angle` in `(0, 180 deg]`, `length`, `measured` `outer_mould` written as nothing, `inner_mould` or `tangent`, `position` `material_inside` written as nothing, `material_outside` or `bend_outside`, `radius` and `relief` the founder's unless written, `setback` 0 unless written, `ends` `{vertex, setback?, relief?}`), feature format 11, `doc::sheet_metal`.
- **Base flanges.** Always found a solid (`combine()` is `new`). Without `depth`, the sketch's closed loops extruded by the thickness under the feature's `OpKey`, named as the same extrude. With it, the sketch's one open chain walked from its end first in natural order, the material on its left (`reverse`: right), offset exactly into a closed profile (`sheet_metal::section`): each sharp corner between two lines takes arcs of `r` and `r + t` (the inner radius where it falls), each arc keeps its centre, arcs meet what they join tangentially (within `1e-9` rad); keys `ryucad.sheet\0<part>\0<entity>\0inner|outer`, `…\0<vertex>\0corner_inner|corner_outer`, `…\0<point>\0end`, `…\0<first point>\0loop`, so sizes rename nothing. Either must recognize (`ryukei::sheet_metal::recognize`, the default tolerance) at its own thickness (`thickness_mismatch`, ryucad's reason, not a kernel variant, else; a plate narrower than its thickness is usually refused by recognition itself first, as `sharp_bend`).
- **Edge flanges.** Act on their target as a shell does: edges, sides and end vertices resolved by stable name; an end vertex sets the setback and relief at whichever end of each listed edge it is (an agent can't see an edge's direction); radius and relief from the founder's file unless given (`invalid_param` at `/radius` when no base flange founded the solid). One `ryukei::sheet_metal::flange` call; the request's cancel is checked before and after it (the kernel's sheet metal calls take no `Work`). Origins `{feature, flange_of: <edge>, part: bend | flange | cut}`. The input hash adds the founder's `radius` and `relief` by value. A new angle, length or radius renames nothing; a setback cuts the base anew, so the base's cut faces and the bend's end walls take new names, while the bend's cylinders and the flange's sides keep theirs.
- **Refusals.** `sheet_metal_failed` (new code): `reason` (the `SheetMetalError` variant in snake case; `not_sheet_after` with `fault`, `bad_size` with `what`), `kernel`, `entities` (role, reference, kind, origin, or a name), `hint` for `bend_loop`, `relief_needed`, `flanges_meet`, `not_a_flange_edge` and what isn't sheet metal. Kept in the disk cache as other kernels' refusals are (`failure_kept`). A flange's boolean is `boolean_failed`.
- **The report.** `sheet.get` (`ryucad sheet <part> [<solid>] [--rev]`, `schema: 1`): thickness, tolerance, `state` (`folded` in 16.A), `tree`, `cycle`, flanges' sides and bends (least inner face as `bend`, faces, radius, angle, direction, flanges, `developed_m` from the founder's allowance, `null` with a note where it can't be: no base flange, a deduction at a half turn). A solid that isn't sheet metal is `sheet_metal_failed`.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `allowance`, `relief`, `length` (or an extent of only a length: `--length`), `measured`, `position`, `setback`, `ends`, `sides`, and a base flange's `depth` (or a hole's blind depth: `--depth`); CLI `--k-factor`, `--deduction`, `--relief`, `--relief-width`, `--relief-depth`, `--measured`, `--position`, `--setback`, `--side EDGE=FACE`, `--end-setback VERTEX=LENGTH`, `--end-relief VERTEX=KIND[,WIDTH[,DEPTH]]`, `--no-ends`. A flange's target is the part's one solid, else its one sheet metal solid.
- **Git.** Textconv a line a field (`allowance = k_factor 0.44`, `relief = obround 1 mm × 1.5 mm`, `edges = … (side …)`, `ends[<vertex>] = setback 5 mm, relief tear 0.5 mm`); merges per key, `allowance` and `relief` one value each, `edges` and `ends` arrays; renumbering rewrites `sketch`, `target` and every reference.
- **Testing.** `doc::sheet_metal` and `sheet_metal` unit tests (sections against their areas, refusals, resolution, the founder's hash), `tests/sheet_metal.rs` (plates, an L, a U and a Z against their sections, an L flange against its closed form, a tray bit for bit the kernel's own call, names across every size with a hole on a flange, refusals, the report), `sheet_metal_from_the_cli` both ways, `sheet_metal_recomputes_as_from_nothing`, the `sheet_metal` and `sheet_metal_report` golden sections, the `feature` fuzz target's seeds and the `merge` target's `0xf0` mode.

## Unfold, refold and the flat pattern (decided in ryucad phase 16)

The design is document 346 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 16.B (phases 4 to 6).

- **Files.** `unfold` (`target`, `fixed` a side face of the flange held still, `only` the bends to unfold each by any of its inner or outer faces, `allowance` the founder's unless written, `bends` overrides `{bend, k_factor | deduction | allowance}`) and `refold` (`target`, `unfold` an earlier unfold of it with no refold between, `only` bends its unfold unfolded), feature format 14, `doc::sheet_metal::UNFOLD_FORMAT`; base flanges and flanges stay 11. Both act on their target; the request names an unfold's fixed face `fixed_face` (CLI `--fixed-face`), since `fixed` is an instance's.
- **Allowances.** `sheet_metal::allowance`: a K-factor as it is; an allowance as the developed length; a deduction `bd` as `2 (r + t) tan(θ / 2) - bd`, refused at a half turn (`invalid_param` naming the bend, hinting at its `allowance`) and where it eats the bend's whole length. `sheet.get`'s developed lengths come from the same function, bit for bit. A solid no base flange founded must give its unfold's `allowance`; folded, its `flat.get` and DXF give every bend its own.
- **Building.** An unfold recognizes its target, resolves `fixed`, `only` and overrides through the `SheetModel` (a face that isn't one is `invalid_param` at its pointer), and calls `ryukei::sheet_metal::unfold` under its `OpKey`; its `Made` carries a `rebuild::Flat` (the unfold, the kernel's `FlatMap` and `FlatPattern`, each unfolded bend's faces, whether it was partial), in memory and in the disk cache entry after the import report, written only when there is one, so no other entry's bytes moved. Its input hash adds its founder's allowance by value. A refold reads its unfold's `Flat` and calls `refold` on the solid as the chain left it; a name its target had keeps its origin, a face it split its parent's, and a new edge lying in the plane through a folded bend's axis at either of its ends is `{feature, bend_line_of: <bend>}`. A cut made in the flat across a bend line already splits its walls at the bend lines (the boolean keeps them), so its refold makes no new edge there and every name keeps the cut's origin. The request's cancel is checked before and after each kernel call. A bend loop's `bend_loop` refusal hints at a rip: a thin cut that opens the loop, then unfold again (the tests slit a flat wall of a closed tube). Writes check an unfold or refold whatever it references (`check_references`), so a refold of what isn't an unfold is refused when written.
- **States.** `sheet.get`'s `state` is `flat` while a whole unfold of the solid is in force (no refold of it since), `partly` while a partial one is or after a partial refold, else `folded` (`sheet_metal::unfold::in_force`).
- **The flat pattern** (`sheet_metal::flat`). Of a solid as shown: while an unfold is in force, `ryukei::sheet_metal::redraw` of the shown body through that unfold's pattern (cuts made in the flat show; the bend lines are the unfold's), and `fixed` or `bends` given then is `invalid_param`; otherwise the shown body recognized and unfolded whole in memory, nothing written. The session keeps the last 16 by the shown body's hash and the options. `flat.get` (`ryucad flat <part> [<solid>] [--rev] [--fixed REF] [--bend REF=FORM:VALUE]`, `schema: 1`; `--bend` is read by the same parser as `feature add … unfold --bend`, `cli::bend_override`): the frame, box, loops (each segment's kind, its exact data in millimetres, its edge reference and a polyline at the display quality's chord) and bend lines with their notes.
- **DXF.** `ryuformats::dxf`: ASCII AutoCAD R2000 (`AC1015`), built in memory, the same pattern the same bytes. `LINE`, `ARC` (counterclockwise; a clockwise segment written from its end), `CIRCLE`, `ELLIPSE`, `SPLINE` (knots, weights and control points as written), `TEXT`; layers `OUTLINE` (7), `INNER` (1), `BEND_UP` (2, `DASHED`), `BEND_DOWN` (4, `DASHED`), `BEND_NOTES` (7); notes `UP 90%%d R1 K0.44` (`BA<length>` for a developed length) 2.5 mm high above each line's middle; `$INSUNITS` and `$MEASUREMENT` from the unit (`mm`, or `in`); numbers as the shortest text that reads back. `export` takes `format: dxf` (or `.dxf`) with `solid`, `unit`, `notes`, `fixed` and `bends`; an assembly is `invalid_param`. The test reader is `crates/ryuformats/tests/common/dxf.rs`; `ryukei-bench`'s `dxf` test reads every DXF the tests write with ezdxf (devcontainer), audits it and checks each loop's area and bend line.
- **Renders.** `render --flat [SOLID]` draws a solid's flat body seen along its pattern frame's `z`, its `y` up, its bend lines dashed in `render::BEND_UP` and `BEND_DOWN` and, with `labels`, their notes, as the app's flat view will (16.C). `compare`, `frame` and `view` don't apply (`invalid_param`), nor a `section` or `explode` the request names; a participant's own planes and explode are left out.
- **Git.** Textconv one line a field: `fixed = plate/<hex>`, `only = f1/<hex>, f1/<hex>`, `allowance = k_factor 0.44`, `bends[f1/<hex>] = deduction 2.2 mm`, `unfold = u1`. Merges per key, an unfold's `allowance`, `only` and `bends` one value each. Renumbering rewrites `target`, a refold's `unfold` and every reference. `diff --model` gives an unfold whose result differs `flat: {from, to}`, each `{bends, box_m}`.
- **The bar.** ryukei's sheet metal bar enclosure (40 flanges, 2,000 holes) built through ryucad features and exported as DXF, on one thread (`tests/sheet_metal_flat_bar.rs`, release, `RYUCAD_BARS=1`, else ten times looser): rebuilt cold in 10.20 s against the kernel's own 6.86 s (1.49 times, bar 1.5), the DXF in 0.017 s (bar 0.5 s), on bc2 (cox-worker-3: Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus, 18 threads, load average 0.9 to 1.5, 2026-11-08). The holes are sketched 1 mm above the sheet, as the kernel's own cylinders run from below it to above: with their tops flush on the sheet the 1,500-hole feature took 19 s against 7 s and the rebuild 3.3 times the kernel's (the flush-tool cost of #77, on sheet metal). The DXF is of the folded enclosure before it is drilled: the refolded drilled enclosure doesn't unfold again (#606).
- **Testing.** `doc::sheet_metal`, `sheet_metal::allowance`, `unfold`, `flat` and `report` unit tests; `ryuformats::dxf` against its reader; `tests/sheet_metal.rs` (a tray unfolded and refolded, a slot across a bend line in the flat refolded and checked clean, a partial unfold, deductions against the K-factors they equal, a closed tube's `bend_loop` and the rip that clears it, names across edits, `sheet.get` and `flat.get` of each); `unfold_and_refold_from_the_cli` and `flat_patterns_from_the_cli` both ways (with `export --format dxf` and `render --flat`); `unfold_cut_and_refold_recompute_as_from_nothing`; the `sheet_metal_flat` golden section (bodies, `sheet.get`, `flat.get`, DXF bytes and `render --flat`, one thread and four); the `feature` fuzz target's unfold and refold seeds over `w`, a bent section in its base; the `merge` target's `0xee` mode; the `export` target's DXF of seeded flats (`0xd5`).
- **Limits.** No gauge or bend tables, no automatic rips. A part drilled across its bend lines in the flat and refolded doesn't unfold again (`flat.get` and the DXF of it refuse `uncertain` at a hole's wall, #606), nor does a hole centred on a bend-line edge cut in the flat (the boolean is `uncertain`). Bend directions are the kernel's, seen from the side it draws: the golden tray's four read `down`. A cut whose corner lands within microns of a bend line it doesn't cross is refused `uncertain` at the refold (the kernel's limit). Bend-line origins are found from the geometry, since the kernel's imprint names can't be traced back to their bend from outside it.
