## Booleans (decided in phase 5)

The design is document 32 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; these are its phases 4 to 6.

- **API.** `boolean::boolean(key, op, &target, &[Tool { key: ToolKey, body, place: Rigid }]) -> Result<(Body, Journal), Error>` unites, intersects or subtracts any number of placed tools in one pass; the tools act as their union. Refusals are `Error::InvalidBoolean(BooleanError)`: `DuplicateToolKey`, `OutsideSizeBox`, `Unsupported` (surfaces no intersector takes), `Uncertain` (an intersection, ordering or classification rounding can't decide) and `NonManifold` (kept faces meeting at an edge or a point only), naming faces by their names. `Error` is no longer `Copy`.
- **Pipeline.** `place` moves the tools (`Body::transformed`); `table` intersects every pair of faces of different operands whose boxes overlap, cuts every edge with the faces it may meet, and clusters every point found into global vertices (closer than the sum of their tolerances; a touching point reaches 100 tolerances, the touch rule, and snaps to an edge's end within that), never merging two vertices of one operand. Edges and stretches are carriers, cut at their vertices; carriers known to run together share them, and their pieces between two vertices are one global edge with a pcurve on every face that uses it. `split` walks each face's arcs in its parameter plane (darts ordered by angle about the outward normal, tangent ties by where they go a little way out, poles and apexes ordered by the surface's directions), lifts the cycles (stretches along a pole's line between chains count as crossings), and groups them: outer boundaries and holes by certified winding numbers, bands by where winding cycles cross a line across them, caps at poles and apexes. `classify` makes one certified ray cast (`intersect::point_in_body`) per group of pieces joined across untouched own edges and per other operand whose box holds its point; pieces on another operand's face are `On` it. The point steps off a piece's longest arc along the surface, never in its parameters (which may stretch one way far more than the other, or put a neighbour's pcurve a period round): at right angles by the first fundamental form, under half the 3D distance to the rest of the boundary (the arc's own other use excepted), so nothing it decides depends on scale. `select` applies the design's table. `build` grows each shell through Euler operators from the vertex rotations the kept pieces' fans give (MEV, `mev_reversed`, MEF, MEKR, KFMRH), builds a shell found to be a void again by MVS in the region around it (a lone shell is never a void), and fills tolerances to cover rounding at joints. `merge` joins faces either side of an edge on one surface (KEF), removes edges with one face both sides (KEMR, or MFKRH then KEF for a closed edge alone in two loops, where a tool touched a periodic face all round) and lone vertices no operand had (KVR), and joins edges on one line or circle (JEKV, sharing pcurves first). What an operand had and the boolean left as it was (an edge between two of its faces, a vertex between two of its edges) is never merged, so faces a file holds on one surface keep their names (#204).
- **Slivers and bands** (release 0.7.1). A stretch under 10 tolerances long whose middle lies on an edge of both faces is a contact, not a stretch: within the tolerances it lies along both faces' edges, so it can't say which carriers run together, and it clusters as a point reaching its ends (`Contact::reach`), so the corner is one vertex (#276: neighbouring blind bores whose bottoms meet, axes written to six decimals). A touch point on an edge of both faces joins what lies within ten tolerances of it: it may be a sliver the faces' box overlap clipped off a curve, which `surface_surface` gives as its middle; the vertex it joins grows only to cover the point (#478, v0.8.2: a copy's side leaving a box's bottom at 12° from a corner on its edge stayed in a few-nanometre overlap for 75 nm, and its middle, 38 nm off, was a vertex of its own). Since release 0.7.2, arcs leaving a vertex within the touch rule's tangent wedge of each other (`sqrt(2 t / size)` for a vertex of tolerance `t` on a face `size` across) are tied and ordered by their chords, as tangent arcs are, and a touch's reach is 100 times the edge's and the face's edges' tolerances, not a tolerant vertex's elsewhere on the face, which had grown a hundredfold with each touch made there (#280: boxes joined flush under a flange, each far edge's middle on its rim).
- **Parts placed in single precision** (#436, v0.8.2). A file's poses written in `f32` tilt a part by 4e-8 to 2e-7 rad, so a part standing on a board meets the board's plane at that angle, though over its footprint the two lie within a nanometre of each other. Two planes within `coincide::PLANE_TILT` (1e-5) of parallel, each within resolution of the other wherever it passes through the faces' region, are coincident faces there (`coincide::planes_within`, in `face_face`); a cylinder whose axis is that close to along a plane and whose side keeps within resolution of it through the region touches it along its nearest generator (`ss_exact::plane_cylinder`), where the exact section is an ellipse hundreds of metres long whose hairpin parted nothing. Only those faces' pairs changed: no golden hash moved. On the owner's NUCLEO board (689 occurrences, 1,181 pairs) interference left 313 pairs unchecked on main and 151 after.
- **Faces sunk into each other by less than a touch** (#558, v0.8.3). Two blocks with flush sides, one sunk 10 to 80 nm into the other across their touching faces (square, or both turned 30°), were `Uncertain`. Three rules: pieces of carriers not known to run together, between the same two global vertices that clustering grew past the carriers' tolerances, which lie within those vertices' tolerances of each other at their quarter points and middle, are one global edge, its tolerance covering their gap (`table::identify`, `alongside`: the corners clustered into one vertex each, but the top edge of one and the line where the other's bottom met its side stayed two edges the split couldn't order); in coincident faces' rims, an edge of the face on the other surface takes its exact pcurve on the common surface within its tolerance plus resolution, the gap coincidence already allows (`face::coincident_by`: turned, one resolution apart, a line had been fitted as a spline of thousands of spans that `crossings` couldn't settle against the other's line); and a kept shell whose pieces are all planar with straight edges and whose volume summed exactly over fans of their global vertices is zero (two faces sharing every edge) is left out before building (`build::enclosing_something`: `check` refused it as `NotOutward`, which a debug build asserted, already at 20 nm on main). So their intersection under a hundred nanometres is empty (interference says clear); 2 µm is the slab's volume. Exact bodies never make such vertices or shells: no golden hash moved.
- **Copies of one face in a file** (#240, v0.8.2). Two solids of one representation on copies of one surface (a gear's shaft in its bore, skirts glued face to face) are united by ryustep; the union's coincident rims now take a cylinder copy sitting elsewhere along its axis, its axis within `ANGULAR_RESOLUTION` of the other's, by its own pcurves moved by the turn and the offset along the axis (`face::turned`), and `intersect2::crossings` meets a curve and an identical copy over the same range as one overlap. No golden hash moved. Since v0.9.0 (#584) a cylinder copy framed the other way along its axis takes its own pcurves too, by a half turn of the parameter plane, `(u, v)` to `(α - u, d - v)` (`face::Turn`): two solids glued across a plane each held a copy of one 29 mm cylinder framed opposite ways (Voron 2.4r2's Z Belt Drives), whose rims fell to exact pcurves and fits and ended `Uncertain`. With the tangent rims of PR #640 (see Faces running on tangentially across a rim both carry), all four Z Belt Drives heal, 355 faces each (cox-worker-4, AMD Opteron 6380, release, four rayon threads, the four side by side, about 1,290 s each).
- **Faces running on tangentially across a rim both carry** (#584, v0.9.0). A file's two solids glued across a plane, a torus tube of one running on into a straight cylinder of the other across the circle both carry (Voron's Z Belt Drives), were `Uncertain`: as surfaces the two are tangent along the rim and cross again along a curve through two of its points, each beyond the other's face, and no trace settles that. Where a pair of faces of different operands stays `Uncertain` after the trace and `faces_apart`, `face_face` looks for edges of each lying along each other end to end (both closed, or ends within the touch reach, every point certified within a reach of the other by `within_reach`), with the faces' normals within a hundredth of a radian at the middle (`along_rims`). If one face keeps off the other's surface outside the touch rule's strip round them (`face_beside`), or both lie either side of the rims' plane (`beside::section_plane`, the self-intersection check's section rule, now shared), the faces meet only along those edges: each pair is a tangent stretch on the first's curve, placed on both edges, its other pcurve the second edge's own or fitted. Only pairs that were refused take the path, so no golden hash moved. On Voron 2.4r2's Z Belt Drive A (#11566) all four torus and cylinder pairs now pass (cox-worker-4, AMD Opteron 6380, release, four rayon threads, shared): the union is refused after 413 s rather than 1,835 s, on two copies of one cylinder whose axes run opposite ways, whose coincident rims `face::turned` doesn't map. The Y Chain Bed Mount (a cylinder and a spline blend across an ellipse) is the same form, untried here. `tests/tangent_unions.rs` (release only) unites the quarter tube and its rod to Pappus's volume, which main refuses `Uncertain` after 61 s.
- **Names.** Target entities kept whole keep their names; tool entities are `Placed` under a `Tool` name from the boolean's key and the `ToolKey`, so copies never collide and moving a tool renames nothing; split faces are `Piece`s named by their cutters, cut edges `Cut`s, new edges and vertices `IntersectionEdge`/`IntersectionVertex` by the faces meeting there, merged ones `Merged`, with an ordinal only where names still collide. `Event::Split` and `Event::Merged` are n-ary; `Event::Placed` records every renamed tool entity.
- **Guarantees.** Nothing is built until every intersection, split and classification has succeeded. Results pass `check::check`, `check_intersections` and mesh without faults (debug builds assert `check`; release builds run `check_topology` and report `Topology(Inconsistent)`). One thread and many give the same bits.
- **Testing.** `tests/booleans.rs` and `tests/boolean_corpus.rs` check exact volumes (a plate's hole, a lens, the Steinmetz solid, torus and cone cuts, flush, coaxial, tangent and coincident cases, cavities, refusals; holes and cross bores at a millimetre and a centimetre too, release only) and `tests/boolean_names.rs` the names and journal; `tests/boolean_props.rs` checks the set identities on random placed primitives and sweeps, in release only (minutes in a debug build), replaying the cases it found first; the `boolean` fuzz target puts primitives on a coarse grid (`ryukei_testkit::boolean_bytes`); golden hashes cover booleans (`booleans` section). In the devcontainer, `ryukei-bench`'s `booleans` test checks ryukei against OCCT's exact volumes, with SMLib beside them.
- **Performance.** A block minus 1,000 through-cylinders takes about 0.55 s on one thread, against OCCT's 1.1 to 1.3 s; single traced pairs (crossed cylinders, a cone and a ball) are 3 to 5 times slower than OCCT's.
- **Limits.** A traced branch whose points come out of order along the curve (seen near a cone's apex) is refused by the fit as `Uncertain`, as are the cases the intersection limits list. Equal crossed cylinders unite only to a pinch at their tangent points (`NonManifold`). The `boolean` fuzz target still finds results the mesher refuses: a cone, a block and a torus whose middle plane is the cone's base intersect into faces whose loop segments keep crossing however much they are refined (`BadDomain`; work item #532 has the bytes).

## Booleans on real bodies (decided in ryukei phase 16)

The design is document 444 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 16.B (issues #626, #606, #550, #436, #322, #585, #695, #696).

- **Tied darts ordered where they lie apart** (#626). `split` breaks ties between darts leaving a vertex within the tangent wedge by their chords at fractions of each dart, the nearest first. A stretch leaving a boundary arc tangent to it, whose start lies a rounding off the vertex, could be put on the arc's wrong side there. A hole's plug cut through a remade fillet does this: its top circle crosses the fillet's tangent line, the stretch dips below the circle as `Δy² / 2r`, and at 1.6e-8 m its start's 1.6e-10 m offset decided. When a face's darts don't walk, they are ordered again at common distances from the vertex, from the nearer of their middles inwards (`Arrangement::far_cmp`). Arcs of one face meet only at vertices, so where two are apart that order holds all the way in. Faces that walked keep their pieces.
- **A ray through a gap in a loop** (#322). A healed loop's consecutive pcurves may end apart within their edges' tolerances. KiCad's CP_Radial_D8.0mm top torus sectors are 4.9e-6 apart in v (2.4e-9 m). `point_in_face`'s ray from a neighbouring sector's rim slipped through and read the rim inside, so healing called abutting sectors overlapping. Loops within `TOUCH_TOLERANCE` keep being read from their pcurves alone, but their gaps (`closing`'s segments) are `Rays::joints`: a ray through one settles nothing, and another is aimed. Both cans now heal without a repair and check clean.
- **A file's union capped per face pair** (#550). ryustep unites a file's crossing solids through `boolean::unite_solids_with`. That is `boolean_with`'s union with each face pair under `work::Scope::Union`'s cap (`1 << 24`: 16 times the largest pair of a union that builds, by the caps' rule; see `kernel-work.md`). KiCad's L_Wurth_WE-HCFT-2012 traced one coil-against-lead pair for 23.5M units (26 s in all) before refusing. It now refuses as the file's `FacesOverlap` in 19.8 to 20.4 s on one thread (cox-worker-2, load 2). That is over design 444's 10 s bar, which is left to 16.D's record: by the rule the cap is still 70% of the pair that can't settle (question 495). A union no pair of which reaches the cap is the plain union's, bit for bit.
- **Triple points at a third face's corner** (#585). A stretch starting at a vertex of a third face, touching its surface there tangentially (a plane–plane stretch from a spline's corner in its tangent plane), left `curve_surface` a double root it couldn't certify. When the search fails and an end of the stretch lies at a vertex of the face, the stretch within the touch reach of the vertex is a touch and the vertex a hit. The rest is searched, and its hits within that reach are the same touch (`table::corner_trimmed`).
- **#606** was fixed on main before 16.B: ryucad's sheet metal bar now exports the refolded drilled enclosure's DXF.
- **Measured** on cox-worker-2 (AMD Ryzen 7 9700X, shared, load 2 to 8), release, one rayon thread a body, 2026-11-11: the D8.0 cans check in 2.8 and 3.3 s. Before, they refused `FacesOverlap` in 20 s on cox-worker-3. No golden hash moved.
- **Testing.** `blend_edits.rs`' `a_hole_moved_into_a_fillets_strip_at_every_position` (its lower volume bound allows the mesh's 1e-5, since at 0.27 the hole barely reaches under the fillet), `inface`'s `a_ray_through_a_gap_between_pcurves_settles_nothing`, `booleans.rs`' `a_files_solids_unite_as_the_plain_union_does`, `table`'s `a_stretch_from_a_faces_corner_is_trimmed_there`, and ryustep's `checks.rs` (`cp_radial_d8_cans_heal_and_check`, `the_wurth_inductors_union_refuses_in_bounded_time`, on the KiCad corpus).
- **Limits.**
  - Voron's molex body (representation 11774) still refuses. After the corner rule its self-union stops at a stretch lying on a spline's flat last span (u ∈ [1, 1.01] of #1789906), which needs coincidence by Bézier piece (#585).
  - The NUCLEO PCBA leaves 6 of 1,214 interference pairs unchecked. They are slivers 4.8e-11 to 1.02e-7 m deep, and a connector's near-parallel planes, all between exact bodies placed in single precision. The sliver collapse decided for tolerant bodies (question 424) doesn't reach them (#436).
  - NIST ctc_02 AP242's #1333 and ftc_10 AP242's #17968 are construction surface models whose sheets overlap or are non-manifold as written, ledgered `file` (#695, #696).
