## Booleans (decided in phase 5)

The design is document 32 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; these are its phases 4 to 6.

- **API.** `boolean::boolean(key, op, &target, &[Tool { key: ToolKey, body, place: Rigid }]) -> Result<(Body, Journal), Error>` unites, intersects or subtracts any number of placed tools in one pass; the tools act as their union. Refusals are `Error::InvalidBoolean(BooleanError)`: `DuplicateToolKey`, `OutsideSizeBox`, `Unsupported` (a non-analytic face that meets another), `Uncertain` (an intersection, ordering or classification rounding can't decide) and `NonManifold` (kept faces meeting at an edge or a point only), naming faces by their names. `Error` is no longer `Copy`.
- **Pipeline.** `place` moves the tools (`Body::transformed`); `table` intersects every pair of faces of different operands whose boxes overlap, cuts every edge with the faces it may meet, and clusters every point found into global vertices (closer than the sum of their tolerances; a touching point reaches 100 tolerances, the touch rule, and snaps to an edge's end within that), never merging two vertices of one operand. Edges and stretches are carriers, cut at their vertices; carriers known to run together share them, and their pieces between two vertices are one global edge with a pcurve on every face that uses it. `split` walks each face's arcs in its parameter plane (darts ordered by angle about the outward normal, tangent ties by where they go a little way out, poles and apexes ordered by the surface's directions), lifts the cycles (stretches along a pole's line between chains count as crossings), and groups them: outer boundaries and holes by certified winding numbers, bands by where winding cycles cross a line across them, caps at poles and apexes. `classify` makes one certified ray cast (`intersect::point_in_body`) per group of pieces joined across untouched own edges and per other operand whose box holds its point; pieces on another operand's face are `On` it. The point steps off a piece's longest arc along the surface, never in its parameters (which may stretch one way far more than the other, or put a neighbour's pcurve a period round): at right angles by the first fundamental form, under half the 3D distance to the rest of the boundary (the arc's own other use excepted), so nothing it decides depends on scale. `select` applies the design's table. `build` grows each shell through Euler operators from the vertex rotations the kept pieces' fans give (MEV, `mev_reversed`, MEF, MEKR, KFMRH), builds a shell found to be a void again by MVS in the region around it (a lone shell is never a void), and fills tolerances to cover rounding at joints. `merge` joins faces either side of an edge on one surface (KEF), removes edges with one face both sides (KEMR, or MFKRH then KEF for a closed edge alone in two loops, where a tool touched a periodic face all round) and lone vertices no operand had (KVR), and joins edges on one line or circle (JEKV, sharing pcurves first).
- **Names.** Target entities kept whole keep their names; tool entities are `Placed` under a `Tool` name from the boolean's key and the `ToolKey`, so copies never collide and moving a tool renames nothing; split faces are `Piece`s named by their cutters, cut edges `Cut`s, new edges and vertices `IntersectionEdge`/`IntersectionVertex` by the faces meeting there, merged ones `Merged`, with an ordinal only where names still collide. `Event::Split` and `Event::Merged` are n-ary; `Event::Placed` records every renamed tool entity.
- **Guarantees.** Nothing is built until every intersection, split and classification has succeeded. Results pass `check::check`, `check_intersections` and mesh without faults (debug builds assert `check`; release builds run `check_topology` and report `Topology(Inconsistent)`). One thread and many give the same bits.
- **Testing.** `tests/booleans.rs` and `tests/boolean_corpus.rs` check exact volumes (a plate's hole, a lens, the Steinmetz solid, torus and cone cuts, flush, coaxial, tangent and coincident cases, cavities, refusals; holes and cross bores at a millimetre and a centimetre too, release only) and `tests/boolean_names.rs` the names and journal; `tests/boolean_props.rs` checks the set identities on random placed primitives and sweeps, in release only (minutes in a debug build), replaying the cases it found first; the `boolean` fuzz target puts primitives on a coarse grid (`ryukei_testkit::boolean_bytes`); golden hashes cover booleans (`booleans` section). In the devcontainer, `ryukei-bench`'s `booleans` test checks ryukei against OCCT's exact volumes, with SMLib beside them.
- **Performance.** A block minus 1,000 through-cylinders takes about 0.55 s on one thread, against OCCT's 1.1 to 1.3 s; single traced pairs (crossed cylinders, a cone and a ball) are 3 to 5 times slower than OCCT's.
- **Limits.** A traced branch whose points come out of order along the curve (seen near a cone's apex) is refused by the fit as `Uncertain`, as are the cases the intersection limits list. Equal crossed cylinders unite only to a pinch at their tangent points (`NonManifold`). The `boolean` fuzz target still finds results the mesher refuses: a cone, a block and a torus whose middle plane is the cone's base intersect into faces whose loop segments keep crossing however much they are refined (`BadDomain`; work item #532 has the bytes).
