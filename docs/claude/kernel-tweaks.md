## Face tweaks (decided in ryukei phase 9)

The design is document 384 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.A (phases 1 and 2).

- **API.** `tweak::move_faces(key, &body, &MoveSpec { faces, motion, tangent })`, `offset_faces(…, &OffsetFacesSpec { faces: [(name, d)], tangent })` and `replace_faces(…, &ReplaceSpec { faces: [(name, surface)] })`, each with `_with` under `Work`: chosen faces take new surfaces (transformed, offset, replaced), their neighbours' edges and vertices are found again on them, and the body keeps every face, edge, vertex and name. Refusals are `Error::InvalidTweak(TweakError)`, before anything is built. The key names nothing.
- **The engine is the offset's.** `offset::classify::Plan` holds a `Motion` per changed face (`Offset(d)`, `Rigid`, `Replace`) and a replaced face's sense; `Plan::goes` (where an edge's point goes: on both faces' moved tangent planes) and `reach_of` (how far) are what `edges::closing` and `traced` read, the old expressions bit for bit for offsets. The tweak's own classes, carried curves, extended faces and vertices are in `tweak/`; the layout, naming, pcurves and checks are `offset::build`'s. The files stay in `offset/` (decision 2 of plan 9.A).
- **Edges.** Kept (nothing at it moves), carried (its faces stay, a vertex moves: its own curve carried past its ends by `intersect::extended_curve`, else `Unsupported { CarriedCurve }`), moved (one rigid motion, or one offset across a tangent edge: the offset's smooth path), closing (the branch of the new surfaces nearest where its middle goes). Faces tangent along an edge (`bend` smooth and within `TANGENT`, 1e-6 rad, at 17 samples) that a tweak would part are `TangencyBroken`; `tangent: true` grows the set across tangent edges in name order, each face taking its neighbour's motion. A sharp edge between faces offset alike closes, never fills.
- **Faces carried.** An unchanged NURBS, extrusion or revolution face whose edges move, where its box comes within three reaches of a bounded domain end, is carried past it exactly (`intersect::carried_by`), else `ExtensionUnsupported`.
- **Vertices.** One point each: unchanged, moved with one rigid motion or one offset (tangent all round), else a base curve (carried, then moved, then closing) met with a third face's new surface nearest the faces' moved tangent planes' least-squares point; every other curve there must pass within `MEET`, else `VertexSplits` (four faces or more) or `Uncertain` (three). New ends ten resolutions apart, else `EdgeVanishes`. A closed edge's two ends take one parameter, snapped to its curve's start where projection lands within rounding of its period's end, so the build runs it once round (a hole moved across its plate).
- **Special cases.** Every face moved by one motion is `Body::transformed`; no face's surface changed (a plane slid along itself), or every offset zero, is the body as it was with an empty journal. A replacement's sense keeps its outward side out at the foot of its middle (`Ambiguous` within 0.01 rad of square); a face with a laminar edge can't be replaced (`LaminarFace`).
- **Journal.** `Modified` only for faces with a motion, edges not kept and vertices that moved.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`tweak::{motion, classify, curves, extend, vertices, engine, tests}`, the offset plan's `goes` against `moved_point` bit for bit); `tests/tweaks.rs` (a box's top raised and tilted, a hole moved across a plate, a boss slid, an L's end offset, a rounded box's side offset with its chain, a top replaced by a tilted plane and a flat spline, a top beside its fillet, a pyramid's apex refused `VertexSplits`, a NURBS box's top raised carrying its sides, a sheet's top raised, one thread and eight); `tests/work.rs`' `tweaks_are_bounded`. No golden hash moved. Golden sections, property tests, fuzzing and references are 9.C's.

## Draft (decided in ryukei phase 9)

The design is document 384 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.B (phases 3 and 4).

- **API.** `tweak::draft(key, &body, &DraftSpec { pull, angle, hinge, faces, tangent })` (and `draft_with` under `Work`) gives the body, its journal and a `DraftReport` (each drafted face's certified least and greatest angle from its pull): a third element beside the design's `(Body, Journal)` (question 258). `Hinge::Neutral { origin }` (the plane through it square to the pull) or `Hinge::PartingLine(edges)`; `DraftFace { face, angle, flip }`. A positive angle tapers a boss inward along the pull and opens a hole; the angle is the face's final angle, not a tilt added. Refusals are `TweakError`'s, before anything is built: `BadAngle` (not finite, or its size outside `[ANGULAR_RESOLUTION, MAX_ANGLE]`, `π/2 − 0.01`), `NoHinge`, `HingeAmbiguous`, `HingeNotOnFace`, `HingeAlongPull` (within a hundredth of a radian), `LaminarFace`, `Unsupported { case: HingeChain }`, and the engine's. A parting line naming an edge the body hasn't is `NoSuchFace` naming the edge (no edge-shaped variant was added for it).
- **Hinges.** A neutral-plane hinge is the face's surface's meeting with the plane, the one branch coming into its box (searched for half the box's diagonal past it). A parting-line hinge is the parting line's edges on the face: one chain on one curve (a chain turning a corner on one face is `HingeChain`, question 257: ryucad divides the face there first); a face on the far side of its hinge from the pull drafts against it, `flip` reverses either.
- **Drafted surfaces.** A line hinge drafts to a plane through it, a circle about an axis along the pull to a cone (half-angle the angle, narrowing along the pull for a boss and widening for a hole), any other hinge to a `Surface::Sweep` of a straight profile along the hinge (carried half the face's diagonal past its ends, not at all where it closes) in the binormal frame of the pull. The ruling at every point is `cos θ d̂_p − sin θ ê`. The profile reaches twice the face's diagonal each way, halved while `Sweep::new` can't certify it regular, down to what the face needs along the pull (a tenth more than its box's farthest corner): a ruling of 0.98 m along a rational circle of 0.1 m wasn't certifiable, though the cone's apex is a metre off. A point of a drafted face goes to its foot on its drafted surface (`Motion::Draft { pull, angle }`, as a replacement), which chooses every closing edge's branch.
- **The engine.** A parting line's edges are `Class::Hinge`: kept where they are, carried along their own curves, a vertex taking one as its base first. Two faces drafted alike (equal pull and angle) tangent along an edge that crosses their hinge stay tangent: the edge is the ruling through the crossing (`Class::Moved`); one not crossing it is `TangencyBroken`. `tangent: true` grows the set across tangent edges, each face drafted about its own hinge at its neighbour's angle and pull. A traced closing edge (`offset::edges::traced`) on a spline edge is carried past its ends by three reaches, as a line is, when either face is drafted: a spline wall's top edge stopped a centimetre short of its corner. A vertex whose changed faces are all drafted and which comes back within resolution of where it was (on its hinge) isn't journalled `Modified`: found again on the drafted surfaces it differs by rounding alone. Offsets and 9.A's tweaks take none of these paths.
- **The report.** A plane's or cone's angle is exact; any other face's is certified over 8 × 8 cells of its parameter box, each the tighter of the enclosure of `S_u × S_v` and the mean value form of the angle's sine (its gradient's enclosure from the same jets), a cell wider than a milliradian quartered up to three times. On the spline wall's sweep (one angle all over) the plain enclosure gave 0.034 rad of spread, the mean value form 0.017, quartering under 2e-3 (the plan hoped for 1e-6). Every neutral-plane draft and every parting line square to the pull hold the angle exactly; a parting line climbing along the pull doesn't, and the report says by how much.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`tweak::draft::{surface, hinge, report}`, the classes and rulings, `tweak::tests`'s box and refusals); `tests/drafts.rs` (a box's walls about its bottom and middle as prismatoids, a boss and a hole as frusta, a sphere's band as a frustum, a rounded prism's walls with their corners by tangency, a parting line at a ledged box drafting both halves (a box united on a larger one: ryukei can't split a wall at its middle without the boolean merging it back), a spline wall's sweep against its report (release only, its own step in `full.yml`; 84 s in a debug build on cox-worker-1, an Intel Xeon E5-2650, shared, two test threads), names across angles, one thread and eight, refusals: a wall drafted past the opposite wall is refused `Uncertain` or by the engine); `tests/work.rs`'s `drafts_are_bounded` (342 units). No golden hash moved. Golden sections, property tests, fuzzing, OCCT references and the 1,000-hole bar are 9.C's.
- **Limits.** A parting line turning a corner on one face; a draft about a plane crossing a face twice; tangent drafted faces whose edge runs along their hinge; whatever changes topology (a wall drafted past its neighbours: `EdgeVanishes`, `VertexSplits`, `SelfIntersecting`, `Uncertain`), ryukei 13's.

## Hardening and references (decided in ryukei phase 9)

The design is document 384 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.C (phase 5).

- **Cases from bytes.** `ryukei_testkit::tweak_bytes::tweak_case` builds a case from bytes:
  - The first byte chooses a body as `offset_bytes` makes it, plain or blended. The next 24 bytes choose the operation, and the rest the body.
  - One or two of the body's faces take one of four operations:
    - a move: a translation along an axis by 1 to 20% of the body's least side, or a turn of 0.01 or 0.05 rad about its middle;
    - an offset, by the same steps;
    - a replacement by the first face's own surface, turned about the middle of the face's box;
    - a draft: a ±x, ±y or ±z pull; 0.01, 0.05 or 0.2 rad, either sign; a neutral plane through the box's middle or corner, or a parting line of one of the face's edges.
  - `tangent` is either way.
- **Property test.** `tests/tweak_props.rs` is release only, its own step in `full.yml`. It tries 64 seeds, and requires that:
  - every result keeps the body's names;
  - it passes `check` and `check_intersections` and meshes;
  - its volume is within mass's bound, plus twice the chord times the area, of its mesh's at a thousandth of its size;
  - a draft's faces' mesh normals lie within the report's angles;
  - one thread and eight give the same dump;
  - every refusal is `InvalidTweak`.

  On bc2 (Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus, `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=4`, load 0.5 to 1.0, 2026-11-07), 44 seeds make a case: 24 built and 20 refused in 34.7 s. The refusals were `LoneVertex` 5, `EdgeVanishes` 3, `SelfIntersecting` 3, `Uncertain` 3, `FaceCollapses` 2, `NoHinge` 2, `HingeAlongPull` 1 and `TangencyBroken` 1.

  `KNOWN_SLOW` is empty. `KNOWN_UNMESHED` lists seed 4, whose volume and angles aren't judged (#599). Seed 4 offsets a closed revolution's one face, which `offset_body` makes bit for bit alike. Its `Offset` face's box is about four times the base's, so the mesh estimate refuses it at a thousandth of that box.
- **Fuzzing.** The `tweak` fuzz target runs each case under `fuzz_case`. It asks the same of every result, except mass and angles. It is in `fuzz.yml` at 6 GiB with a 600 s timeout. A three-minute smoke on bc2 made 6,370 runs with no crash. An hour on cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, 8 threads, shared at a load of 7 to 13, 2026-11-07), from an empty corpus, made 15,392 runs. It ended with a corpus of 1,573 inputs, peaked at 987 MB, and found no crash, timeout or running out of memory.
- **Goldens.** `tweaks` holds a box's top raised and tilted, its top and a side offset, a hole moved, a rounded box's side offset with its chain, and a top replaced. `drafts` holds a box's walls about its bottom and its middle, a boss, a hole, and a ledged box's parting line, with the reports' angles. The offsets' sections and ryucad's goldens are byte for byte as before.
- **References.** `ryukei-bench`'s `drafts` test runs in the devcontainer with `--features occt`. It compares against OCCT's `BRepOffsetAPI_DraftAngle` (tangent propagation on) and `BRepOffset_MakeOffset` with `SetOffsetOnFace` (joined by intersection). OCCT's draft angle has ryukei's sign (`OCCT_SIGN` is 1). On cox-worker-2 (AMD Ryzen 7 9700X, 8 threads, OCCT 7.8.1, release, 2026-11-07), OCCT agrees with the closed form in all four draft cases and both face offsets: the box's walls about its bottom (0.227403012) and its middle (0.240053422), the boss (0.202836924), the hole (0.192371635), and the box's top raised (0.3) and raised with a side moved in (0.2619). Its drafted faces' angles are the 0.05 and 0.1 asked. SMLib has no draft and takes no part.
- **The bar.** The plate is the booleans' race plate (40 × 25 × 0.5, 1,000 holes of 0.3), every wall drafted 1° about its bottom, on one thread. Three runs with `RYUKEI_BARS=1` on the same machine, after other work finished (load 1.0 to 3.1, the test's own), ran with `--test-threads=1`. ryukei took 1.060, 1.025 and 1.024 s on one thread (0.34 to 0.36 s on all eight), against OCCT's 1.165, 1.173 and 1.170 s: 0.87 to 0.91 times OCCT's time. Both volumes match the exact 354.475695381 within the mesh's bound. On bc2 (Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus) ryukei's side took 0.979 s.
- **Fixes found.** None in the tweak engine. #599 (the offset mesher) is listed, not fixed here.
