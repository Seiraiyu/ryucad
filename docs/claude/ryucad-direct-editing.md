## Deletes and topology-changing tweaks (decided in ryucad phase 22)

The design is document 445 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 22.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **Files.** `delete_face` (`doc::edit::DeleteFace`): `target`, `faces` and `seeds` (each a list of `{feature, name}` in the target as the feature sees it, written only when not empty, at least one of them given, no face twice in either), and `heal` (`auto`, the default and never written, `cap` or `grow`). The three face tweaks gain `topology` (`keep` or `change`) and `blends` (`keep` or `rebuild`), held as `doc::edit::Retrim` and written only when set; `keep` is read and never written, so a tweak that sets neither is ryucad 17's, byte for byte. Feature format 20 (`edit::FORMAT`) holds a `delete_face` and any tweak that sets either field; `feature_format` and `Feature::format` read it, and `FEATURE_FORMAT` is the largest of every stage's constants.
- **Building.** `delete_face` joins ryucad 17's tweak family in `ryucad::tweak` (`Spec::Delete`; `tweak::TYPES` is the four, `doc::tweak::TYPES` stays the three): resolution, protocol sorting, reports, checks and `tweak_failed` are shared. A seed is checked as a face of the target when written; `ryukei::tweak::feature_faces` expands it only when the delete builds, so a feature changed earlier in the history is found again (the report's `seeds` says what each found). The kernel calls take `Topology` and `Blends` from the file. A budgeted call (`tweak::budgeted`: a delete always, a tweak only when its `Retrim` isn't keep) runs under the edit budget; a keep tweak runs as ryucad 17's, unbudgeted, with the same bits, hashes and reports. Origins (`tweak::edit_origins`) are `rebuild::replaced_origins` (a grown edge in a fillet's place is `{feature, replaces}`), then splits before merges whatever order the journal gives them: a split's child takes its parent's origin, and a merge's child its parents' when they share one (a parent being a split's child too). ryukei's re-trim splits faces it only re-trims and merges them again, merges first in the journal (#691), and those faces keep their origins. A delete takes a sheet where the kernel does (a cap builds on one); growth on a sheet is `not_a_solid`.
- **The inside-out guard.** A budgeted edit whose result's coarse mesh (1 mm chord, half a radian) has a negative signed volume is refused as `tweak_failed` with reason `inside_out`, its `volume_mm3` and a hint. The kernel's body checks decide winding only for planar faces with straight edges, and `measure::mass` doesn't read it, so a curved solid turned inside out passed both (#692). It runs outside the edit's budget, so `spent` doesn't move. A departure from the plan, accepted by the coxswain.
- **The edit budget.** `edit.budget`, a user preference (`config set edit.budget <units>`; `Rule::Int(1, 1 << 62)`), read when the session opens and before each request (`Session::set_edit_budget`, which lets results go as `heal.budget` does), carried in `rebuild::Run` and `assembly::Rebuild`. The default, `tweak::DEFAULT_EDIT_BUDGET`, is `1 << 24`: the smallest power of two at least four times `LARGEST_EDIT`, 3,249,017 units (ryukei's `edit_props` seed 70, a replace on 10 faces; ryucad's own largest is 29,957, `direct_edits`' groove run through), measured in a release build on bc2 (Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus, 18 threads, load average 2 to 4) on 2026-11-10; units are the same on every machine, and 22.B measures again with the committed corpus. A budget other than the default keys a budgeted feature's input hash (`ryucad.edit_budget`); the default keys nothing, so no result hashes differently. Past it a feature fails `edit_budget_exceeded` with `feature`, `spent`, `budget` and a hint, kept as any refusal (`failure_kept`). A budgeted call's kept report (`Made::report`) carries `spent`.
- **Reports.** `inspect --feature` and the rebuild report give a budgeted feature's `created` and `deleted` (counts of faces, edges and vertices, with references): names of the target before the feature against after it, not the journal's event kinds, so a split's children are created and its parent deleted, and a face the re-trim renamed (#691) is both. `spent`, and a delete's `seeds` (each seed with the faces it found). `feature.show` gives `topology` and `blends` always on a tweak (`keep` when not set), and a delete's `heal` always; it answers with the feature's fields at its top level. The rebuild report keeps a tweak's report off `import` (its fields come with `tweak::resolved`). ryucad 17's reports are unchanged, a draft's gaining `spent` only when budgeted.
- **Refusals and hints.** New reasons: `not_a_solid` (the kernel's `NotASolid`) and `inside_out`. `edge_vanishes`, `vertex_splits` and `face_collapses` hint `topology: change`, keeping the word "topology"; `self_intersecting`, `unsupported` `lone_vertex` and `uncertain` hint it too, only when the tweak keeps its topology (`tweak::hint_for`): the fixed-topology engine refuses a wall drafted past its neighbour as `uncertain` (#687). `tangency_broken` also names `blends: rebuild`. `gap_not_healed`, `heal_ambiguous`, `region_too_small`, `cap_not_on_face` and `grow_would_cap` gained or sharpened theirs.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `FeatureFields` gains `topology`, `blends`, `heal` and `seeds`; `--topology`, `--blends` and `--heal` are flags, and `--seed` (a pattern's seeds) is sent as `features`, which `take_given` moves to a delete's `seeds`. `tweak::request_fields` gives a delete `doc::edit::DELETE_REQUEST`. A bad `topology`, `blends` or `heal`, or a delete left with neither faces nor seeds, is `invalid_param` at its param (`retrim_given`, `delete_given`); a face of another solid is `targets_mixed`. `feature_faces.get` (`ryucad feature-faces <part>/<solid>/<name> [--rev]`) gives what a seed takes, or `tweak_failed` `no_feature`, `invalid_param` for an edge, an assembly's face or anything not one face, and `reference_body` (with the convert hint) for a reference import's.
- **Git.** Textconv prints `topology`, `blends` and `heal` a line each, and a delete's `faces` and `seeds` a line each. A merge is per key with `faces` and `seeds` one value each; a merge that drops both `topology` and `blends` writes ryucad 17's format again (`feature_format` of the merged value). Renumbering rewrites a delete's `target`, `faces` and `seeds`. The merge fuzz target's mode `0xe8` (kind 32) edits a delete's faces, seeds and heal and a tweak's topology and blends; the feature target has three seeds (a delete by face with `grow`, a delete by seed, an offset with both fields set).
- **Testing.** `tests/direct_edits.rs`: `a_wall_drafted_past_its_neighbour_is_a_wedge`, `a_boss_slid_over_its_plates_edge`, `a_groove_run_through_splits_the_top_keeping_its_origin`, `a_top_raised_past_its_fillet_makes_it_again`, `a_tweak_that_builds_kept_builds_the_same_bits_changed`, `the_edit_budget_refuses_and_keys_its_result`, `a_through_hole_deleted_by_its_seed_is_capped`, `feature_faces_shows_what_a_seed_takes`, `a_counterbore_added_before_the_delete_is_followed_by_its_seed`, `a_boss_with_its_foot_fillet_and_a_pocket_are_deleted_whole`, `an_edges_fillet_grows_back_to_the_box`, `a_reference_to_a_deleted_face_fails_naming_the_delete`, `a_reference_import_is_converted_then_its_hole_deleted`, `deletes_refuse_with_their_hints`, `deletes_build_alike_on_one_thread_and_eight`, `an_inside_out_result_is_refused`, and the ignored `a_drilled_plates_side_is_not_healed` (#692), each against exact volumes at millimetre scale. The `deletes` and `topology_tweaks` sections of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt` (every state, origins and kept report, one thread and four). `direct_edits_recompute_as_from_nothing` in `tests/recompute.rs`. `direct_edits_from_the_cli` in `tests/cli.rs`, both ways, with undo and redo. Unit tests in `doc::edit`, `doc::tweak`, `doc::files`, `tweak`, `config`, `diff`, `merge` and `renumber`.
- **Limits.** A seed's feature is the smallest connected set a cap closes (ryukei's `feature_faces`), so a hole made a counterbore after the delete was written loses only its bore, the counterbore's floor capping it; seed from the counterbore's wall to take it all (a departure from design 445's "the counterbore goes too", accepted; the `feature-faces` help says so). ryukei refuses a kept-topology wall drafted past its neighbour as `uncertain`, not a topology reason (#687). The re-trim renames faces it only re-trims, the sides round a groove run through among them, so references to them don't survive the edit (#691). Deleting a side of a drilled plate builds the hole's plug where nothing should close the gap: inside out from a cylinder's cut (refused by the guard), the right way out from a hole feature's, which only the kernel's fix refuses (#692).
