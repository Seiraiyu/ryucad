## Sheet topology (decided in phase 12)

The design is document 295 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 12.A.

- **Kinds.** A sheet's shells lie in the body's outer void region, so both sides of every face are void (Parasolid's laminar edges). `Body::kind()` reads it from the shells' regions: `Empty`, `Solid`, `Sheet`, or `Mixed` (a builder's passing state, which `check` refuses as `MixedKinds`). Nothing is stored, so the codec (`ryukei.body.1`), the dump and `compact` are unchanged. A sheet is manifold and orientable, open (laminar edges on its boundaries) or closed.
- **The checker.** Solids keep every rule and fault. A sheet shell's edge has one fin (laminar) or two of opposite sense, else `NotManifold`; each of its vertices is one fan of faces (`NonManifoldVertex`, sheets touching at a point); its Euler characteristic plus its boundaries (rings of laminar fins) is even and at most 2. `check_intersections` takes sheets unchanged.
- **Operators.** `euler::mvsr` moves a shell between the void and a solid region, its own inverse (a solid's only shell out, the region gone; a closed shell in the void into a new region; a solid with cavities `NotRemovable`, an open shell `OpenShell`). `euler::kfl` kills a sheet's face, leaving each edge it shared laminar and killing edges and vertices left with nothing, refused for a solid's face (`SolidShell`: make it a closed sheet with MVSR first), the last face, a split (`WouldDisconnect`) and a pinch (`NonManifoldVertex`); `euler::mfl` makes a face on a boundary (`NotLaminar` off one), its fins without pcurves. `vertex_edges` walks open fans; JEKV joins laminar edges, SEMV and MEF take them as they find them.
- **The builder.** `layout::build_sheet` caps each boundary with a face of its own, builds through the unchanged `Plan`, moves each shell into the void and kills the caps, so solids build the same bits.
- **Meshes and measures.** Sheets mesh as solids; `check_mesh` lets a laminar edge's segments have one use. `measure::properties` of a sheet is `Properties::Sheet { area }`; `mass` is `Error::NotASolid { op }`, a sheet holds nothing in `distance`, and `intersect::point_in_body` of a sheet is `Failure::Unsupported` (`Failure` has no kind variant).
- **Refusals.** `blend::blend` (until 12.F), `offset::offset_body` (until 12.E), `offset::shell`, `boolean::boolean` with a sheet as target or tool (until 12.D) and `measure::mass` refuse sheets as `NotASolid`.
- **Faces into sheets.** `sheet::extract(key, &body, &[Name])` copies named faces of a solid or a sheet into a sheet, `sheet::remove` the rest (none named: the whole body as a closed sheet); edges shared by copied faces stay inner, the rest become laminar (`Modified`), everything keeps its name and recipe, what is left behind is `Deleted`. Refusals are `Error::InvalidSheet(SheetError)`: `NoFaces`, `NoSuchFace`, `NonManifoldVertex`.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `topo`, `check`, `euler::sheet`, `layout`, `mesh` and `sheet`; `tests/sheets.rs` (measures, refusals, the codec); the `sheets` golden section.

## Swept and bounded sheets (decided in phase 12)

The design is document 295 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 12.B.

- **Bounded surfaces.** `sheet::from_surface(key, &surface, [[u0, u1], [v0, v1]])` is one face bounded by its parameter box, front along the natural normal. A periodic direction may run at most a period from anywhere; a period (within a part in 10¹²) is whole and has no sides, so the other direction's sides are closed edges. A bounded direction stays in the domain. A side on a row the surface collapses (`mesh::uv::collapsed_rows` at the domain's end) is no edge, its corners one vertex, and so is a side whose 17 samples lie within resolution of the row's point (that end of the box moved onto the row); one within twice resolution, the row's tolerance and its own, is `BadRect`; a cone's apex a whole turn leaves alone is a lone vertex, any other pole none. A whole surface must close up. Sides are exact parameter lines (planes, turned surfaces' circles, extrusions, and healing's seam curves, `heal::seamed::iso`), else fitted along the line (`intersect::fit_along`), its bound the edge's tolerance; each carries its exact pcurve. Names: `SheetRole::Face` from the key alone, `SideU0`/`SideU1`/`SideV0`/`SideV1` from the face, a `Corner` from the side the loop arrives on and the one it leaves by (one for a closed edge's vertex), an apex from the face; a new box renames nothing. Refusals: `SheetError::BadRect`, `Uncertain`.
- **Planar faces.** `sweep::planar(key, &profile)` is the start cap of a 1 µm extrude against the profile's normal, extracted: one face per lump facing along the normal, holes inner loops, named exactly as an extrude's start cap. A profile with chains or no loops is `SheetError::OpenProfile`.
- **Chains.** `ValidProfile::surface(profile, chains)` validates loops as `new` does and `Chain`s (`ChainKey`, one key space with loops; segment keys unique across both): `EmptyChain`, each segment's range and length, a whole period or an end within resolution of the start (`ChainCloses`), a gap after a segment (`ChainBroken`), and separation of every segment from all but its neighbours (a chain's first and last aren't). Chains are passed beside `Profile` (whose struct literal ryucad and the tests build in 74 places), not as a field; a profile made so `is_surface()`, and a profile can't hold chains otherwise, so no `MixedProfile` is needed.
- **Sweeping a surface profile.** `extrude`, `revolve`, `along` and `loft` (the first section deciding) take it unchanged in their signatures. Loops are swept as a solid by the existing code (`ValidProfile::loops_only`) and their faces copied without the caps (found by their names); each chain is a grid (`sweep::sheets::Grid`: stations of rails along the chain, stretches of sides and laterals with the motion, closed round a full turn, closed path or closed loft, with no rails where one stretch closes). Both go into one `Layout` (`sheet::copy`, `name_copied`) built by `build_sheet`. A side faces the chain's right in the profile's plane, as a loop's outward side does; its loop runs up the lateral at the chain's far end when the motion runs along the profile's normal, the other way otherwise.
- **Revolve and along rules.** A chain may end on a revolve's axis: a partial turn's rails share the vertex, a full turn makes an apex (`Apex`) or a pole (no vertex) as solids do. A vertex inside a chain on the axis is `PinchedOnAxis`, a segment along it `SweepError::ChainOnAxis`. Along a path a chain is never judged one to one: the whole sheet goes through `check_intersections` (`SelfIntersecting`, `Uncertain`); a chain's last lateral on a carried leg is its side's rail at the span's end (`Carried::lateral_at`).
- **Names.** A chain's segments are named as a loop's would be: sides `Side` (with the path segment along a path, every section's segment keys in a loft), laterals `Lateral` from the segments either side of the vertex (a loft's from the sides), rails as `CapEdge`s of `StartCap`/`EndCap` stand-ins of the chain's own (`Section`s between legs or round a closed path), vertices `StartVertex`, `EndVertex`, `JointVertex`, `Vertex` and `Apex` as the solid sweeps give them. Where a loop has the segment before or after, a chain has a `SweepRole::ChainEnd` stand-in from the chain and 0 or 1, so extending a chain renames only its old end's lateral and vertices. Loops' sides keep the solid's names. A loft's chain refusals name it as `LoopKey(chain key)`.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `sheet::bounded` and `sweep::profile`; `tests/swept_sheets.rs` (walls, part cylinders, bands, domes, cones, ribbons along bends and closed paths, a helicoid, lofts open and closed, loops as surfaces in every sweep, planar faces, refusals, names); the `swept_sheets` golden section; the `sheet` fuzz target (`ryukei_testkit::sheet_bytes`: faces extracted from blend-target bodies, bounded surfaces of their faces, planar faces, chains extruded or revolved), its seeded test in the testkit.
- **Limits.** Chains aren't judged one to one along a path, so every chain swept along one pays for the self-intersection check. A revolution meeting the axis at an angle is bounded with no apex vertex (only a cone's apex is one). Scaling or twisting along a path a chain that touches nothing still carries its laterals as fitted rails, as loops' are.

## Sewing and STEP surfaces (decided in phase 12)

The design is document 295 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 12.C.

- **Healing open shells.** `HealOptions::open` is `Open::Refuse` (the default: today's `OpenShell`, unchanged) or `Open::Sheet`. `heal::Shape::sheets` lists the shells a file gives as surfaces, in no solid; with `Sheet`, a piece of them heals into a sheet, open or closed (by what the file declares, not by whether it closes, so a closed sheet written and read back stays one), its edges used once laminar, its faces turned from its least-named face (no shell is judged by volume), built by `layout::build_sheet`; `HealReport::laminar` lists the boundaries. A solid's shell that doesn't close is `OpenShell` whatever the option; pieces of both kinds, or one joining a solid's shell and a surface's, are the new `HealError::MixedShape`. With `Refuse` surface shells heal as solids' did, the same bits. `ryucad` constructs `ReadOptions` by literal, so the option lives in `HealOptions` only (`ReadOptions::heal.open`), not as a field of its own: no ryucad code changes.
- **Sewing.** `heal::sew(key, &[Body], &SewOptions { tolerance, solid })` (tolerance from `LINEAR_RESOLUTION` to the cap `1e-4`, else `Malformed`; a solid given is `Error::NotASheet { op }`, new) gathers the sheets into one shape under their own names, sews it by healing's rules (7.C and 7.D), and heals each connected piece from healing's third step on, in parallel: closed and `solid`, a solid facing out by its volume; otherwise a sheet. One body per piece, ordered by least face name, so the input's order doesn't matter. Faces keep their names, sewn edges and joined vertices are `Merged` as healing names them, copies of one name left unjoined in a piece take parts of it (`Split`); the journal has no `Created`. A solid cut into its faces sews back to a solid per shell: each lump, and each cavity on its own (facing out).
- **Debug text.** `HealReport` and `Shape` write their new fields (`laminar`, `sheets`) in `Debug` only when they hold something, so the golden sections that record a solid's report or shape (`heal`, ryustep's `read`, `write_m`, `write_mm`) didn't move.
- **STEP.** ryustep decodes `FACE_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` (`Brep::Faces`) and `CONNECTED_FACE_SET` (`Entity::FaceSet`) beside `SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL`, and gives both models' shells to `Shape::sheets`; `Imported::kind()` says what a body is. A representation holding solids and surface models together is one body as before, `MixedShape` with `Sheet` (no committed file has one; the design's reading them apart is left until a file needs it). `heal::seamed` lists a sheet's shells (closed where none of their edges is laminar), and `ryustep::write` writes a sheet as one `SHELL_BASED_SURFACE_MODEL` of an `OPEN_SHELL` (or `CLOSED_SHELL`) per piece in a `MANIFOLD_SURFACE_SHAPE_REPRESENTATION`; sheets go round bit for bit, as solids do. The corpus's two NIST PMI surface models heal with `Sheet` (one face each; `manifest.txt`'s `sheet <faces> <laminar>`), and `ryukei-bench`'s `step` test reads the pinned corpus's open shells again as sheets, against `pinned.txt`'s `sheets <healed> <path>` lines, recorded by the devcontainer run that executes this plan.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `heal` (open boxes, closed sheets, mixed shapes, a solid that doesn't close) and `heal::sewing` (a block and a cylinder sewn back, closed and open sheets, order, refusals); ryustep's `read.rs`, `write.rs` and `corpus.rs`; `tests/sheet_props.rs` (release only, its own step in `full.yml`: seeded sweeps, booleans and blends cut into faces and halves and sewn back to their shells, counts, names and certified volumes); golden sections `sewing` (ryukei) and `sheets` (ryustep's `step.txt`); `sheet_bytes`' fifth kind (faces nudged and sewn) and the `step` fuzz target reading with `Sheet`.
- **The sewing race.** `ryukei-bench`'s `sewing` test (devcontainer, `--features occt`): the race's 1,600 bodies and a plate with 1,000 holes, each cut into faces moved by up to a tenth of the cap, sewn back body by body (one refused piece refuses its whole `sew` call), every body at once on all cores, by ryukei and by OCCT's `BRepBuilderAPI_Sewing` (the shim's `sew`, closed shells made solids by `ShapeFix_Solid`); every body given back (a solid per shell, its counts and certified volume), a refusal only where `REFUSED` lists it with its issue, and ryukei faster (`RYUKEI_BARS=1` as is, else ten times looser). Bodies with a face that touches itself at a vertex can't be cut into single-face sheets (`NonManifoldVertex`) and are left out, counted. While planning, race body 15 (a half torus between two cones) was refused `Uncertain` once nudged: its torus face's loops lie a few µm off the seam rows, the mesher calls it `BadDomain` at every chord, so shell facing has no volume; it sews unmoved.
