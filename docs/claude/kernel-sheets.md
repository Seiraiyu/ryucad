## Sheet topology (decided in phase 12)

The design is document 295 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 12.A.

- **Kinds.** A sheet's shells lie in the body's outer void region, so both sides of every face are void (Parasolid's laminar edges). `Body::kind()` reads it from the shells' regions: `Empty`, `Solid`, `Sheet`, or `Mixed` (a builder's passing state, which `check` refuses as `MixedKinds`). Nothing is stored, so the codec (`ryukei.body.1`), the dump and `compact` are unchanged. A sheet is manifold and orientable, open (laminar edges on its boundaries) or closed.
- **The checker.** Solids keep every rule and fault. A sheet shell's edge has one fin (laminar) or two of opposite sense, else `NotManifold`; each of its vertices is one fan of faces (`NonManifoldVertex`, sheets touching at a point); its Euler characteristic plus its boundaries (rings of laminar fins) is even and at most 2. `check_intersections` takes sheets unchanged.
- **Operators.** `euler::mvsr` moves a shell between the void and a solid region, its own inverse (a solid's only shell out, the region gone; a closed shell in the void into a new region; a solid with cavities `NotRemovable`, an open shell `OpenShell`). `euler::kfl` kills a sheet's face, leaving each edge it shared laminar and killing edges and vertices left with nothing, refused for a solid's face (`SolidShell`: make it a closed sheet with MVSR first), the last face, a split (`WouldDisconnect`) and a pinch (`NonManifoldVertex`); `euler::mfl` makes a face on a boundary (`NotLaminar` off one), its fins without pcurves. `vertex_edges` walks open fans; JEKV joins laminar edges, SEMV and MEF take them as they find them.
- **The builder.** `layout::build_sheet` caps each boundary with a face of its own, builds through the unchanged `Plan`, moves each shell into the void and kills the caps, so solids build the same bits.
- **Meshes and measures.** Sheets mesh as solids; `check_mesh` lets a laminar edge's segments have one use. `measure::properties` of a sheet is `Properties::Sheet { area }`; `mass` is `Error::NotASolid { op }`, a sheet holds nothing in `distance`, and `intersect::point_in_body` of a sheet is `Failure::Unsupported` (`Failure` has no kind variant).
- **Refusals.** `blend::blend` (until 12.F), `offset::offset_body` (until 12.E), `offset::shell`, `boolean::boolean` with a sheet as target or tool (until 12.D) and `measure::mass` refuse sheets as `NotASolid`.
- **Faces into sheets.** `sheet::extract(key, &body, &[Name])` copies named faces of a solid or a sheet into a sheet, `sheet::remove` the rest (none named: the whole body as a closed sheet); edges shared by copied faces stay inner, the rest become laminar (`Modified`), everything keeps its name and recipe, what is left behind is `Deleted`. Refusals are `Error::InvalidSheet(SheetError)`: `NoFaces`, `NoSuchFace`, `NonManifoldVertex`.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `topo`, `check`, `euler::sheet`, `layout`, `mesh` and `sheet`; `tests/sheets.rs` (measures, refusals, the codec); the `sheets` golden section.
