## The window (decided in ryuplm 1.E)

The design is document 938 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei` (phases 9 to 11); this is its plan 1.E (document 980), with story 14555 (the Documents tab) moved from ryuplm 3.B.

- **Its own crate.** `crates/ryuplm-app` is ryuplm's window on the shared shell `ryu-app`, a client of the catalogue's daemon (question 1216: never inside ryucad-app). It depends on `ryuplm` with `default-features = false` (no `cad`: the daemon does the reading), `ryu-app`, `ryu-session` and `ryu-doc`, and never on ryucad, ryueda, the kernel or another window (`tests/deps.rs`; CI's `ryuplm-core` job checks `cargo tree`). `core` is the window without egui, tested headless; `ui` is a thin egui view over it.
- **It watches, points and judges** (question 1217): it writes nothing but Undo and Redo. An empty tile shows the CLI command that would fill it, with Copy.
- **Tiles.** Navigator (left: classes, states, items with errors or warnings, import mappings, each with its count, each filtering Items), Items (centre), Item (right: fields, declared properties with their types, the AML preferred first, representations with Open, previous numbers, provenance, its findings), and BOM, Where used, Findings, Changes, Releases, Imports, Documents and Activity below. Every tile has its tab (`all_panes_must_have_tabs`).
- **Readers** (`core::reader`, `Window::want`). Each tile's answer is one reader with one request in flight: asked again while one is out, it is sent once more when that one is answered, so a burst of `project.changed` (a large import) is one round per reader. A tile's readers are first asked the first frame it is drawn (`Window::shows`) and on every `project.changed` after. `check`, `bom.where_used` and `reps.list` go on the aside connection. `Window::asks` counts sends, for the tests.
- **The Items list** (`core::rows`) is windows of `item.list`: the rows in view and `AHEAD` (200) each side, one ask in flight, asked again when the filter, sort or catalogue revision moves; the old rows stay shown until the new arrive. Counts in the Navigator are `item.list` with `limit: 0`.
- **Marks** (`core::marks`): this window's selection, hover, others' selections in their colours, highlights in their style's colour, then a changed flash (2 s, `ui.flash_changes`), drawn as a stripe at the row's left edge and a tint. Colours are held to the tile's background by `ryu_app::style::held` (moved there from ryueda-app's `core::paint` by this plan, question 1278): 3:1, the selection 4.5:1, the hover 7.5:1. Only glyphs the shell's fonts have are drawn (findings as `2 err 1 warn`).
- **Pointing** (`core::pointing`): the selection and view (`item`, `tab`, `list`) go to the daemon at most every 150 ms, the last winning; a participant's view is adopted when it moved and is the window's own Go to or (with `ui.follow_shows`) another's show, never an echo of its last eight `view.set`s; the People strip follows another participant's item, tab and list.
- **A lost daemon** greys every tile and says so in the status line; the link reconnects from 250 ms, doubling to 5 s, and every reader is asked again.
- **Documents** (story 14555): the open item's documents (`item.show`'s `documents`, each this catalogue's read by `document.show`), or with Every document `document.list` (text search, 200 at most). Open writes the bytes to `<temp>/ryuplm-app-<pid>/<ID>/<name>`: a Git-stored document's through the window's own `document.get`, a service-stored one's through `ryuplm --no-daemon document get` (the CLI fetches service bytes, ryuplm 3.B), then the OS's opener (`xdg-open`, `open`, `cmd /c start`), or `RYUPLM_OPENER` (tests).
- **Representations' Open** runs `ryucad open` or `ryueda open` on the folder `reps.list` found, when that program is installed (`core::launch`).
- **Preferences** are ryuplm's schema's `ui.*` and `app.window`, in the user's file.
- **Binary.** `ryuplm-app [DIR]`; `--version` prints the CLI's envelope; `--smoke [DIR]` opens the catalogue, waits for its first rows and draws two frames offscreen.
- **Testing.**
  - Unit tests in `core` (rows, readers, lists, marks, flash, pointing, window, documents, opener, launch, command, product, people) and `ui::tiles`, `ui::app`.
  - `tests/cli.rs` (the command line, and `--smoke` on a seeded catalogue), `tests/window.rs` against real daemons (the list and a search, a pick read by an agent, an agent's show adopted and not with follow off, a finding's Show, Undo, an import as one round, a lost daemon found again, a document opened), `tests/snapshot.rs` (the window in both themes on lavapipe, `UPDATE_SNAPSHOTS=1`, and every control named), `tests/frames.rs` (the bars, ignored).
  - The tests run `target/<profile>/ryuplm`, built if stale, or as given (`RYUPLM_TEST_PREBUILT`). They skip without an adapter unless `RYUPLM_REQUIRE_GPU` (or `RYUCAD_REQUIRE_GPU`) is set; they draw one at a time (a lock in one process, nextest's `window` group across processes). Their folder is a repository of its own, and each seeded catalogue is `ryuplm::testing`'s files with `ryuplm init`'s project ID.
  - CI runs `--test window` in a job of its own; `--test snapshot` and `--test cli` in the workspace partitions.
- **Bars** (design 938; `tests/frames.rs`, hardware adapters, `RYUPLM_BARS=1`): the first rows within 2 s of opening a 100,000-item catalogue on a warm daemon, a scrolled frame within 16.7 ms (median), a search's rows within 100 ms of the last key, a pick seen by an agent within 200 ms. Not yet measured: they need a hardware adapter (plan 1.E Task 31).
- **Shipping.** See `ryuplm.md`'s live session section: ryuplm's archives carry the app from v0.2.0 (question 1219).
- **Commands.**
  - `cargo run -p ryuplm-app -- <catalogue folder>` (`cargo build -p ryuplm` first; it needs `ryuplm` beside it).
  - `RYUPLM_REQUIRE_GPU=1 cargo test -p ryuplm-app -- --test-threads=1`.
  - `UPDATE_SNAPSHOTS=1 cargo test -p ryuplm-app --test snapshot`.
  - `RYUPLM_BARS=1 cargo test -p ryuplm-app --release --test frames -- --ignored --nocapture` (hardware adapters).
  - `sh scripts/smoke-ryuplm.sh target/release/ryuplm target/release/ryuplm-app`.
