## Solids (decided in ryucad phase 4)

The design is document 59 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 4.A.

- **Solids.** A feature whose `operation` is `new` (primitives, extrudes and revolves; the default, written as nothing) founds a solid named by its own ID. `join`, `cut` and `intersect` name their `targets` (solids by founder, at least one, each once; always written, `feature.add` filling in the part's one solid when left out) and apply the feature's own body to each with one ryukei boolean. A `boolean` feature (`op` `unite`/`subtract`/`intersect`, `target`, `tools`) combines whole solids and consumes its tools: nothing acts on or refers to them after it (`body_consumed`). `solid::Solids` gives each solid's chain (founder, then each feature acting on it, in list order), a feature's view of a solid (the last of its chain listed before it), and the tips the model shows, in list order. These fields are feature format 3; files without them don't change.
- **Building.** A feature reads each solid as its view (`graph::reads`; `graph::inputs` is what it names, for order and deletion), so a solid's chain builds in order and separate solids side by side. Output is `rebuild::Output::Solids(Made)`: the solid a feature founds, or each it acts on as it leaves it; one solid's hash is its body's, so older files hash as they did. A join, cut or intersect that leaves nothing of its tool and takes nothing of its target is `operation_no_effect`; a result with no faces `body_emptied`; a kernel refusal `boolean_failed`, its faces as the target's references or the tool's entities with their origins. A failure fails the rest of its solid's chain (`dependency_failed`). Tools are `ToolKey`s from `ryucad.tool\0<part>\0<feature>` (a boolean's per solid, `…\0<solid>`), so a tool's entities are named by the feature that made them. Every entity's origin names its feature. `through_all` extents reach a hundredth of the targets' depth past their boxes, one way or `both`; only with `cut` or `intersect`, refused when all the targets lie behind the sketch.
- **References.** `{feature, name}` names a solid by its founder; the name is looked up in the reader's view. A non-founder is `reference_wrong_kind` with `solids`; a name that is gone is `reference_unresolved` with `removed_by`, the feature of the chain that took it (or the feature that made it and makes it no more: its sketch drawn again), read by the session from the rebuild it explains (never baked into the cached error).
- **Cache.** `rebuild::Cache` holds each feature's result by its inputs' hash and each join, cut or intersect's own body by its own inputs (the feature less its operation and targets, its reads less its targets unless it goes through all), kept even when applying it fails: moving a target rebuilds the operation from the cached body.
- **The model.** A solid shows as its tip left it, or, when the tip failed, as the last feature of its chain that built (and is reported failed). Rebuild reports list `solids` (founder, chain, `ok`, the feature `shown`, hash, counts, error) and give each feature its `solids`, `operation` and a boolean's `consumes`. Meshes, renders, framing, `inspect` (the model, or any feature's states with `feature`) and entity references (`<part>/<founder>/<name>`) are the solids'; a selection's unresolved references say why (`errors`).
- **Git.** `diff --model` adds each solid that differs (`solids`). Renumbering a collided feature rewrites `targets`, `target` and `tools`, and maps names per solid across every state a reference may read (a name mapping two ways means no repair). `merge_broke` blames through views, and explains its errors (`was`, `removed_by`). `check` checks each solid as it shows, once (`solids` in its report).
- **App.** Meshes are keyed by solid. The tree lists each solid with its chain, a consumed solid under the boolean that consumed it, sketches where listed, each row with its operation. Choosing a feature frames its solid and paints the faces it made (from `inspect` origins, read again as its part changes). The property panel edits operations, targets, extents and booleans (`edit::{Choice, Picks, Flag, ExtentForm}`), offering the part's founders.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module, `tests/cli.rs` (`solids_from_the_cli`, both ways), the `solids` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt` and `solids_are_sound`, `tests/recompute.rs` with a cut and a boolean, the `feature` fuzz target after two boxes (operations seeded), and the app's `tests/solids.rs` and window test against real daemons.

## Fillets and chamfers (decided in ryucad phase 4)

The design is document 59 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 4.B.

- **Files.** `fillet` (`groups: [{radius, edges}]`) and `chamfer` (`groups: [{form, edges, …}]`: `equal` with `d`, `distances` with `d1` along `face` and `d2`, `distance_angle` with `d` along `face` and `angle` in `(0, 90 deg]`) are feature format 3 (`doc::blend`). Edges and faces are references (`{feature, name}`, the feature a solid's founder); sizes are expressions; `propagate` is true unless written `false`. A feature's solid is the one its references name (`Feature::targets`); several is `targets_mixed`. Reference roles are `groups.<i>.edges.<j>` and `groups.<i>.face`.
- **Building.** Each listed edge resolves in the feature's view of its solid (an edge, else `reference_wrong_kind`), grows to `blend::tangent_chain` when it propagates, and joins its group; an edge reached from two groups is `edge_listed_twice` (twice in one group, once). One `ryukei::blend::blend` call keyed by the feature's `OpKey`. A blend face's origin is `{feature, replaces}`: the names of the edges and corner vertices it took the place of, sorted; other new entities `{feature}`; the rest keep theirs.
- **Refusals.** The kernel's are `blend_failed` with `reason` (the `BlendError` variant in snake case: `overflow`, `radius_too_large`, `open_tangent_chain`, `not_a_face`, `unsupported`, …), `kernel`, and `entities`: each name it gives with its `role` in the refusal, as `<part>/<solid>/<name>` with its `kind`, `origin` and, for an edge, the listed edge whose chain reached it (`listed`).
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `groups` whole (edges and faces as `<part>/<feature>/<name>`), or one group's fields: `radius`, or `form`, `d`, `d1`, `d2`, `angle`, `face`; and `edges`, `propagate` (CLI `--radius`, `--form`, `--d`, `--d1`, `--d2`, `--angle`, `--face`, `--edge` repeated, `--propagate`, `--groups JSON`). One group's fields edit a feature of one group only; a new chamfer is `equal`; a new form keeps only what it takes. Writes refuse (`rebuild::check_blend`, against the current rebuild) mixed solids, edges that aren't, faces that aren't, edges in two groups, names gone (`reference_unresolved` with `removed_by`); what the kernel makes of them only rebuild tells.
- **Git.** Textconv lists a group's edges on one line; a merge takes `groups` as one value. Renumbering walks arrays, and pairs names round by round, so a fillet of a renumbered solid builds with its edges' new names.
- **App.** The form shows each group's form, face and sizes (editable) and its edges, each a checkbox to take it out (never to add one), and `propagate`; a save sends every group. Choosing a fillet paints its faces (by origin).
- **Testing and limits.** Unit tests in `doc::blend`, `rebuild` and `server`; `tests/blends.rs` (every box edge rounded and bevelled, a slot's rim from one edge, a boss's foot, against exact volumes; `check` clean); the `blends` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt` and `blended_solids_are_sound`; `tests/recompute.rs` with a fillet and a chamfer; the `feature` fuzz target seeded with fillets and chamfers of `b`'s edges; the app's `tests/solids.rs` and a window test. The kernel's limits show as refusals: a chamfer ending on a fillet it can't certify (`uncertain`), a fillet rolled round an arc twice its radius (`radius_too_large`), a chamfer meeting a fillet's end at a vertex (`unsupported`).

## Holes and patterns (decided in ryucad phase 4)

The design is document 59 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 4.C.

- **Mirror.** `ryukei::Body::mirrored(&Plane)` is a body's mirror image: points reflected (`geom::Mirror`); curves keep their parameters (a circle or an ellipse takes `Mirror::frame`, `x` and `y` reflected); an analytic surface takes `Mirror::frame_reversed` (`x` and `z` reflected), so a plane's `v` and the others' angle `u` run the other way (`geom::ParamFlip::V(0)`, `U(2π)`), a revolution's `u` and a pipe's `θ` too; a NURBS or an extrusion keeps its parameters and its faces flip sense. Pcurves follow (`Curve2::mirrored`), every loop runs the other way, names and tolerances stay. The one kernel change of phase 4; `tests/mirror.rs` (release only, its own CI step) and the `mirror` golden section.
- **Holes.** `hole` (`doc::hole`, format 3): `sketch`, `points` (empty: every point no curve uses, in natural order), `form` `simple`, `counterbore` (`counterbore: {diameter, depth}`) or `countersink` (`{diameter, angle}`, included), `diameter`, `depth` `{"blind": q}` (to a drill point's shoulder), `"through_all"` or `{"up_to": {target, offset}}`, `bottom` `"flat"` (unwritten) or `{"drill": angle}` (blind only), and `targets`: a hole always cuts. Its profile (`ryucad::hole`) is lines in the half-plane through its axis, keyed `ryucad.hole\0<part>\0<feature>\0<segment>` (`top`, `counterbore_wall`, `counterbore_floor`, `countersink`, `wall`, `bottom`, `tip`, `axis`), turned all round once at the sketch's origin against its normal and placed at each point under `ryucad.tool\0<part>\0<feature>\0<point>`: one kernel call per target, however many points. Its faces' origins name the point and segment, so resizing or moving renames nothing.
- **Patterns.** `pattern` (`doc::pattern`, format 3) repeats `features` (joins, cuts and holes, sharing one operation and its `targets`, which the file writes and `feature.add` fills from the seeds): `form` (a feature file's `kind` is its header) `linear` (`direction` an origin axis `x`/`y`/`z`, a straight edge or a planar face's normal; `spacing`, `count`, `reverse`; optionally `direction2`, `spacing2`, `count2`, `reverse2`), `circular` (`axis` an origin axis, a straight or circular edge, or a cylindrical or conical face; `count`, `angle`: a whole turn spaces evenly, less spans first to last) or `mirror` (`plane` an origin plane or a planar face). Counts are plain numbers (`units::Field::Number`), whole, at most 100,000 instances. `skip` lists instances (`i`, or `[i, j]` with two directions; never the seed, none for a mirror). A new pattern's form, left out, is a mirror with a plane, circular with an axis, else linear.
- **Building.** A join, cut or hole's result carries what it applied (`rebuild::Applied`: its own body and each place); a pattern applies every instance of every seed's body, moved (`Rigid`) or reflected (`Body::mirrored`), in one kernel call per target, under `ryucad.tool\0<part>\0<pattern>\0<seed>[\0<point>]\0<i>[\0<j>]` (`j` only past the first row, so a second direction renames nothing of the first). Its input hash adds the seeds' `Applied` hashes. An instance's entities' origins are `{feature: <pattern>, seed, instance, point?}` with the seed's segment or sketch entity.
- **Refusals.** A tool (a hole's point, a pattern's instance) that changes nothing is `operation_no_effect` naming it (`point`, or `seed` and `instance`); a pattern with every instance skipped too. When the kernel refuses several tools, the one that fails alone is found by halving them (`rebuild::culprit`) and named. Seeds that aren't joins, cuts or holes are `reference_wrong_kind`; mixed operations or targets `invalid_param`; parallel directions `invalid_param` at `/direction2`.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take a hole's `sketch`, `points`, `form`, `diameter`, `counterbore`, `countersink` (either in part), `depth` (an up-to target as text), `bottom`; a pattern's `form`, `features`, `direction`, `spacing`, `count`, `direction2` (`""` drops it), `spacing2`, `count2`, `reverse`, `reverse2`, `axis`, `angle`, `plane`, `skip`. CLI `--point`, `--all-points`, `--diameter`, `--counterbore-diameter`, `--counterbore-depth`, `--countersink-diameter`, `--countersink-angle`, `--depth` (a length, `through_all`, or `up_to` with `--up-to`, `--offset`), `--bottom`; `--seed`, `--direction`, `--spacing`, `--count`, `--direction2`, `--spacing2`, `--count2`, `--reverse2`, `--skip` (`i` or `i,j`), `--no-skip`. A hole's points and a pattern's seeds stay while it names them (`has_dependents`).
- **Git.** Textconv gives a two-way pattern's `skip` one line; a merge takes `points`, `features` and `skip` as one value each; renumbering rewrites a pattern's `features` and a hole's `points`; `merge_broke` blames a pattern left hanging on the side that moved what it reads.
- **App.** The property panel edits a hole's sizes, its depth (blind or through all; up to shown) and takes points out; a pattern's spacings, counts and angle, its reversals and skips (typed as JSON), and takes seeds out. Choosing a hole or a pattern paints what it made.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `doc::hole`, `doc::pattern`, `hole`, `pattern`, `server`, `validate`, `diff` and the app's `edit`; `tests/holes.rs` and `tests/patterns.rs` (every form against exact volumes, a grid with skips, a bolt circle and a mirrored pocket against the same features one by one; `check` clean); the `holes` and `patterns` sections of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt` and `drilled_solids_are_sound`; `tests/recompute.rs` with a hole and a pattern; CLI scenarios both ways; the `feature` fuzz target after a sketch with a point and a pocket (seeded with holes and patterns) and the `merge` target's pattern mode; the app's `tests/solids.rs` and a window test.

## Hole intent (decided in ryucad 28.A)

- **Standards.** `ryu-standards` (product-neutral, no dependencies, no floats) holds what may be built in by the owner's ruling (design 533, question 657): formulas (ISO 724's basic profile; ISO 965-1's T_D1 and EI, rounded to R40 then whole µm), public-domain FED-STD-H28, and plain facts. Unified limits are H28/2B's formulas, checked against every row it prints; the one value it prints otherwise wins (`unified::PRINTED`, citing its table). Metric limits come from ISO 965-1's formulas alone: H28/21B prints no limits of size, and no paid table is copied. At P = 0.5 and 2.5 mm the formula's R40 rounding falls one step below ISO's printed T_D1, so those pitches' maximum minor diameter (M3, M20, M22 coarse) is tighter here, never looser; a project needing the printed value sets the hole's `tolerance` (`iso_metric_limits.rs`). Also built in: plain facts (sizes, pitches, TPI, pipe designations and the 1:16 taper, drill series) and ISO 286 (moved from `drawing::fit`, unchanged). Values are integer nanometres with a `Source`. Internal classes only; external ones arrive with 28.B's cosmetic threads. Fasteners, pipe-thread sizes and roll-form drills are never built in.
- **Project tables.** `hole_standards.json` (`ryucad.hole_standards`, format 1, a root file) holds house thread and fastener standards and gaps in a built-in one; restating a built-in value is `invalid_document` (`reason: shadows_builtin`). A part's revision hashes the entries its holes read (tag `ryucad.hole_standards`; `missing` for an absent one), the project revision the file. Test and corpus values are made up.
- **The hole file.** `thread {standard, size, pitch?|tpi?, class?, hand?, starts?, depth?, drill?}`, `fastener {standard, size, fit?}`, `fit` or `tolerance`; `diameter` and mouth sizes optional when derived, an override when written. Any of them makes the feature format 23 (`hole::INTENT_FORMAT`); a hole with none keeps its bytes, format and hash. Refusals at read: `thread_and_fastener`, `fit_on_thread`, `unknown_thread_size`/`_class` with `known`, `pitch_not_standard` with `listed`, `class_not_taken`, `thread_depth_needed` with `suggested` (drill depth − 3P), `thread_past_drill`, `fit_undefined`.
- **Derive.** `hole::derive(hole, tables)` is pure: every size with its source (`formula`, `public`, `fact`, `rule`, `project`, `override`, `fit`). Tap drills: a project's for the size, else ryucad's rule (metric D − P at or above; unified the nearest drill to the 75% depth D − 9√3/16·p; both within the class's minor limits), `engagement` (D − 5√3/8·P·e, the next drill up) or `roll_form` (the project's). Missing values are one `needs_project_table` naming `missing` and the `entry` to add; at rebuild a vanished entry is `not_found`. A hole with intent hashes its sizes into its input.
- **Specs.** `hole::spec`: the grammar (with `THREAD std size`, `FULL`, `n STARTS`, `DRILL` taking its diameter only to override, a plain hole's point after its depth, `, CBORE`/`, CSK` mouths), ASCII canonical text with pitches to one decimal and classes always, `parse ∘ text` the identity over `Spec` (a test and the `hole_spec` fuzz target), `spec_syntax` with the byte `position`. A spec sets what it names; given beside it is `usage`, except its defaults.
- **Geometry.** A blind thread short of its drill splits the bore: `wall` (threaded, from the mouth) and `drill_wall`; making a hole tapped renames nothing that was.
- **Holes as data.** `holes.list`/`ryucad holes` (JSON, `--csv`, `--group`, `--ref`): each place of each hole feature per solid, pattern instances (`<part>/<pattern>/<hole>/<point>/<i>[,<j>]`) and mirror images by motion, faces by origin, `gone` with `removed_by`; cached with the rebuild. Cosmetic threads (`ryucad::thread`) are data on `wall` faces; mirrors keep the hand. `inspect`/`measure` add `hole` (and `thread`); `select`, `highlight` and `measure` take hole references. `hole.derive`, `standards.list`, `feature.add|edit --dry-run`. Renders draw rings (`--no-threads`).
- **The app.** Each shown part's `holes.list` is asked for with its sketches and kept per part at its revision (`core::threads::HoleTable`; a part that doesn't rebuild has none). Tapped holes draw two dashed rings at the major diameter, at the thread's mouth and end (View → Cosmetic threads, `display.threads`, on by default). Hover's last line is a hole face's spec and reference; a face's menu lists Select hole and Select siblings (every hole of its feature, patterns' and mirrors' too) only when a hole has that face (`command::on_hole`). The tree reads `h1 (M6x1.0-6H THRU, 100×)`, the count when more than one. The panel shows the spec and each derived size read-only with its source; a hole with a thread or fastener lists its diameter as an edit only when written.
- **Git.** Textconv gives `thread = M6x1.0-6H, full` and `fastener = …` one line each; a merge takes `thread`, `fastener`, `fit` and `tolerance` each as one value; a model diff reports `spec: {from, to}`.
- **Testing.** #722's fixture plate (100 × M6 on a 20 mm grid) through the CLI both ways; tapped, house-fastener and fitted holes against exact volumes; spec and `hole_standards.json` edits recomputing only what reads them, at 1 and 8 threads; the `hole_specs` golden section (bodies, every hole's JSON, every thread); the app's `threads` snapshot and a hover test.


## Hole geometry and checks (decided in ryucad 28.B)

- **The file.** `form` gains `counterdrill {diameter, depth, angle?}` (118 deg) and `spotface {diameter, depth?}` (0; its wall reaches up past the targets' box); any form but a countersink takes `near {diameter, angle?}` (90 deg; at the part's face, or a spotface's floor), and through depths take `far {countersink: {diameter, angle}} | {counterbore: {diameter, depth}}`. Depths gain `through_next` and `{tip: q}` (to a drill point's tip), bottoms `ball` (blind only), tapered pipe threads `thread.wall: "taper"` (1:16 from the project's `gauge_diameter`/`gauge_length`, a length needed). Any of them makes the feature format 25 (`hole::GEOMETRY_FORMAT`; 25.C's following instance patterns took 24 first, merging before 28.B); a hole with none keeps its bytes, format and hash. Refusals: `near_on_countersink`, `far_needs_through`, `wall_not_tapered`, `external_class`, a mouth or near no wider than what it widens.
- **Sizes.** `derive` gives each with its source; a size the hole takes when none is written is `default`. `thread_start` is below the mouth form and a near chamfer on the drill.
- **Geometry.** New segments, each its own key and none a size: `near`, `counterdrill_wall`, `counterdrill_floor`, `spotface_wall`, `spotface_floor`, `taper_end`, `far_countersink`, `far_counterbore_floor`, `far_counterbore_wall`, `ball`. `hole::exits` gives where a place's axis is inside each target (curve–surface hits, gaps classified by `point_in_body`; uncertain refuses). A far side or `through_next` needs each place's exit: places are grouped by exit (equal within the resolution) with a tool each, carried in `Applied::others` (empty, and unhashed, for every other feature), never in the tool cache; a pattern re-measures each instance's exit on its targets (mirror motions along the reflected axis). `through_next` ends a hundredth of the targets' depth past the first exit.
- **Cosmetic threads.** `cosmetic_thread` (`doc::thread`, format 25): `faces` (cylindrical, one body, coaxial, one radius; `All`), a hole's `thread` without depth or drill, `from` (an edge, `One`; needed with a length) and `depth`. The class's side must be the faces' (`thread_side`); with none written, 6g/2A on a shaft, 6H/2B in a bore. `Output::Thread`; `thread_size_mismatch` (a warning) when the face's diameter is outside the class's range widened by 0.1 mm. Listed in `holes.list`'s `threads`, `inspect`/`measure` of its face, renders and the app (rings at the minor diameter, the hole's icon). External classes built in: ISO metric g and h at grades 4, 6 and 8 (T_d by formula, R40; es(g) as EI(G), which may differ from ISO's printed table by a micrometre at some pitches). Unified 1A, 2A and 3A are **not** built in: FED-STD-H28/2B prints no external major-diameter limits for the standard series (its 5.4 defers to ASME B1.1-1989, which isn't public domain), so nothing public-domain checks the published formulas' length of engagement or rounding; a project's `hole_standards.json` gives them, as it gives ISO e and f and grades 5 and 7. A unified cosmetic thread with no project entry is `needs_project_table`, and a shaft's 2A default applies only when the project gives 2A (the coxswain's call, 2026-11-13).
- **Mirrors (#808).** Every mirror in a solid's chain, founding or joined, lists its source's holes reflected at its place in the chain, through chains of mirrors (`holes::Source`).
- **Checks, only when asked.** `holes --check` (`holes.check`) and `check --holes`: `hole_breakout` (closed-form segment distance, confirmed by a shared edge in the tip; fix: the later blind one stopped a tenth of the smaller diameter clear), `hole_breaks_through` (a blind hole's bottom gone; fixes: through, or the depth leaving 3P / D/4 of floor), `hole_thread_past_material` (material along the axis from the solid as its feature saw it, `Row::material`), `hole_edge_distance` (`measure`'s certified distance to any face not its own or beside it; no fix: distance, needed, face, `away` and a hint), `hole_thread_engagement` (`--min-engagement`), `hole_tap_drill_nonstandard` (`drill::nearest`), and `hole_cannot_check` for what can't be decided. Rows per solid in parallel, findings in the rows' then the codes' order; fixes are `feature.edit` requests, lengths floored or ceiled to 0.01 mm. Nothing else runs them (`hole::check::runs()`, counted per thread). A face the bore's wall meets along its axis (a floor) is `hole_breaks_through`'s, never `hole_edge_distance`'s. `holes.list`'s cache is keyed on the rebuild's hash and the part's feature files: an edit can leave every body as it was (a bore already through, made `through_all`) and still change the rows.

## Holes at places and fill patterns (decided in ryucad 28.C)

- **The file.** A hole takes `places` (an object by place ID) instead of `sketch`/`points`; exactly one is written. A place is `{at, face?}` (a point; on the planar face named, radial on the cylindrical or conical one named, or on the one face of the targets it lies on), `{face, from: [{edge, distance}, {edge, distance}]}` (inside a planar face, at those distances from two straight edges' lines) or `{coaxial}` (on a cylindrical or conical face's, a circular edge's or any axis), each with `direction?` (any axis; never coaxial) and `reverse?`. Every reference is a `GeomRef`, its roles `places.<key>.<field>` (`from.#` `One`, the rest `Carrier`). A hole at places, and a `fill` pattern, is feature format 26 (`hole::PLACES_FORMAT`); every other feature keeps its bytes. Refusals: `hole_place_off_face` (`distance_m`), `hole_place_ambiguous` (`faces`), `edges_parallel`, a coaxial `direction`, a sketch and places together. A place that can't resolve is refused when written (the hole kind's `check`), not left to the rebuild.
- **Mouths.** `hole::place::mouth` gives each place's mouth, drilling direction and whether it is flush (on a planar face, drilled along its inward normal); on and inside a face is `point_in_face`, certified. A coaxial place starts at the first, along its axis, of the face's circular edges' centres (a circular edge's own, else where the axis enters the targets) and is never flush. A mouth that isn't flush reaches back past the targets' box: the first vertical segment starts higher (its name kept), a cone gains a `reach` segment above it.
- **Tools.** `hole::Drill` is the frame a tool is built in: a sketch's (as before, so no sketch hole moved) or the canonical one (origin, down −z) for places, each place's `Rigid` (`Drill::place`) carrying it to its mouth. Places share a tool when depth, reach and exit agree within the resolution (a through-all depth and a reach are the greatest any place needs); the rest go in `Applied::others`. Place holes never use the tool cache. Tool keys and origins are a sketch point's with the place's key (`{"point": <place>}`); places come in `natural` key order; the CLI keys them `a`, `b`, … `z`, `a1`, …. A pattern of an exit-dependent place hole builds its instances' tools in the canonical frame (`ExitTools::at_places`), without a curved mouth's reach.
- **Fills.** `pattern` form `fill`: `face` (planar, `Carrier`), `direction?` (in its plane; the face's x when left out), `spacing`, `spacing2?`, `margin?`, `skip` (`[i, j]`, signed). Seeds are holes only, drilled into the face along its normal (`seed_off_face`). `pattern::fill::lattice` runs the lattice both ways from the seeds' places over the face's box and keeps an instance when every seed place's widest mouth circle (`hole::mouth_radius`) plus the margin lies inside the face: inside certified, the distance to a straight or circular edge exact in closed form, to any other `measure`'s certified distance from a one-vertex probe body moved to the instance (an instance whose bound straddles the threshold is `rebuild_failed`, never guessed). The face is the pattern's view of it, so the seeds' own bores are inner loops of it: an instance must keep clear of them too, and a spacing tighter than the hole clips the seed's neighbours. More than 1,000,000 lattice points, or more than 100,000 kept, is `invalid_param` at `/spacing`. `doc::pattern::Instance` is `[i32; 2]`: nothing of an existing pattern moved; a fill's instances are always `[i, j]`, keyed `…\0<i>[\0<j>]` with `j` written when not 0, listed `j` outer, `i` inner. A fill's instances aren't in its file: an instance pattern can't follow one yet (`fill_not_followed`), and ryupdm can't count them (a problem in `occurrences`). Instance patterns keep unsigned indices.
- **Git.** textconv gives each place one line (`places.a = at pt, direction z, reverse`); a merge takes each place as one value (`feature_atom`), a fill's `skip` as one value; renumbering follows a place's point (`/places/<key>/at` is the part's own, not a connector's) and needs nothing else of its own. Merge fuzz mode `0xe3` (a hole's places; it never removes place `a`, so a clean merge always keeps one).
- **Testing.** `doc::hole`/`doc::pattern` round trips and refusals; `hole::place` per kind against exact mouths; `pattern::fill` lattices, margins, negative indices, an elliptical edge on the margin, and its limits; `tests/holes.rs` (every placement against exact volumes, a radial hole through a tube's near wall, a dowel skipped from a fill and drilled alone); `tests/patterns.rs` (a fill against its holes one by one, a 1,000-place guard in release); `tests/cli.rs` (places from the CLI, #722's plate by one hole and a fill) both ways; `tests/names.rs`; `tests/recompute.rs` (places and fills at 1 and 8 threads); the `hole_places` golden section.

## Hardening (decided in ryucad phase 4)

The design is document 59 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 4.D.

- **Names.** `tests/names.rs` pins what edits rename: moving and resizing a cut, a fillet, a chamfer or a hole, and a pattern's spacing, rename nothing; a hole's new form renames only what its new segments touch (the bore's wall keeps its name), a pattern's extra instance or skip only that instance's entities, and a second direction nothing of the first row.
- **The spike's cases.** A notch in the sketch under a fillet keeps the fillet on its corner (the corner edge is named from the segments either side of its vertex, which the notch doesn't touch); the sketch drawn again with new IDs fails the fillet as `reference_unresolved` with `was` (`{kind, from}`) and `removed_by` naming the extrude, and nothing lands on another edge. Both run in one session (`tests/names.rs`) and through `git merge` (`tests/merge.rs`), where the redraw is `merge_broke`, blamed on its side. `merge.status` learns the names its sides built (`Session::learn_from_revisions`), so its explanations say what a name was.
- **Explanations.** A rebuild explains its failures against its own results, not the rebuild before, so `removed_by` is never stale.
- **The 1,000-hole bar.** `tests/bar.rs` (release only, its own CI step): a plate with a 40 × 25 hole pattern rebuilds on one thread within twice ryukei's own boolean on the same geometry (about 1.0×). A hole's tool starts on its sketch's plane, so its top is flush with the face it drills; flush tools cost the kernel about nine times as much as tools out of both faces until classification skipped coincident partners whose boxes don't hold a piece's point (issue #77: every piece of the drilled face was tested against every tool's cap), and now about the same (0.53 s against 0.50 s on davids-m4's Apple M4); the test prints both and fails if the gap grows past twice.
- **Recompute.** Editing the last of a solid's 50 features builds that feature alone; editing its founder builds the chain once, reusing every cut's own body (`tests/recompute.rs`).
- **Fuzzing and benchmarks.** The `feature` target requires every solid it builds to pass `check_intersections` (`CannotCheck` allowed) and mesh without faults, as ryukei's `boolean` and `blend` targets do. It still finds one kernel limit: a sphere intersected with a box whose face its pole lies on meshes that sphere face as `BadDomain` (issue #78). `cargo bench -p ryucad` adds a plate with rounded corners, 50 patterned counterbores and a chamfered rim: a cold rebuild, and one after the pattern's spacing changes.
- **App.** The viewport snapshot gains a drilled part (`solids-lavapipe.png`), and a window test picks a pocket's floor, a face of the target solid whose origin is the cut.

## Shells and offsets (decided in ryucad phase 15)

The design is document 327 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 15.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **Files.** `shell` (`target`, `thickness` a length over zero, `direction` `outward` or nothing for inward, `open` faces, `faces` groups `{thickness, faces}`) and `offset` (`target`, `distance` of either sign, zero allowed, `faces` groups `{distance, faces}`), feature format 9, `doc::body`. Faces are `{feature, name}` references into the target; empty `open` and `faces` are written as nothing. A face open and grouped, or in two groups, is `invalid_param` at its second pointer; an offset whose every distance is zero `invalid_param` at `/distance`.
- **Building.** Both act on their target (its chain, `feature_acts` for files that don't load), resolve their faces in the target's view (`targets_mixed`, `reference_wrong_kind`), and make one kernel call under the feature's `OpKey` and the request's cancel (`ryucad::body`): `ryukei::offset::shell_with` or `offset_body_with`. Results are cached like any solid's.
- **Origins.** A shell's face its boolean `Placed` is `{feature, wall_of}`, the target face it is the wall of (inwards the inner faces; outwards the solid's old surfaces, while the outer faces keep the body's names); an offset's fill `{feature, replaces}` (`rebuild::replaced_origins`, which blends share); kept faces keep theirs. A new thickness or distance renames nothing.
- **Kept edges carried.** A face pushed outwards across convex edges whose other faces stay at zero (a box's top raised with its sides kept) builds: a kept straight edge whose vertex moves past its interval is carried along its line to the moved face's offset (#396, fixed in 0.8.2). A kept curved edge there is still `offset_failed` `edge_vanishes`.
- **Refusals.** `shell_failed` and `offset_failed`: `reason` (the `OffsetError` or `BooleanError` variant in snake case, an unsupported `case`), `kernel` (`offset` or `boolean`), `refusal` (the kernel's text), `entities` as `blend_failed`'s; a shell's `edge_vanishes` or `mixed_vertex` adds a `hint`.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `target` (else the part's one solid), `thickness`, `direction`, `open` and `faces` (whole, `[]` for none), `distance`; CLI `--target`, `--thickness`, `--outward`, `--open` (repeated), `--no-open`, `--face-thickness REF=Q` and `--face-distance REF=Q` (repeated, equal quantities one group), `--no-faces`, `--distance`. `feature.show`, rebuild reports (`shell` or `offset`) and `inspect --feature` give the fields with quantities in meters and faces as text.
- **Git.** Textconv gives each group a line (`faces[0] = 4 mm: base/<hex>, …`); merges take `open` and `faces` whole; renumbering rewrites `target` and the faces.
- **Testing.** `doc::body` and `body` unit tests (a cancelled job reaching the kernel); `tests/shells.rs` and `tests/offsets.rs` against exact volumes; `shells_and_offsets_from_the_cli` both ways; `shells_and_offsets_recompute_as_from_nothing`; the `shells` and `offsets` golden sections; the `feature` fuzz target's seeds and the `merge` target's `0xf4` mode.

## Mirrors and splits (decided in ryucad phase 15)

The design is document 327 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 15.B (phases 4 to 6).

- **Files.** `mirror` (`solid`, `plane` any plane reference, `operation` and `targets` a `Combine`, `operation` always written, `new` too) and `split` (`target`, `plane`, `flip` written only when true, `keep` `both` written as nothing or `behind`), feature format 10, `doc::body`.
- **Mirrors.** A mirror's own body is its `solid` as it sees it reflected in its plane (`Body::mirrored`, the plane found as a pattern's mirror finds it), every entity keeping its source's name, its origin `{feature, mirror_of}`, its journal empty. Through `build_body` as an extrude's body: `new` founds a solid, `join`, `cut` and `intersect` apply it under `tool_key` (kept by `tool_hash`, which reads its source). Left out, the operation is `join` with the source as target (question 20 of design 327). A reflection leaving the size box is `mirror_failed` (`reason: outside_size_box`); a combined copy refuses as `boolean_failed`, and one changing nothing (mirrored in a plane of its own symmetry) `operation_no_effect`. A mirror is never a pattern's seed.
- **Splits.** The first feature that acts on a solid and founds one: `Feature::founds()` (a body-making feature's `new`, or a split keeping both sides), and `Solids::of_order` joins its target's chain and then founds its own, whose chain starts at the split. Two booleans with a half-space box (one face on the plane, a hundredth of the target's diagonal past its box every other way) under `ryucad.split\0<part>\0<split>`: the target less it for the side behind (the target's), the target and it for the side in front (the split's, with `keep: both`), under tool keys `…\0behind` and `…\0front`, so each cut face keeps its name across a move of the plane. A cut face's origin is `{feature, side}`. Both sides are built side by side (`rayon::join`); the first failure in order is reported. Each side is one solid however many lumps; the report gives each side's `lumps`. A plane the target's box lies wholly on one side of, or a side a boolean leaves empty, is `operation_no_effect` with `side`.
- **Reports.** Rebuild reports and `inspect --feature` give a mirror's and split's fields with the plane as text, and, once built, the plane resolved (`{origin_m, normal}`, a split's normal as flipped) and a split's `sides` (`{side, solid, lumps}`).
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `solid`, `plane`, `operation`, `targets`, `target`, `flip`, `keep`; CLI `--solid`, `--plane`, `--operation` (`new` for a copy of its own), `--target`, `--flip`, `--keep`. A split or mirror that stops founding while something reads its solid is `has_dependents`.
- **Git.** Textconv one line per field. A split's `plane` and `flip` merge as one value (a conflict at `/plane` and `/flip` together, as a sweep's law and direction); a mirror's fields per key, its `targets` one value, as every `Combine` merges (a departure from design 327, which said `operation` and `targets` together). Renumbering rewrites `solid` as `target`, planes as references.
- **Testing.** `doc::body`, `solid`, `body`, `merge`, `renumber` and `diff` unit tests (a split whose plane moved changes both sides' solids); `tests/mirrors.rs` and `tests/splits.rs` against exact volumes; `mirrors_and_splits_from_the_cli` both ways; `mirrors_and_splits_recompute_as_from_nothing`; the `mirrors` and `splits` golden sections; the `feature` fuzz target's seeds and the `merge` target's `0xf2` mode (its base a split or a mirror by the parity of the edit bytes).

## Face tweaks: move and replace (decided in ryucad phase 17)

The design is document 400 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 17.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **Files.** `move_face` (`target`, `by` always written: `translate` with `direction` (any axis, or a plane's normal) and `distance`, `rotate` with `axis` and `angle`, or `offset` with `distance` for the listed faces and `groups` `{distance, faces}` of their own; `faces`; `tangent`, written only when false) and `replace_face` (`target`, `groups` `{with, faces}`, `with` a face, whose surface is taken whole, or a plane), feature format 16 through `doc::tweak::FORMAT` (`FEATURE_FORMAT` is the max of every stage's constants). A zero motion, a face named twice, or a face given its own surface is `invalid_param` at its pointer.
- **Building** (`ryucad::tweak`). Each acts on its target (its chain), resolves its faces by stable name in its view (`targets_mixed`, `reference_wrong_kind`), its direction, axis and surfaces through `geometry::resolve` and `reference::resolve`, and makes one kernel call under its `OpKey` and the request's cancel: `move_faces_with` (a rotation about an axis where it lies, a translation along a unit direction), `offset_faces_with`, `replace_faces_with`. A tweak makes nothing: every entity keeps its name and origin. `tangent` is true by default (ryukei's spec defaults to false), so a fillet beside a moved face moves with it.
- **Sheets.** `move_face` and `replace_face` take a sheet (since ryucad 18.C through `kind::bodies_read`, as every feature's bodies are; `tweak::takes_sheets` now only chooses the tweak's report); replacing a face with a laminar edge is the kernel's `laminar_face`. What else takes a sheet is `ryucad-surfaces.md`'s Trims, splits and exchange.
- **Refusals.** `tweak_failed` (new code): `reason` (`TweakError`'s variant in snake case, `unsupported` with `case`), `kernel: tweak`, `refusal`, `entities` as `blend_failed`'s, and a `hint` for `tangency_broken` (tangent true, or the fillet after the tweak), the topology changes `edge_vanishes`, `vertex_splits` and `face_collapses` (ryukei 13's), `hinge_not_on_face` and `hinge_chain` (split the face first) and `hinge_along_pull`. Kept in the disk cache as other kernels' refusals are.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `target`, `by`, `direction`, `distance`, `axis`, `angle`, `faces`, `groups` and `tangent`; a new move with no `by` is a rotation with an axis, a translation with a direction, else an offset; a new `by` keeps only the fields it takes; with no target, the part's one solid, else the one its faces name. CLI `--face` (repeatable; one is still a chamfer's or a sketch's `face`), `--face-distance`, `--no-tangent`/`--tangent`, `--with` (the `--face` values after it, until the next), `--angle` taking a minus sign. `feature.show`, rebuild reports and `inspect --feature` give the fields (quantities in meters and radians, references as text) and once built `motion` (`{rotation, translation_m}`) and `modified` (counts; references with `--feature`).
- **Offset and move_face.** The 15.A `offset` feature with face groups keeps building as it did; `move_face --by offset` is the tweak (fixed topology, tangent chains, only the named faces move). Each's help names the other.
- **Git.** Textconv a line a field, each group `groups[0] = 1 mm: base/<hex>, …` (a replacement's `groups[0] = lid/<hex>: …`). Merges per key, a datum's rule making the file one value when `by` changes on either side, `faces` and `groups` one value each. Renumbering rewrites `target`, faces, `with`, `direction` and `axis` (no new code).
- **Testing.** `doc::tweak` round trips and refusals; `tweak` unit tests (motions, refusals and hints, the protocol, reports, replacements); `tests/tweaks.rs` (a boss slid, a top raised and tilted about a datum axis, a pocket's faces offset with their fillets and `tangency_broken` without them, a fillet after a move, names, a hole and a mate across edits, a top flush with a face and with a datum plane, a sheet moved and refused a replacement, refusals); `face_tweaks_from_the_cli` both ways; `tweaks_recompute_as_from_nothing`; the `tweaks` golden section; the `feature` fuzz target's tweak seeds and the `merge` target's `0xed` mode.
- **Left for 17.B and 17.C.** Drafts (the `draft` type, its report and `draft_angle_varies`) and the app's rows, painting and forms.

## Drafts (decided in ryucad phase 17)

The design is document 400 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 17.B (phases 4 and 5).

- **Files.** `draft` (`doc::tweak::Draft`, feature format 18 through `doc::tweak::DRAFT_FORMAT`; 17 is ryucad 21.D's): `target`; `angle`, its size in `[1e-11, π/2 − 0.01]` (`MIN_DRAFT_ANGLE`, `MAX_DRAFT_ANGLE`, held to ryukei's), positive tapering a boss inward along the pull and opening a hole; a hinge, `neutral` (any plane: its normal is the pull) or `parting` (edges of the target) with `pull` (an axis or a plane's normal); `reverse` (written only when true) turning the pull round; `faces`; `groups` `{angle, faces}`; `flip`, drafted faces drafted against the pull (written only when not empty); `tangent` (written only when false: true by default, as move_face's). Neither or both hinges, a pull with a neutral plane, a face twice, a flip not drafted and an angle out of range are refused at their pointers when read.
- **Building** (`ryucad::tweak`, 17.A's module). The hinge resolves in the feature's view (`geometry::one` for a plane, `geometry::resolve` `[Axis, Plane]` for a pull, `reference::resolve` for edges, `targets_mixed` and `reference_wrong_kind` as a tweak's faces), each face its group's angle and its flip, and one `ryukei::tweak::draft_with` under the feature's `OpKey` and the request's cancel, `Topology::Keep`. A draft takes a sheet where the kernel does (faces with no laminar edge; `laminar_face` otherwise). Refusals are 17.A's `tweak_failed`, whose draft reasons and hints 17.A mapped (`no_hinge`, `hinge_ambiguous`, `hinge_not_on_face` and `hinge_chain` (split the face), `hinge_along_pull`).
- **The report.** The kernel's `DraftReport` is the feature's `Made::report` (`{target, hinge, angles: [{face, least, greatest, asked?}]}`, radians), which the disk cache keeps as it keeps every feature's report, so a warm rebuild gives it with no kernel call. Rebuild reports and `inspect --feature` give `draft`: the fields as written (quantities with `rad`), `pull`, `neutral` (`{origin_m, normal}`), `angles` (`{face, least, greatest, exact}` in degrees and radians, `exact` where the two are the same bits) and `modified`.
- **`draft_angle_varies`** (a warning): a parting-line draft's face whose angle lies more than a millidegree from the angle asked, or spreads wider (a parting line climbing along the pull holds the angle square to the hinge). Neutral-plane drafts hold their angle by construction and never warn.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `angle`, `neutral` (taking a parting line and pull away), `parting` (taking a neutral plane away), `pull`, `reverse`, `faces`, `groups`, `flip` and `tangent`; a request's `flip` is a bool (a datum's, a split's) or a list of faces (a draft's: `proto::Flip`). CLI `--neutral`, `--parting` (repeated), `--pull`, `--angle`, `--face` (repeated), `--face-angle REF=ANGLE`, `--flip` (alone or `true`/`false` a bool, a face reference a draft's, repeated), `--no-flip`, `--reverse`, `--no-tangent`.
- **Git.** Textconv a line a field, each group `groups[0] = 5 deg: base/<hex>`. A draft merges per key but for its hinge, `neutral`, `parting`, `pull` and `reverse` one value together (`merge::paired`, which a split's plane and flip now share); `faces`, `groups` and `flip` arrays. Renumbering and diff need nothing of their own.
- **Testing.** `doc::tweak`'s round trips and refusals; `tweak`'s resolution, build, protocol, reports and warning unit tests; `cache::results`' entry; `tests/drafts.rs` (walls about the bottom and a datum plane through the middle against prismatoids, a boss and its bore about the plate's top against frusta, a rounded rectangle by tangency, a ledged box from its parting line, groups and a flip, fillets after and before, names across angles, refusals, sheets, the warning); `tests/cache.rs`' warm angles; `drafts_recompute_as_from_nothing`; `drafts_from_the_cli` both ways; the `drafts` and `draft_reports` golden sections; the `feature` fuzz target's draft seeds and the `merge` target's `0xeb` mode.
