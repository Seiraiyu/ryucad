## Recognition and unfold (decided in phase 11)

The design is document 298 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 11.A.

- **Names.** Sheet metal is `ryukei::sheet_metal`, its refusals `SheetMetalError` in `Error::InvalidSheetMetal`, its fixtures `ryukei_testkit::sheet_metal` and its tests `tests/sheet_metal.rs`: `ryukei::sheet`, `SheetError` and `Error::InvalidSheet` are sheet bodies' (plan 12.A; see `kernel-sheets.md`). Design 298 wrote `ryukei::sheet`; plans 11.B to 11.D use these names.
- **API.** `ryukei::sheet_metal`: `recognize(&Body, &SheetOptions { tolerance })` gives a `SheetModel` (thickness, the tolerance used, flanges by name with their two `Side`s (a plane, its `z` the outward normal, and its faces), bends by name with their inner, outer and wall faces, axis frame, radius, angle, flanges (the one nearer the fixed flange first), bend-line edges per end and `BendDirection`, the default fixed flange, and the first `Cycle` if the bends make one); `unfold(key, &Body, &SheetModel, &UnfoldSpec { fixed, bends, allowance, overrides })` gives `Unfolded { body, journal, pattern: FlatPattern, map: FlatMap }`. Refusals are `Error::InvalidSheetMetal(SheetMetalError)`, naming faces, edges and bends. `Allowance::KFactor(k)` (`k` in `[0, 1]`, `ba = θ (r + k t)`) or `Length(ba)`.
- **Bends.** A concave cylinder face and the convex faces coaxial with it, larger, from a box tree over the cylinder faces (boxes grown by their radii), each joined at both ends of its angle tangentially to planes along lines parallel to the axis (the bend lines). The angle is read from those lines, not from pcurve bounds (a spline pcurve's hull overshoots). Pieces on one cylinder joining the same flanges are one bend, named by the least name among its faces. A coaxial pair a whole turn round is a tube (`BendLoop`); a bend past half a turn `NotSheet` (a pair whose lines don't reach both ends is no bend, its faces judged as a flange's); a cone met by a plane along a ruling `ConicalBend`.
- **Thickness.** The bends' radius difference, all agreeing (`MixedThickness`); with no bends, the pair of opposite planes facing apart with the greatest extent (the boxes of their vertices in their planes), ties to the least name, so an extrusion wider than it is long reads as a plate of its profile's thickness. Then planes `t` from a partner meeting another such plane at a sharp edge, or a lone cylinder rounding such a corner on one side, are `SharpBend`.
- **Flanges.** Faces that are neither a bend's cylinders nor its walls (the other faces meeting them) join across shared edges, and the planes across one end's bend lines join each other, so a channel's web is one piece. Its sides are the two planes its bends' lines give, `t` apart; every other face of it must lie between them: planes by their edges, spline edges by their control hulls, halved up to six times where a hull straddles a side (an end outside certifies an edge out, a hull inside in, anything left `Uncertain`), cylinders and cones along the normal by their height range, cylinders across it by their angles, tori about it by their tube, anything else by the height's mean value form over its parameter box cell by cell (4,096 cells at most, else `Uncertain`). A face whose box reaches past the face itself can be refused `OutOfSlab` wrongly there (a NURBS wall cut from a larger surface). A flange's side meeting a bend's cylinder away from its bend lines is `BendBlocked`, naming the bend (its least face over every piece) and that side.
- **Walls.** Planes square to the axis may run on across flanges (their edges there must lie in each flange's slab); planes through the axis, and any other surface certified radial (`|N · r| <= s |N| |r|`, `N = S_u × S_v`, `r` the offset from the axis, `s = tol / √(t² + tol²)`, by the mean value form cell by cell: across the thickness the face strays at most `tol` from radial), must meet no flange face; planes along the axis but off it, tilted planes, and surfaces certified not radial are `NotRadial`.
- **The graph.** A breadth-first walk from the fixed flange, bends in name order, gives each bend's near flange, the tree and the first closing bend (`Cycle`: the loop's bends and the closing bend's faces); flanges it doesn't reach are `Disconnected`. A bend's direction is `Up` when its near flange's inner side, turned flat, faces the viewer: the fixed flange's side away from its first bend's centre.
- **Unfold.** Each flange moves by the product of the unfolded bends between it and the fixed one, each a turn by `-θ` about its axis then a move of `ba` across the strip. Faces, edges and vertices take their region's map: a bend's cylinders become the strip's planes, walls square to the axis move with the near flange, walls through it become planes square to the flat, curved radial walls the extrusion (a plane, for a line) of their inner edge's image along the flat's normal, their other edges' tolerances growing by `tol · max(1, ρn / r)`; lines along the axis and radial lines keep their parameters, arcs about it become lines with parameters scaled by `ρn = ba / θ`, any other edge on a bend face its pcurve's affine image lifted into the strip. Pcurves of a face and an edge that move by one motion are kept, the rest found again by `pcurve::fill` (a plane takes every curve kind exactly). The flat must pass `check` and `check_intersections` (`FlatOverlaps`). `key` names nothing: every name is kept and journalled `Modified`.
- **Pattern and map.** `FlatPattern`: the fixed flange's viewer side and everything laid flat with it, its boundary as loops of `PatternSegment`s (each its fin's pcurve carried rigidly into the pattern's frame, naming its edge), outlines (positive area) first, then holes, each group by least edge name; each unfolded bend's centre line across the strip with its edges, direction, angle, radius, length and allowance. `FlatMap`: each unfolded strip (bend, near and far flanges by name, folded axis frame, flat strip frame, radius, angle, length, allowance) and each flange's motion; `codec::encode_flat_map`/`decode_flat_map` (`ryukei.flatmap.1`).
- **Testing and limits.** Unit tests per module (`sheet_metal/tests.rs` over extruded bent strips, a notched bend and its spline wall, a tube, refusals), `codec`'s flat-map round trip, and `tests/sheet_metal.rs` (brackets at any angle against `L₁ + L₂ - 2 (r + t) tan(θ / 2) + ba`, channels and jogs with their directions and outline areas, a hem, the codec), all in CI's debug builds. No body 11.A can build overlaps in the flat (strips unfold to strips), so `FlatOverlaps` is first reached by 11.C's trays. Golden sections, the `sheet_metal` fuzz target, benches and the bar are plan 11.D's.

## The bent surface and refold (decided in phase 11)

The design is document 298 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 11.B (phases 3 and 4).

- **The surface.** `Surface::Bent(Arc<Bent>)` (`geom::bent`) is a base surface in a strip's coordinates (`x` across, `y` along the axis, `z` through the sheet) carried by the fold map `F(x, y, z) = O + y a + (r + z)(cos(κ x) e₁ + sin(κ x) e₂)` over a box of the base's parameters. It keeps its base's parameters, so pcurves carry over exactly. It stores the neutral curvature `κ = θ / ba` (rounded once) rather than the neutral radius.
  - Bases are planes, cylinders, extrusions and NURBS. Cylinders are added to the design's list: a round hole's wall in the flat is a cylinder.
  - `Bent::new` certifies, over the base's own cells cut to the box (halved up to 16 times, 16,384 cells at most), that `B_u × B_v` doesn't vanish and `r + z > 0`, and that `κ x` spans less than a turn. Otherwise it is `Degenerate`.
  - Cell sides closer than a millionth of the box are merged. A box ending a few ulps from a side of the base's cells left a sliver cell, over which the NURBS form's Hermite spans magnified rounding: its second derivatives came out 10⁴ times the surface's, and healing's shell mesh was refused as 12M triangles.
  - `F`'s Jacobian is orthogonal with determinant `κ (r + z)`, so the natural normal is the base's carried by `F`'s cofactor: `n_x t + κ (r + z)(n_y a + n_z ρ̂)`.
  - Jets to third order are the base's (closed forms for planes and cylinders, `Sheet::jets3_at` otherwise) composed with `F` by Leibniz's rule and the chain rule, in `f64` and over intervals. So it is a `Sheet`: every intersector, the mesher's bounds, `beside`, projection (`sheet::nearest`) and offsets (`Offset::new` takes a bent base) take it.
  - `transformed` moves the axis frame. `mirrored` reflects the frame (its axis negated, so it stays right-handed) and the base across the strip's `y = 0`, and its parameters map as the base's do: the design said `ParamFlip::Kept`, which holds only for NURBS and extrusion bases.
  - `to_nurbs` is `Degenerate`. `Bent::to_nurbs_within` is the offset's certified bicubic Hermite fit, which now serves both through `offset::fit::Fitted`; the offsets' arithmetic is unchanged, so no golden hash moved.
  - Codec tag 12; the dump writes `bent axis=… r=… k=… over=… base=(…)`. Blends refuse bent faces (`blend::spec::supported`), as they do sweeps.
- **The flat map.** `Strip` gains `span`, its extent along the axis in its frame (from the bend's faces' vertices). Refolding cuts the flat at bend lines only over it. The flat map's bytes changed in place: no release ever wrote `ryukei.flatmap.1`, so the tag stays.
- **API.** `sheet_metal::refold(key, &flat, &FlatMap, &RefoldSpec { bends })` gives `(Body, Journal)`. `bends: None` folds every strip; named ones fold only those (`NoSuchBend` otherwise). A strip left flat is carried with its near flange.
- **Imprint** (`sheet_metal::imprint`). Each folded strip's bend lines (`x = 0`, `x = ba`, over its span) cut the flat. Edges crossing a line are split there (SEMV, crossings found by `intersect::curve_surface`). Then faces crossing it are cut:
  - a side (a plane square to `z`) along the line between consecutive vertices on it;
  - a wall square to the sides (a plane, or a cylinder or extrusion along `z`) by a line through the sheet;
  - each only where the segment's middle is inside the face and no edge already joins the two vertices.

  A segment joining two loops is MEKR, one splitting a loop MEF. MEF is refused (`Uncertain`) on a face with other loops, since which side they belong on isn't worked out; no case needed it, because booleans in the flat keep the bend lines as edges and leave the sides split there. Planes square to the axis are left whole. Every cut is named by the Euler operators under a key from the refold's key, the bend and the line (`name::SheetMetalRole::Imprint`, role code 15).
- **Regions** (`sheet_metal::regions`). A face is in a strip when every point sampled along its edges is in the strip's box (grown by `DEFAULT_TOLERANCE`) and one lies strictly between its bend lines. A plane square to every strip it crosses runs across (a bracket's end face: refolding takes it to one plane). Any other face crossing a bend line is `NotRadial` (a countersink cut across it).
  - The other faces join into groups across shared edges. Faces beside one strip's near line, or beside its far line, are one flange's and join, though only the end faces running across connect them (a flange between two bends has just its two sides).
  - A group beside both lines of one strip, or beside a line along the strip's middle, is `BendBlocked`.
  - Motions: folding every strip, a group goes back by the inverse of its flange's motion in the flat map, exactly. Folding some, a group goes back by the product of the folds between it and the fixed flange, each `M_near M_far⁻¹` in the flat.
- **The fold map** (`sheet_metal::refold`).
  - Faces:
    - Sides become the bend's cylinders, their seam opposite the bend's middle (`u = κ x + π - θ / 2`), their pcurves mapped by that affine map (`curve2::mapped`).
    - Planes square to the axis stay planes. Planes through it become planes through the axis.
    - Any other wall square to the sides becomes a bent surface of its own surface. Its box comes from points along its edges, unwrapping a periodic direction at its widest gap (a whole turn only where no gap passes an eighth of it). Each pcurve is moved by whole periods into that box. A box of the whole turn put the bent surface through the hole's flat half at mid-thickness, where `F` keeps lengths, and the self-intersection check couldn't part them.
    - NURBS walls in a strip are refused `NotRadial`; the design's "certified to be one within the tolerance" is left to 11.D.
  - Edges:
    - lines along the axis stay lines, lines through the sheet become radial lines, both on the same parameter;
    - lines across the axis become circles about it, on the parameter `σ κ x₀ + κ t` (pcurves follow);
    - any other edge is on a side and is fitted along its pcurve there (`intersect::fit_along`), its certified bound joining its tolerance.
  - Faces and edges in groups move rigidly with their pcurves. The imprint's new fins get pcurves (`pcurve::fill`) before regions are read, and the dropped ones after.
  - The result must pass `check::check` and `check_intersections`: crossing or overlapping faces are `SheetMetalError::FoldCollides { faces }` (new), anything else `Uncertain`.
- **Recognizing refolded walls.** A bent surface about the bend's own axis whose base is ruled along the strip's `z` (a plane square to the sides, or a cylinder or extrusion along their normal) is radial in closed form (`radial::ruled_about`): the fold map takes its rulings to radii. The mean value form couldn't certify it within `1e-7`.
  - A radial wall may now meet a flange face across an edge in the plane through the axis and one of the bend's lines (`recognize::at_line`), where a wall cut across a bend line in the flat runs on into its flange as another face.
  - So a refolded flat with a hole and a slot across its bend line is recognized, and unfolds again to the flat's volume.
- **Round trips.** `refold(unfold(b))` has `b`'s topology and names and its points within ulps (`1e-12` m in the tests), and unfolding it again gives the flat as closely. Bit for bit, as the design asked, doesn't hold: the fold map and its inverse round.
- **Exchange.** `heal::seamed` writes a bent face as its NURBS form within `pipe_tolerance` on the same parameters, keeping its pcurves (`bent_to_nurbs`); seams on a bent face that wraps are fitted as an offset's are. USD gets the same through `seamed_with_origins`. A refolded bracket drilled across its bend line goes through STEP and back to the same volume and is recognized again. Its NURBS wall is accepted as radial at a tolerance of `1e-5` (a hundredth of the thickness), not at the default `1e-7`: the NURBS form is itself `1e-7` off, and the mean value form's cells can't certify within `1e-6`.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `geom::bent` (sides onto cylinders, jets against differences, enclosures, normals, refusals, moves and mirrors, projection, offsets, the NURBS form), the codec's geometry round trip, and `sheet_metal::tests` (strips, groups and their motions, a partial fold, imprinting a hole and a slot). `tests/sheet_metal.rs`:
  - round trips of brackets, channels, jogs and hems;
  - a hole and a slot at 30° across a bend line refolded: they check, mesh, keep the volume at a K-factor of a half, and each side's area scales by `(r + z) κ`;
  - a partial refold;
  - refusals: a countersink, a flange that folds through another, an unknown bend.

  ryustep's `tests/write.rs` holds the STEP round trip. Golden sections, the `sheet` fuzz target, seeded round-trip properties, benches and the bar are 11.D's.
- **Limits.**
  - The self-intersection check settles a bent wall against the flat half of its own hole, tangent along the cut, only from the flat half's side, through `beside`. The bent wall's own surface crosses the flat half's cylinder (inside it below mid-thickness, outside above).
  - A face to be split while it has other loops is refused.
  - Refolding re-runs the whole self-intersection check, which dominates its time.

## Edge flanges (decided in phase 11)

The design is document 298 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 11.C (phase 5).

- **API.** `sheet_metal::flange(key, &Body, &[EdgeFlange], &SheetOptions) -> (Body, Journal)`. Each `EdgeFlange` names its `edge`: a straight edge between a flange's side and a planar wall square to it. It may also name the `side`, which must be one of the edge's faces. It gives the `angle` in `(0, π]` and the `length`, `measured` from the `OuterMould`, the `InnerMould` or the bend's end (`Tangent`). It gives the inner `radius`, the `position`, `setbacks` from the edge's start and end vertices, and a `Relief` at each end: `None`, `Rectangular { width, depth }`, `Obround { width, depth }` or `Tear { width }`. The flange folds towards the side's outward normal, the inner radius on that side. The body is recognized first, and its thickness taken.
- **Positions.** These follow SolidWorks and Onshape, a departure from design 298's wording, which the coxswain chose. `MaterialInside` cuts the base back by `r + t` (a square flange's outer side is flush with the old wall), `MaterialOutside` by `r` (its inner side flush), `BendOutside` not at all. The design gave `r` to `BendOutside` and nothing to `MaterialOutside`. The trims are those at every angle.
- **Measures.** The straight length past the bend is the length (`Tangent`), or the length less `(r + t) tan(θ / 2)` (`OuterMould`) or `r tan(θ / 2)` (`InnerMould`). At a half turn the mould lines are at infinity, so a length measured to one is `BadSize { what: Length }`.
- **Reliefs.** A relief is cut only where the flange's trim cuts into the base beside material a setback leaves: a trim and a setback at that end. There, `None` is `ReliefNeeded { edge, vertex }`; elsewhere a relief given is ignored. A relief runs from past the wall to `depth` past the bend line, `width` wide beside the bend's end. An obround's `depth` is its farthest point, at least half its width; a tear reaches the bend line. Recognition doesn't see material against a bend's end face (it allows walls across a bend's ends), so this check is the plan's, not recognition's.
- **Tools.** Each flange is three exact sweeps in its edge's frame (`s` along the edge from its start vertex, `x` into the base from the wall, `h` along the side's normal):
  - the bend, the section `h ∈ [-(r + t), -r]` about the axis through the bend line `r` along the normal, revolved through `θ`;
  - the flange, the bend's end section extruded `straight` along the way the bend leaves it;
  - the cut, one profile holding the trim and both reliefs, from `t` past the wall to the bend line, extruded through the sheet and `t` past both sides.

  Segment keys name what a segment is (wall, ends, relief floors and sides, bend line), so sizes, setbacks and reliefs rename nothing they don't make or take away.
- **Two booleans.** A boolean takes one operation, so the design's "one boolean pass" is two. Every cut is subtracted at once, under the key `SheetMetalRole::Cut` derives from the call's key with no parent. Then every bend and flange is united, under the call's key. Flanges are planned and their tools listed in edge-name order, so the order given changes no bit. A boolean's refusal comes back as `Error::InvalidBoolean`, as `offset::shell`'s does, not wrapped in a `SheetMetalError::Boolean` as the design said.
- **Names.** A tool's sweep is keyed by `Name::derive(key, Role::SheetMetal(r), &[edge])` for `r` in `Bend`, `Flange` and `Cut` (role codes 15/1 to 15/3), and its `ToolKey` takes the same value. Its entities are `Placed` under that key; the base's keep their names. The design's `Trim` and `Relief` roles are one, `Cut`: a flange's trim and reliefs are one tool. The journal is the two booleans' in order, then `Created` for each new bend's inner and outer face. A flange's radius, angle, length and setbacks rename nothing (`tests/sheet_metal.rs`).
- **Refusals,** all before anything is built where planning can tell: `NoSuchEdge`, `DuplicateFlange`, `NotAFlangeEdge`, `BadSize { what: FlangeSize, edge }` (`Angle`, `Length`, `Radius`, `Setback`, `Relief`; a bend's inner arc, a straight flange, the edge left between setbacks and a relief's width at least `10 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION`), `ReliefNeeded`, and `FlangesMeet { vertex }`. Flanges meet when, on one side's plane, their footprints overlap by more than `10 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION`; the vertex is the one their edges share, else the least of their four. A footprint is the span from as far out as the bend and flange reach (`r + t + straight` past the bend line) to as far back over the base (`-cos θ × straight` past it), and each relief. After the booleans, a body that doesn't recognize, or where a planned bend's inner face is on no bend, is `NotSheetAfter { fault }`: recognition's refusal, or `NoSuchBend` naming that face. That is what a flange colliding with the rest of the body gives.
- **Recognition.** Planes `t` apart that face apart count as a pair for the sharp-corner check only where their faces overlap seen along the normal (`shape::overlap_along`, by face boxes). A wall the material-inside trim leaves beside a set-back flange is flush with the flange's outer side, so it lies `t` from its inner side, facing away, and was read as a sharp bend. No golden hash moved.
- **Testing.** Unit tests (`sheet_metal/flange/tests.rs`: planning in the edge's frame, trims and measures, every refusal, the tools' vertices, the three reliefs, the angle the flange leaves at, a flange in every position recognized and journalled; `sheet_metal/tests.rs`'s flush wall). `tests/sheet_metal.rs` covers:
  - flats against `L₁ + L₂ - 2 (r + t) tan(θ / 2) + ba` in every position at four angles, and a hem;
  - a tray of four set-back flanges with rectangular reliefs, unfolding to the plate less its cuts plus each strip and keeping its volume at a K-factor of a half;
  - trays with obround and tear reliefs;
  - names across edits, and the order flanges are given in;
  - a flange on a flange's end;
  - the refusals;
  - a tray and a channel unfolded and refolded to what they were.

  Golden sections, the `sheet` fuzz target's flanges, benches and the bar are plan 11.D's.
- **Limits.**
  - Flanges go on edges between a side and a planar wall square to it only.
  - A setback of 0 at a concave corner leaves the other leg's material against the bend's end face, which recognition doesn't see (see Reliefs).
  - A tab or slot exactly `t` wide between two walls square to the sides reads as a sharp bend (two planes `t` apart facing apart, overlapping), so a setback of `t` beside a relief is refused `NotSheetAfter`.
  - Flanges on different side planes aren't checked against each other before the booleans.
