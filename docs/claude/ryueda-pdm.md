## Parts and properties (decided in plan 10.B, design 629)

- **Parts.** A design project's designs and a library project's components are ryupdm parts; a design project's `self` library holds none (question 852). `ryueda::Eda` is ryupdm's `Product` (`src/pdm/mod.rs`); ryueda links ryupdm's core only (`default-features = false`). Answers say a part's kind as `"design"` or `"component"`; ryupdm's own part kinds wait for 10.D (question 877).
- **ryupdm for a second product.** Each reader of ryupdm's own files names the product reading it (`…_in(…, product)`; every `…_for::<P>` passes `P::NAME`), so ryueda's messages say "upgrade ryueda" and ryucad's read as they always have. `Product::defaults` and `Product::shared` have default bodies, so ryucad's `Cad` is unchanged.
- **Files.** `ryupdm.json`, `properties/<part>.json` and `releases/<part>/<label>.json` sit beside `ryueda.json`, in the layout (`ROOT_FILES`, `git::FOLDERS`, `project_file`), the session (`Project::tracked`) and the attributes. `props` commands are PDM's; `project edit --property` is designator properties (design 236).
- **Defaults.** A component's `name` (its `value`), `description` and `make_buy` (`buy`: every library component is bought, question 875), and a design's `name`, where `properties/` doesn't set them: read, never written, never revising (`Product::defaults`, `PartProperties::effective`; `props show` says `from: product`). Faults are judged on the file's values alone.
- **Shared files.** The manifest, `erc.json`, blocks, rails, classes and the library's components, symbols and footprints may belong to any part (`Product::shared`): the commit guard treats a staged one as touching every part with records. A library component is a part too (`part_of`).
- **Content.** `ryueda.content.1` (`src/pdm/content.rs`): BLAKE3 over length-prefixed pieces, the tag and the default unit first. A component: its file, its symbol, its footprints. A design: the manifest's properties and schematic settings, its design file, each block it reaches from its root (block file, components, nets, instances by name, layout), the rails its components' pins and instances' ports name and the classes they and the nets name, `erc.json`, then the `self` components placed with their objects. Not style, lint, standards, sourcing or models' bytes (a footprint names its model's BLAKE3). Another project's component at `working` has no hash (`component_not_released`); a pinned one's piece is its pin (see Library labels).
- **What a part's revision reads.** `PART_FOLDERS` and `part_files` take the whole `library/` folder, models and `standards.json` included: ERC reports a footprint's model missing (`model_file_missing`) when they aren't read, and results worked out again at a commit must match those recorded. The content hash still never reads a model's bytes. (10.B's executor widened the plan's list.)
- **The PDM revision** guards PDM writes: BLAKE3 over the project's revision and the PDM files' bytes (`pdm::revision`, answers' `pdm_revision`, writes' `expected_revision`); the project's revision is unchanged (question 876).

## Releases (decided in plan 10.B)

- Records are ryupdm's at format 2 (`built_with: {product: "ryueda", version, commit}`); `results` is `{netlist, erc}` for a design (BLAKE3 of the flattened netlist's and the configured ERC findings' canonical JSON), worked out over the part's files alone (`release::part_source`) so `release check --results` agrees; none for a component.
- Readiness (`release::readiness`): a component's library errors and its properties; a design's load and doctor errors, schematic errors and `layout_stale`, ERC errors unwaived, `component_not_released`, every placed line without a maker part number (`part_unidentified`, read from `erc::placed_by_libcomp` whatever its category or the severity ERC gives it: ERC's own skips often-generic categories), supply errors, and its properties. Supply findings are reported (`supply`), never recorded.
- `release propose` takes several parts or `--all` (parts in work or modified) in one logged change; one part alone is refused whole as ryucad's is; `--dry-run` answers each part's label and problems. `obsolete`, `list` (with `inherited` in a fork), `check [--results]` and `sync` (tags `ryupdm/<folder>/<part>/<label>`, records under approval rules left to the service) follow ryucad's.
- The session-free steps are `ryupdm::release`'s (`next_for`, `obsolete_target`, `listed`, `approval`, `state_value`, `sync`, `refs_key`, `guard_for`); ryucad keeps its own copy until the follow-up issue moves it (question 874). The release branch as read is kept in the session by its refs (`Session::pdm`).
- Git: the PDM files have `.gitattributes` lines through ryueda's drivers (which moved the `merge.breakout.clashes.files` golden, approved), merge by ryupdm's rules (records whole, settings per key), and textconv through `ryupdm::git::textconv`. `merge status` adds `pdm_broke` (judged once every file merged, when the project has PDM files) with the `withdraw` repair for a proposal; a property fault offers no choice (`props set` settles it). The shared hook runs the commit guard outside merges too. `diff` gains `pdm` when it moved; `pdm` subscribers hear `pdm.changed`, the first look telling nobody.
- `ryueda check` gains a `release` section only when there are release findings, so reports without PDM read as before.
- Tests: `tests/pdm.rs` (in process and through the daemon), the `pdm.*` goldens (a breakout of their own, so no other key moves), the `eda_pdm` fuzz target and the smoke's release. A record committed on `main` is official at once (`main` is the default release branch): tests of proposals work on another branch.

## Library labels (decided in plan 10.C, design 629)

- **Pins.** In the manifest: `libraries.<alias>.labels.<component>`, with the library's `project` written once a component is pinned (decision 1, question 900), format 3. An alias with a `url` is a library in another repository at `path` inside it (`""` for its root), and names its `project`. A manifest without either writes its old bytes. There is no `lib remove` (question 901): an alias is removed by editing the manifest.
- **Reading.** `ryueda::pin` reads each pinned component at its label's official commit (same repository: the library's release branch read through `ryupdm::history::Releases`, memoised by refs key) or locked commit (`ryueda.lock`), with its symbol, footprints, models and standards there, in a library of its own (base `@<alias>/<label>`, judged by `library::judge_own`). Two components pinned at different labels may bring different versions of one footprint. `Project::pinned` holds each pin or why it can't be read; `Project::home`, `placed_symbol` and `placed_footprint` are how placements read them, and `libcomp(SELF, …)` is pin-aware, so netlists, ERC, renders and `check` see the pinned objects. A pin that doesn't resolve is its placement's file fault, as an unusable working library is.
- **The resolver.** `pin::Resolve` (`Pins` in a session, `NoPins` outside a repository, a fake in tests) also gives an alias's releases (`releases_of`), a file of its project at a commit (`file_at`) and the dependency cache's reader. The session keeps a key of the pinned libraries' release-branch refs and reads the project again when it moves, so a label made official or obsolete shows at the next request; a project with no pins computes no key.
- **Content.** A design's hash adds, after its `self` components, each other project's component placed, in the order first placed: `pin`, the project, the component and the label; another repository's adds the content hash `ryueda.lock` holds (`dependency_unlocked` without it). An unpinned placement is still `component_not_released`.
- **Readiness.** `component_not_released` for an unpinned placement, the pin's error for one that doesn't resolve, `component_obsolete` for one an obsolete record names.
- **`check`'s `deps` section.** Present only when it has findings: `lock_invalid`, `dependency_unlocked`, `lock_unused`, then pin errors (`dependency_not_fetched`, `dependency_rewritten`, `dependency_project_mismatch`, `label_not_found`, `label_not_official`), `component_obsolete` and `component_outdated` (warnings). Doctor's `dependency_unlocked` and `lock_unused` are left out of `check`'s doctor section and readiness, which report them already.
- **Other repositories.** URL aliases are fetched into ryueda's own dependency cache (`RYUEDA_DEPS_DIR`, else `deps` in ryueda's cache), keeping ryupdm's `refs/ryucad/head` name (decision 5). They have no working library (`library_foreign_working`, which isn't a fault of the project; `lib pin --working` refuses it). `lib add --url [--path]`, `lib fetch` and `lib update` are long requests, fetched without the daemon's lock (`libdeps::prepare`).
- **The lock.** `ryueda.lock`: ryupdm's lock format (`ryu_doc::lock`), kind `ryueda.lock`, a root file the session tracks, holding exactly the url aliases' pins (`via` always empty: a library project names no libraries). `lib pin` and `lib update` write it with the manifest in one change; an empty lock removes the file. It merges per label (`ryupdm::git::lock_atom`) and diffs through the generic textconv. Doctor's `deps_lock` repair writes it again offline from the cache; when that can't (a label not fetched) the issue names `ryueda lib fetch`.
- **Commands.** `lib pin <alias>/<component>… --label L | --latest | --working`, `lib outdated`, `lib parts <alias>` (each component's labels latest first, `released`, `superseded` or `obsolete`, number and name), `lib fetch [alias…]`, `lib update [alias…] [--to L] [--offline] [--dry-run]` (what moved, what the lock moved, the ERC errors the move brings; a component with no current label stays where it is, listed in `skipped`, and a local label `--to` names must resolve first; only the url aliases among those named are fetched, and one the daemon didn't prefetch is fetched under its lock), `lib uses [--rev]` (each design's placements from other projects by alias, component and label, at the revision and in each official release).
- **`diff`.** `deps` (the lock's labels added, removed and changed) and `pins` (each pin moved, `working` for none) blocks, only when they moved.
- **Golden moved.** `.gitattributes` gains `ryueda.lock merge=ryueda diff=ryueda text eol=lf`, which moved `merge.breakout.clashes.files`; `pdm.pins` and `pdm.pins.lock` are new.
- **ryupdm's neutral pieces.** The lock type in `ryu_doc::lock`, its kind a parameter (ryucad's calls through `ryucad_doc::doc::lock::CadLock`, its bytes unchanged); `history::file_at`; `deps::read` and `deps::parts` without `cad`, with `Commands` naming each product's commands; `Reader::verify_for::<P>` and `read::official_with` beside ryucad's own `Reader::label` and `lock::official`, which stay as they were (decision 3, question 902).

## BOMs and where-used (decided in plan 10.D, design 629)

- **Part kinds.** `ryupdm::product::PartKind` (`part`, `assembly`, `design`, `component`); `Product::parts` gives each part's kind, `product::part_ids` the IDs alone. ryucad reads `part_kind` from each `part.json` (`part` when it says nothing or doesn't read); ryueda's come from the project's kind.
- **Lines, not a tree.** ryupdm's ungated `lines` module is what a product's line BOM shares: `Line` (key, part, project, revision, quantity, properties with where each came from, the product's columns), `columns` (core, then the product's, then declared custom properties), `csv`, `csv_grouped` and `diff`. ryucad's tree BOM (`bom`, `structure`, `where_used`, feature `cad`) is unchanged and shares only the CSV's quoting and the custom properties' list. `Product::children` waits for ryuplm 6.
- **A design's lines** (`pdm::bom::lines_of`) come from the flattened netlist, keyed by part (`self/<id>`, `<project ID>:<id>`, else `<alias>/<id>`), effective value and footprint; the revision (label or `working`) isn't in the key, so a diff pairs a line across a pin bump. Populated occurrences count (times `build_quantity` with `--build`); DNP ones are listed in `dnp`, never counted. `refdes` and `dnp` are in designator order, joined with `,`.
- **Check.** `check`'s report gains a `bom` section (and its counts) only when a design has unidentified lines in a project with `ryupdm.json`; a revision's `check` and projects without PDM report as before.
- **Properties.** A line reads its part's `properties/<c>.json` at the pin's commit, a sibling's at `working` from its folder (or the commit read), over the component's defaults (`name` its value, `description`, `make_buy: buy`); `self` lines have defaults only.
- **Revisions.** `--rev` is a Git revision or `label:L`, the commit where the design's release `L` became official (`label_not_official`, `label_not_found`). The working files' BOM borrows the session's project; a revision's reads it through `Session::at`. `--out <file>` writes the CSV beside where the CLI runs (implies `--format csv`).
- **Diff.** `bom diff` compares revision, quantity, `refdes`, `dnp`, `value`, `footprint`, `manufacturer`, `mpn`, `alternates` and properties (marked `revising`), never offers.
- **Identification.** `bom_unidentified` (warning) in `check`'s `bom` section, only in projects with `ryupdm.json`; readiness lists it per line as a `release_not_ready` problem (`part`, `refdes`), replacing 10.B's per-component `part_unidentified`; this moved one golden, `pdm.breakout.dry_run`, as the coxswain approved.
- **Where-used** (`ryueda where-used <part>`, `bom.where_used`) runs in a library project. A design project depends on the library when an alias names its project ID, or with none resolves to its folder. Rows come from this repository's ryueda projects (`folder` relative to the work tree), then other checkouts (`--in`, else the `pdm.dependents` preference, unless `--no-dependents`), each working and in its official releases (`release`, `state`, `commit`, the component's `label`). `--direct` changes nothing in ryueda (every placement is direct) and is echoed.
- **Bars** (`tests/bar.rs`): a 10,000-occurrence BOM through a warm daemon in 50 ms, `release propose --all` of 10,000 components in 10 s, `release list` over them in 1 s, where-used over 10 checkouts in 1 s.
