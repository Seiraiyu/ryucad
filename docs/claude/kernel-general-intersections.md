## General intersections (decided in phase 8)

The design is document 94 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 8.A.

- **Surfaces over jets.** `geom::Sheet` takes every surface: a NURBS by the hull of its Bézier patches' enclosures (`NurbsSurface::jet_box`, sharing the mesher's patch arithmetic, so mesh bounds keep their bits), an extrusion and a revolution by their curve's (`Curve::jet_box`). `intersect::cells` gives any surface's first cells near a box: patches, curve pieces, eighths of a turn, and the range a box reaches along an unbounded direction.
- **Curve–surface.** `curve_surface` meets a NURBS, extrusion or revolution by subdivision of `(t, u, v)` (`intersect::sheet_cs`): boxes excluded by enclosures, crossings by Krawczyk's test, contacts certified along a path that follows the curve to second order (a curve lying on a surface needs boxes only as short as curvature allows), a tangency the one root of `((C - S) · S_u, (C - S) · S_v, C' · N)`. Hits follow the analytic rules.
- **One implicit form.** Any other surface against an analytic one is traced in its own parameters against the analytic implicit form (`intersect::sheet_trace`, the pipe tracer generalized: a pipe's grid as before, bit for bit; periodic directions in sixteenths, an extrusion's height over the range the region reaches), cut at a revolution's poles and a NURBS's collapsed rows (`NurbsSurface::collapsed_rows`), and fitted with that surface read through its signed distance from its foot.
- **No implicit form.** Two such surfaces are traced in their four parameters (`intersect::general`): pairs of cells excluded (boxes apart, or a component of `E = A - B`, or `n · E` along either cell's middle normal, of one sign by the mean value form), simple (a component of `N_A × N_B` of one sign: a regular curve, monotone along it, so no loop hides) or singular. A simple pair's crossings on its eight faces are solved by Krawczyk's test and matched across neighbours by their certified boxes; a pair holding more than two is split again; a singular cluster is settled by one certified point of tangency (`((A - B) · B_s, (A - B) · B_t, N_B · A_u, N_B · A_v) = 0`, regular at a touch where the squared distance's system is not). Branches on two surfaces with no implicit form are fitted with a local foot and certified by a pcurve bound that encloses `d²S(P) - C''` as a vector (`fit::sheet_pcurve_bound`), so the curvatures cancel.
- **Tangency along a curve.** Past 20 splits a pair where Gauss-Newton steps on the tangency system rest inside it, within the touch reach, is kept whole. A cluster with no single certified point is marched along the system's null direction (Newton on three of its equations, the fastest parameter held) until it closes, leaves a domain's edge or the region; every part of its first surface's cells outside a strip `sqrt(2 × size × reach)` round the curve is certified apart from the second (`general::apart`), and no transversal arc may end in it. It becomes a tangent branch, fitted along its pcurve on the first surface (`fit_along`) and certified against the second up to the reach. `surface_surface` tries this path where the implicit one is uncertain.
- **Coincidence.** `intersect::coincide` also names equal surfaces; a NURBS (or a revolution by its exact NURBS form) certified patch by patch within resolution of an analytic implicit form; an extrusion along a cylinder's axis whose curve the cylinder's form certifies; two NURBS whose nets agree after one is turned, mapped affinely onto the other's domain and both refined to each other's knots; and a NURBS that is such a part of another. `surface_surface` asks it before any path.
- **Self-intersection check.** `face_face` takes every pair (the patch-sign test first, as a fast way apart). `beside` takes a face on any surface; another surface with no implicit form has no side, so every part outside the strip is certified apart from it instead. A rail's 256 pieces are halved where they lie wider across both parameters than half the strip, so rails off the parameter lines cover the parts beside them. Faces touching at a vertex they share touch at a corner of both faces' boxes, where the region's sides cut the trace through the touch: a pair that fails there is intersected again in a region reaching an eighth of the smaller face's size past it (`face_face_within`). Coincident faces place an edge that can't be fitted onto the first surface (a tolerant edge read from a file) by its own pcurve, when its face is on that very surface. Swept and NURBS bodies check with no `CannotCheck`, as does every body healed from the OCCT files and the committed corpus but `nist_ctc_05`, whose spline fillets roll on surfaces split into several faces: 25 pairs tangent along a rail on another face of the other's surface, or on two copies of one torus placed differently, can't be checked yet (ryustep's `tests/checks.rs`; plan 8.F). Three corpus files take minutes each to check (`nist_ctc_04`, `nist_ftc_07`, `nist_ftc_10`), so CI checks the rest and they, like `nist_ctc_05`, are run by name.
- **Booleans.** The table takes a face on another surface (NURBS, swept, a pipe against a pipe) only where it is apart from the other operand, else `Unsupported`: splitting such faces is plan 8.C's.
- **Testing and limits.** Unit tests per module; `tests/intersect_props.rs` (curves against the analytic surfaces' exact NURBS forms agree with the implicit path; issue #83's reproducer); `tests/general_props.rs` (release only, its own CI step: spline–analytic and spline–spline pairs against sampling, splines touching along a line and a circle); the `general` fuzz target; golden hashes (`general` section). A fitted span's curvature in `fit::pcurve_bound` is now scaled to the span's parameter (it was understated by `w²` after halving). Limits: a curve along a bounded surface's domain edge (a ruled chamfer's end ruling lying on its end face) is `Uncertain`; so is a surface passing within tens of microns of a spline's pole (a collapsed row has no normal in the four-parameter trace; issue #128); a pair of splines takes about 0.1 to 1 s, a tangency round a whole circle about 10 s (NURBS enclosures and the strip's certification dominate); `beside` against a surface with no implicit form is 20 to 40 times slower than against an implicit one.
