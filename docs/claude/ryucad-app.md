
## Measure in the app (decided in ryucad phase 10)

The design is document 198 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 2, plan 10.A.

- **Measure tool.** Tools > Measure turns the tool on (`core::measure::Tool`): while on, a click on a face, edge or vertex is measured, not selected; a second pick measures the pair, a third (or the same one again) starts over. Each pick sends `measure` with the picks (`Pending::Measure`), and an answer for picks that have moved on is dropped. The Measure window lists the answer's values (`(exact)` or `± bound` in SI units) with Copy (the picks, then a line per value); between two entities the viewport draws a dimension line between the nearest points with the distance as its label. Esc, or closing the window, leaves the tool, before Esc clears the selection. The picks are measured again when their part changes.
- **Mass properties.** Tools > Mass properties weighs the solid or occurrence of the first selected entity (`core::measure::weighed`), else the one part or assembly shown, and lists mass, volume, area, the centre of mass and the principal moments, each part's material and mass, and the warnings; the viewport marks the centre of mass. Weigh again takes the selection anew; the panel weighs again when its part changes.
- **Clearance.** Tools > Clearance lists the shown assembly's pairs closest first, a pair at 0 marked as touching or interfering, each with Show, which frames both nearest points (`View::fit_points`) and draws the line between them; it asks again when the assembly changes. Closing either window ends what it draws.
- **Order with phase 11.** These are plain menu items and windows, as the app has them now; when phase 11's command registry and tiles land, they move into it under `measure.*` ids (`measure.tool`, `measure.mass`, `measure.clearance`).
- **Testing.** `core::measure` unit tests (picks and stale answers, lines, mass and clearance answers, what a selection weighs), and window tests: two faces measured by clicks (8 mm exact, nothing selected, Esc leaves the tool), and Mass properties and Clearance listing a frame of three bolts, Show framing the pair.
