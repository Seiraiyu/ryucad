## Sheets and views (decided in ryucad phase 9)

The design is document 417 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **`ryu-draw`.** A product-neutral crate (`ryu-math`, `pdf-writer`, `subsetter`, `ttf-parser`, `miniz_oxide`; no kernel, no `serde`; `tests/deps.rs` holds it to that): `Item` (a `Path` of exact segments, `Line`, `Arc`, `Ellipse` and rational `Bezier`, stroked or filled; or one line of Inter text), line styles (`Standard::{Asme, Iso}`, `LineKind`: ASME's 0.6/0.3 mm and dashes in millimetres, ISO 128's 0.5/0.25 mm and dashes in multiples of the thin width), Inter's metrics and subsetter (`font`, the font moved from ryueda), and the PDF and SVG writers. PDF and SVG draw arcs and rational pieces as cubics (`Path::cubics`: a quarter turn at most, rational pieces halved until within `FIT`, a micrometre); DXF keeps every kind (9.B). ryueda's schematic PDF is written through it, every golden byte as it was.
- **The projection codec.** `ryukei::codec::encode_projection` and `decode_projection` (`ryukei.projection.1`): every piece and each placed body's silhouettes, a shared NURBS written once, bit for bit. The kernel's only change in phase 9.
- **Files.** `parts/<part>/drawings/<id>.json` (kind `ryucad.drawing`, format 1, `doc::drawing`): `name`, `standard` (`asme`, `iso`; the project's unless written), `projection` (`first_angle`, `third_angle`; the standard's unless written), `unit`, `precision`, `sheets` by key (`s1`: `name`, `size` `iso_a0` to `iso_a4` and `ansi_a` to `ansi_e`, `orientation`, `template`, `scale` `"1:2"`), `views` by key (`v1`: `sheet`, `at`, `scale`, `hidden` `off`/`dashed`, `tangent` `thin`/`off`/`phantom`, `label`, and `base` with `of`, `rev` and `orient` (a standard view's name, a named view, or `{normal_to: <face>}`), `projected` with `from`, `side` (`top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`, `back`, or `iso` and the cube's corners) and `offset`, `auxiliary` with `from`, `normal_to` and `offset`), and `fields`. Keys are a letter and the next number. Positions are sheet millimetres, written as quantities. Annotations are 9.C's. Each part loads its drawings (`Part::drawings`, each file's error in its place); they count in its revision, tagged after the explodes, and with them the bytes of each project template they name, so parts without drawings hash as before.
- **Templates.** Built-in ones (`drawing::template::compiled`) are made from the paper: `asme-a` to `asme-e` (10 mm margins on A and B, 12.7 mm on C to E; zones about 2 in on B and larger, numbered from the right, lettered from the bottom; a 177.8 mm title block of TITLE, DRAWN, DATE, MATERIAL, SIZE, DWG NO, REV, SCALE, STANDARD, SHEET) and `iso-a4` to `iso-a0` (ISO 5457's 20 mm filing margin and 10 mm elsewhere, zones about 50 mm but on A4; an ISO 7200 block 180 mm wide). A project adds its own at `templates/<id>.json` (kind `ryucad.drawing_template`, `doc::drawing_template`): `area`, `keep_out`, thick `lines`, `thin` lines and `texts` with `{field}`s. `templates/` is a root folder: sessions track it, Git revisions read it, and `.gitattributes` gains `templates/*.json merge=ryucad diff=ryucad text eol=lf` (the `diff` and `scale` goldens moved). A field reads the part's PDM properties, then `name` (the drawing's), `number` (else the part ID), `revision` (the latest official release's label, `*` while it is modified), `date` (that release's official commit's day; empty in work, no clock), `scale`, `sheet`, `sheets`, `size`, `standard`, and the drawing's `fields` last. A name with no value is empty.
- **The project's standard.** `ryucad.json`'s `drawing_standard` (`asme` written as nothing: ASME unless it says ISO, question 337), manifest format 4 only when written; `config drawing.standard`.
- **Releases.** ryupdm's content hash gains a seventh piece: when the part has drawings, `ryucad.drawings`, each drawing's ID and bytes in ID order, then each project template they name (`templates`, its ID, its bytes or `missing`). Parts without drawings hash as before; editing a released part's drawing makes it `modified` (question 336).
- **Git.** Textconv gives each sheet and view a line (`views.v2 = projected from v1, top, offset 70 mm, sheet s1`). The merge driver merges per member and field; a view whose `type` changes on either side, or one both sides added differently, is one value, as are a view's `orient` and `rev`; two sides moving one view is one conflict at its `at`. `diff` gives a part whose drawings differ a `drawings` block by pointer, and the project's `templates`; `diff --model` adds `drawings.model` by drawing: the views whose built drawing (place, box and items, `ViewBuilt::fingerprint`) differs, `added`, `removed`, `changed`. An `id_collision`'s renumbering rewrites theirs' references in drawings (only references). Doctor reports a drawing or template that doesn't read as `invalid_document`, one not canonical `non_canonical`, anything else in `drawings/` or `templates/` `unknown_file`.
- **Building** (`ryucad::drawing::build`). Each view's frame: a base view's from its `orient` (`view::StandardView::axes`, a named view's eye and target, or `server::normal_to_face`); a projected one's a quarter turn of its parent's (top looks along the parent's up with the parent's towards as down; a diagonal by the cube's direction in the parent's frame); an auxiliary one's along the face's normal, refused `invalid_param` with `reason: foreshortened` unless its parent sees the face edge-on. Every view of a family keeps the middle of its source's box at its centre's place, so projected views line up with their parent exactly. Sources are the model's occurrences (`Session::model`) at `working` or a label. Projections come from the session (`Session::projections`, at most 256), the disk cache (kind `projections`, kept on this machine only: the shared cache's protocol names the other three) or `ryukei::project::view_with` side by side under the request's cancel (phase `project`, unit `views`); a cancel inside the kernel keeps nothing of that view.
- **Pieces to items** (`drawing::curves`). Visible pieces are thick continuous lines, hidden ones thin dashes only where the view asks, smooth edges by `tangent`, uncertain ones visible in `drawing::WARNING` with a `drawing_view_uncertain` finding. A piece is drawn only where it is the nearest of those it overlaps, and a source's consecutive pieces of one kind are joined (lines running on along a line made one). Images stay exact: lines, arcs (`Arc` by the frame's turn and sense), ellipses, and a NURBS as its Bézier pieces (interior knots raised to the degree).
- **Layout.** A sheet with no scale takes the largest of `SCALES` (10:1 to 1:1000) at which every view's box, grown by `DIM_ROOM` (12 mm), lies in the template's area clear of its keep-out boxes and each other (`FIT_SLACK`, a micrometre, for places written to a thousandth). A base view with no `at` goes with its family (the views placed along it) at the area's upper left, the next family to its right; projected views go along their row or column (third angle: top above, right to the right; first angle mirrored) just clear of their parent, each grown by its room; an isometric with no `at` at the upper right. Every place ryucad chose, and a scale it chose, is written back by the next write (`drawing.create`, `edit`, `layout`), so the file is what is drawn. A view the kernel refuses is `drawing_view_failed` (`reason`, `kernel`): its box crossed out, the sheet still built.
- **Requests and CLI.** `drawing.list`, `create` (`--views standard`: a base front, its top and right and an isometric), `edit` (`ops`: `add` a sheet or view, `remove`, `set` fields, `move` a view's `at` or `offset`, all or nothing, built in a proposed session first so what a build refuses writes nothing; CLI shortcuts `drawing view add`, `drawing move`, `drawing remove`), `delete`, `rename` (a new ID), `layout` (every place and scale chosen anew; `--dry-run`): each a logged, undoable change guarded by the part's revision and proposable (`annotation::propose::PROPOSABLE`). `get` (`schema: 1`, `--items` every item's exact geometry), `describe` (each sheet in words), `render` (PNG at 4 px/mm by default, or SVG; the bytes after the answer) and `export` (PDF of every sheet, a page each with bookmarks by sheet and view, or one sheet's SVG; the daemon writes `out` as `export` does, never over a project file) take `rev`. Long requests: `get`, `describe`, `render`, `export`, `layout`. New codes: `drawing_not_found`, `drawing_view_failed`, `drawing_view_uncertain`, `drawing_reference_unresolved`, `drawing_reference_not_shown`, `template_not_found`.
- **Writers.** `drawing::output`: SVG on white (`ryu_draw::svg`), PNG by resvg over it with the bundled Inter only (ryucad gains `resvg`, question 338), PDF through `ryu_draw::pdf`. The same drawing gives the same bytes on every platform and thread count.
- **Check.** `check` builds every drawing of the parts it checks (`source: drawing`): a view that fails is an error, uncertain pieces a warning; a drawing that doesn't read is doctor's.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`ryu-draw`'s writers and styles, `doc::drawing`'s and `doc::drawing_template`'s round trips and refusals, templates fitting their paper, curves keeping their ends and kinds, a block's three views and isometric against its edges' lengths, hidden bores dashed only when asked, an auxiliary view's true size and its foreshortened refusal, first angle's placement on an ISO sheet, doctor, merge, renumbering, the content hash, `diff --model`); `tests/cli.rs`'s `drawings_from_the_cli` both ways; the `drawings` golden section (the showcase's standard views and an ISO drawing: `drawing.get` with items, PDF, SVG and PNG, one thread and four).
- **Limits.** Dimensions, annotation and the lint are 9.C's; the app, corpus drawings, property test, fuzzing and bars 9.D's. Layout fills from the upper left and never moves a view the file places.

## Sections, details, flat and exploded views (decided in ryucad phase 9)

The design is document 417 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.B (phase 4).

- **Files.** Drawings stay format 1 and gain four view types:
  - `section`: `from`, `cut`, `flip`, `unsectioned` (instance paths left whole), placed by `offset` or `at`. The `cut` is one of:
    - `{plane, offset?}`: a plane reference of what the view draws, an origin plane `xy`/`yz`/`zx`, a planar face `{feature, name}` or a datum plane `{feature}`;
    - `{origin, normal}`;
    - `{through: [point…]}`, 2 to 32 points, each three lengths or a reference.
  - `detail`: `from`, `centre` (a point), `radius` (in the model), and an optional `scale`.
  - `flat`: `of`, `solid`, `notes` (true unless written).
  - `exploded`: `of`, `explode`, `fraction` (1 unless written), `orient`, `trails`.

  A section's and a detail's `label` is its letter: one or two capitals but I, O and Q, required, and unique in the drawing (`letter_ok`). `drawing.edit`'s `add` gives one left out the next free letter, skipping S, X and Z too (`next_letter`). The merge driver takes `cut`, `centre` and `unsectioned` as one value each. Textconv writes a line per view (`section A from v1, on b/<hex> offset 5 mm`).
- **The cut.** A full section cuts away what its plane's normal points to, flipped by `flip`. An offset section cuts away what lies left of its first segment, as its parent sees it, flipped by `flip`; a step turning back along the first segment is `invalid_param` with `reason: folds`. The plane is resolved in the model the view reads (`section::named_at`, a label's commit). The view looks along the cut's normal reversed, up its parent's up unless that runs along the normal, else away from its parent's eye.
- **Placement.** A section that looks along its parent's plane is placed on the parent's row or column by the drawing's projection angle, as a projected view is, and its `offset` is written back. Any other is placed by its `at`. Layout places families with written places first. A new family goes where 9.A's rule puts it, unless that would meet a family already placed; then it takes the first free room down the area from the top left, in 5 mm steps. 9.A's drawings keep their bits.
- **Cutting** (`drawing::section::cut_all`). Each solid of the source is cut side by side under the request's cancel (`Phase::Project`, unit `sections`), and an `unsectioned` path leaves its solids whole.
  - A full section goes through `project::section_with`, the plane brought into the body's frame.
  - An offset section goes through `boolean_with`, subtracting a stepped tool. The tool is the polygon of the points seen along the parent's line of sight, run on past both ends by the solids' diagonal, closed round the side cut away, and extruded that far each way. Its caps are the result's faces named from the tool's.
  - Cut bodies are kept by body hash, pose and cut, in the session and on disk (`Kind::Section`, folder `sections`, this machine only, `ryucad.section.1`: the caps' names, then `encode_body`).
  - A kernel refusal fails that view alone (`drawing_view_failed`).
  - Each distinct view is projected once: a detail reuses its parent's projection, cut source and caps.
- **Drawing.** Caps facing the view are hatched with lines `ryu_draw::hatch` computes, even-odd over their exact boundaries, in thin lines on DXF's `HATCH` layer: no clip groups, no DXF `HATCH` entity.
  - Solids alternate 45° and 135°, every other pair at six tenths the spacing.
  - The spacing is 2.5 mm up to ANSI B and A3, 3.5 mm up to ANSI D and A1, else 5 mm.
  - An offset section's steps are edge-on: neither hatched nor drawn.
- **Marks.** A cutting-plane line goes on each section's parent, on whatever sheet the parent is drawn on.
  - It is stroked as `LineKind::Cutting`. ISO adds thick 5 mm ends and thick lengths at each bend.
  - Each end has a 6 mm stem, a filled arrowhead along the line of sight, and the letter 5 mm high.
  - A cut square to the parent's plane draws no line.
  - A detail's circle and letter go on its parent.
  - Captions read `SECTION A-A` and `DETAIL B (2:1)` for ASME, `A-A` and `B (2:1)` for ISO.
- **Details.** A detail keeps its parent's pieces inside its circle through `ryukei::project::inside_circle`, the one kernel function this plan added. An uncertain crossing counts the piece uncertain and names it. Its hatch is kept inside the circle. Its scale is its own, else twice its parent's (the parent's own, else the sheet's), in lowest terms.
- **Flat and exploded views.**
  - A flat view comes from `sheet_metal::flat::of` and draws the pattern itself, not a projection: loops thick, bend lines thin phantoms, and their notes (`ryuformats::dxf::note` with `°`).
  - An exploded view projects the assembly with each occurrence's offset applied after its pose, and draws its trails as thin phantoms.
- **DXF.** `ryu_draw::dxf::write` writes AutoCAD R2000 on the skeleton of `ryuformats::dxf`.
  - There is a layer per line kind, with that kind's linetype and lineweight, and `TEXT` in a style naming Inter.
  - Each segment is its own entity: `LINE`, `ARC`, `CIRCLE`, `ELLIPSE` on principal axes, or `SPLINE` (a Bézier piece of its degree, rational when it is). A filled arrowhead is a `SOLID`, and a colour other than black is ACI 30.
  - `drawing.export --format dxf [--unit mm|in] [--sheet S]` writes one sheet. `unit` is refused for PDF and SVG.
- **Testing.**
  - Unit tests: `ryu-draw`'s hatch and DXF, and `tests/dxf.rs`, which reads through `ryuformats`' test reader. `ryukei`'s `project::clip`. `doc::drawing`'s new kinds and refusals. `drawing`'s full section, offset section and detail. Textconv and merge.
  - `tests/drawings.rs`: caps against their areas through one hole and two, a flat view against `flat.get`, an exploded assembly, and a cut on a face across an edit.
  - `tests/cli.rs`'s `drawings_from_the_cli`: a section, a detail and a DXF in inches.
  - The `drawing_sections` golden section: the showcase cut and detailed, with `get`, PDF, SVG, DXF in mm and in, and PNG, on one thread and four. It runs in 3.2 s in a debug build on bc2 (Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus). An offset section in it took 146 s, so it is left out.
- **Limits.**
  - A cut square to its parent's plane has no cutting-plane line; draw the section from a view that sees the cut edge-on.
  - An assembly's section hatches each solid by its order, with no check of neighbours' caps.
  - Half, broken-out, removed and revolved sections are out of scope (design 417).
  - ezdxf references, the corpus drawings, the property test, fuzzing and the bars are 9.D's.

## Dimensions and annotation (decided in ryucad phase 9)

The design is document 417 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.C (phases 5 and 6).

- **Files.** A drawing gains `annotations` by key (format 1 still; written only when it has some, so 9.A's and 9.B's files keep their bytes) and `general_tolerance` (text, shown above the title block). `doc::drawing_annotation`: `linear` (`a`, `b`, or one entity alone: a straight edge, a planar face edge on, a circle edge on or a round face side on, measured over its own extent, `along` `horizontal`/`vertical`/`aligned`), `angular` (two straight edges, an optional `vertex`), `radius`, `diameter`, `ordinate` (`datum`, `of`, `along`), `chamfer` and `hole_callout` (`of` a face) take a `Style`: `offset` (signed sheet mm: above or right positive, an aligned one left of `a`→`b`), `text` holding `<>` once, `prefix`, `suffix`, `tolerance` (`{symmetric}`, `{deviation: [upper, lower]}`, `{limits: [upper, lower]}`, `{fit: "H7"}` or `"H7/g6"`, `{basic: true}`, `{reference: true}`; angles for an angular one; none for callouts) and `precision`. Also `centre_mark`, `centre_line` (`of`, `to`), `note` (`text` up to 20 lines with title-block fields, `leader`), `balloon` (`of` an occurrence path), `bom_table` (`flat`, `depth`, `columns`, `item number name quantity` by default), `feature_control_frame` (`characteristic` of 14, one row or two for a composite frame: `tolerance`, `diameter`, `condition` `m l e p f t`, up to three `datums` each with `m` or `l`; `on` an entity or `to` a dimension), `datum_feature` (`letter`, `on`) and `surface_finish` (`on`, `ra`, `process` `any`/`removal`/`no_removal`). Keys by letter: `d` dimensions and callouts, `c` centre marks and lines, `n` notes, `b` balloons, `t` BOM tables, `g` frames and datum features, `f` finish. Each names a `view`; a note or table may stand on a `sheet` instead. Entities are text relative to the view's source: `<solid>/<name>`, `<instance>/…/<solid>/<name>`, or a datum's ID. `at` is relative to the view's centre (it moves with the view), a sheet's own note's or table's on the sheet. A frame's characteristic must name datums or not as ASME Y14.5 and ISO 1101 say (`invalid_document` at the row); a datum letter is used once.
- **Values.** Measured in the view's plane from the exact geometry (`annotate::value`): a vertex, an edge seen end on, a circle's or sphere's centre, a round face's axis end on, are points; a straight edge, a planar face edge on, a round face's axis side on, a datum line or plane edge on, are lines. Horizontal and vertical take each end's coordinate (a line must run square to it, `not_square`); an aligned one must be true length (the model's distance equal to the image's within 1e-9, else `invalid_param` with `reason: foreshortened`). Radius and diameter need the circle face on (a cylinder side on takes a diameter between its silhouettes; a radius there is `side_on`; a cone its largest circular edge). Angles need both edges in the view's plane. Values show in the drawing's unit at its precision (2 mm, 3 in), rounded half to even, ASME inches without the leading zero; angles in whole degrees unless `precision` says. Fits are ISO 286-2's tables (`drawing::fit`, integers in tenths of a micrometre, sizes to 3150 mm; a class the standard leaves undefined at the size is `invalid_param` with `reason: fit_undefined`); one class shows its deviations, `25.00 H7 (+0.021/0)`.
- **Callouts.** A hole callout reads its hole feature through the face's origin (a pattern's instance by its seed): `n×` (ASME `nX`) counting the solid's bores by feature, instance and point, `⌀D`, `THRU` or `↧depth`, a counterbore's `⌴ ⌀D ↧d` or a countersink's `⌵ ⌀D × angle°`. A chamfer callout reads its chamfer's group (`d × 45°`, `d1 × d2`, `d × θ`).
- **Drawing.** `ryu_draw::symbol`: ⌀ ⌴ ⌵ ↧, the 14 characteristics and circled modifiers as strokes on Inter's em; `Run`s of text and symbols; filled arrowheads and dots. `LineKind::Dimension` (thin, continuous, DXF layer `DIMENSION`) for dimension, extension and leader lines and frames. Text 3.5 mm (ISO, along its line, read from below or the right) or 3 mm (ASME, level, its line broken round it); arrows 3 × 1 mm; extension lines 1.5 mm off the feature and 2 mm (ISO) or 3 mm (ASME) past the line. Arrows go outside where the text and both don't fit. A dimension with no offset stands outside its view's box by `DIM_GAP` (8 mm) a slot, per view and side; its offset and its text's place are written back by the next `drawing.edit`, as views' places are.
- **Failures.** An annotation whose entity is gone is `drawing_reference_unresolved` (with `was`, `removed_by` and `candidates` from `Session::explain_gone` at `working`), one the view doesn't draw (hidden lines off, behind a section's cut, outside a detail) `drawing_reference_not_shown`; one that can't be measured keeps its own code. It is kept, drawn as its key and `?` in the warning colour at its place, and is an error in `check`. `drawing.edit` refuses a batch that adds or changes an annotation that fails, with every fault; one the model breaks later is reported, never refused.
- **The lint.** `drawing::lint`: `dwg_view_overlap` (10), `dwg_outside` (5), `dwg_text_overlap` (3), `dwg_dimension_crossing` (3), `dwg_dimension_to_hidden` (2), `dwg_duplicate` (4), `dwg_chain_closed` (4), `dwg_untoleranced` (1), `dwg_datum_missing` (5), `dwg_hole_uncalled` (3), `dwg_balloon_missing` (2, one balloon per part placed: a pattern's copies count as their seed's part); `dwg_hole_uncalled` counts a hole called out or given a diameter on its wall anywhere on the drawing; each finding `lint: true` with `points` and `fixes` (`drawing.edit` batches), each sheet's score 100 less its points, at least 0 (`scores`). `drawing_lint.json` (kind `ryucad.drawing_lint`) sets weights (0 to 100) and severities (`off`, `warning`, `error`) by rule: a root file, in the project's revision only when present, doctor's `invalid_document`; no attributes line (it merges as lines). `drawing.lint` (`--explain CODE`) and `drawing.lint.configure`.
- **Requests and CLI.** `drawing.edit` adds annotations (`{op: add, kind: annotation, value}`, the type's letter's next key), and sets, moves (`at` or `offset`) and removes them. `drawing.get` gives each sheet's `annotations` (value, text, entities, place, offset, error) and `scores`; `describe` each annotation in words. CLI `drawing dim add`, `drawing note add`, `drawing gdt add`, `drawing lint`, `drawing lint-configure`.
- **Git.** Textconv gives each annotation a line (`annotations.d3 = linear v1, a b/<hex>, b b/<hex>, tolerance symmetric 0.1 mm, along horizontal`); the merge driver merges annotations per member and field, a retyped one whole, its `tolerance`, `rows`, `columns` and `at` one value each; renumbering rewrites theirs' entity texts on views of the renumbered part.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`doc::drawing_annotation`, `doc::drawing_lint`, `ryu_draw::symbol`, `drawing::fit` against ISO 286-2's printed values, `annotate::{text, value}`, `lint`, merge, textconv, doctor); `tests/drawing_annotations.rs` (every dimension type against the plate's closed forms, a hole pattern's callout, foreshortened and hidden refusals, names following an edit and a cut taking an edge away with `removed_by`, the lint's fixes); the `drawing_annotations` golden section. `drawings` and `drawing_sections` moved: `drawing.get` gains `annotations`, `scores` and the lint's findings, and DXF gains the `DIMENSION` layer.
- **Limits.** Annotations on flat views are notes only. A default place can land on another annotation (the lint says so, `dwg_text_overlap`). A BOM table too tall for its sheet continues in blocks to the left, never on another sheet. ASME dimensions keep the precision's trailing zeros.

## Hardening (decided in ryucad phase 9)

The design is document 417 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.D (phase 8). The app's side is Drawings in the app in `ryucad-app.md`.

- **What the app reads.** `drawing.get --items` gives each item drawn from a view's curve its `entity` (`<part>/<solid>/<name>`, an occurrence's with its instance path), each view and annotation its `items` (a range of the sheet's), a linear dimension the `normal` its offset is measured along, and `file`, the drawing as written. Added fields under `--items` only (`schema: 1`), so only `get --items` answers moved.
- **An empty sheet chooses no scale.** A sheet with no view wrote back the largest standard scale (10:1: every scale fits nothing), so `drawing.create` with no views froze it there for every view added after. A chosen scale is written back only once the sheet has a view (`request::with_placements`). It moved the `drawing_sections` golden, whose `cuts` drawing is created empty: it was drawn at 10:1, off its paper.
- **Ranges within a knot's snap.** `ryukei::geom::chain2` snaps a NURBS range's ends to a knot within `KNOT_SNAP`; a range lying wholly within that of one knot (a hidden-line stretch halved there) snapped to nothing either side and panicked (`a trimmed curve has pieces`: the radial engine's crankcase drawn). Such a range takes the pieces it meets, cut exactly. Only ranges that panicked change: no ryukei golden moved.
- **A view retyped on one side.** The merge driver took a view whose `type` changed on either side as one value and conflicted it even when the other side left it alone (the `merge` target's drawing mode found it at once). A retype is one value only when both sides changed the view, differently; changed on one side it is that side's, as any value. No golden moved.
- **The corpus.** Each committed project's script makes its drawings through the CLI, as an agent: the gearbox's housing (standard views on ANSI C, its holes called out by the lint's fixes, a section through its bores and a detail of a bolt hole, laid out), each gear's standard views with its bore's diameter at H7, and the gearbox in isometric with its BOM and a balloon on every part (the lint's fixes again); the radial engine's crankcase and propeller, and the engine in isometric with its BOM; the printer frame's corner bracket with its holes called out, and the frame in isometric with its BOM and balloons. `model.py` gains `drawing`, `drawing_add`, `drawing_ops` and `take_fixes`. `manifest.json` records each drawing by `<part>/<id>`: its views (type, scale, pieces drawn, failure), its annotations' texts, its findings by code, each sheet's score and its PDF's BLAKE3.
- **What the corpus found.** ryukei can't certify a silhouette of the radial engine's assembly view, from any side tried: the engine's view fails (`drawing_view_failed`), the sheet keeps its crossed frame and BOM, and the manifest records it, so the engine's balloons wait for the kernel.
- **Reading PDFs back.** `crates/ryu-draw/tests/common/pdf.rs`, a reader of our own (the cross-reference table against every object, each page's size and its text through the font's ToUnicode map, the font file inflated, the bookmarks depth first), used by `ryu-draw`'s `tests/pdf.rs` and ryucad's `tests/drawings.rs` (a page per sheet showing every text its items hold, in order, a bookmark per sheet and view).
