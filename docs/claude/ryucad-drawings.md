## Sheets and views (decided in ryucad phase 9)

The design is document 417 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 9.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **`ryu-draw`.** A product-neutral crate (`ryu-math`, `pdf-writer`, `subsetter`, `ttf-parser`, `miniz_oxide`; no kernel, no `serde`; `tests/deps.rs` holds it to that): `Item` (a `Path` of exact segments, `Line`, `Arc`, `Ellipse` and rational `Bezier`, stroked or filled; or one line of Inter text), line styles (`Standard::{Asme, Iso}`, `LineKind`: ASME's 0.6/0.3 mm and dashes in millimetres, ISO 128's 0.5/0.25 mm and dashes in multiples of the thin width), Inter's metrics and subsetter (`font`, the font moved from ryueda), and the PDF and SVG writers. PDF and SVG draw arcs and rational pieces as cubics (`Path::cubics`: a quarter turn at most, rational pieces halved until within `FIT`, a micrometre); DXF keeps every kind (9.B). ryueda's schematic PDF is written through it, every golden byte as it was.
- **The projection codec.** `ryukei::codec::encode_projection` and `decode_projection` (`ryukei.projection.1`): every piece and each placed body's silhouettes, a shared NURBS written once, bit for bit. The kernel's only change in phase 9.
- **Files.** `parts/<part>/drawings/<id>.json` (kind `ryucad.drawing`, format 1, `doc::drawing`): `name`, `standard` (`asme`, `iso`; the project's unless written), `projection` (`first_angle`, `third_angle`; the standard's unless written), `unit`, `precision`, `sheets` by key (`s1`: `name`, `size` `iso_a0` to `iso_a4` and `ansi_a` to `ansi_e`, `orientation`, `template`, `scale` `"1:2"`), `views` by key (`v1`: `sheet`, `at`, `scale`, `hidden` `off`/`dashed`, `tangent` `thin`/`off`/`phantom`, `label`, and `base` with `of`, `rev` and `orient` (a standard view's name, a named view, or `{normal_to: <face>}`), `projected` with `from`, `side` (`top`, `bottom`, `left`, `right`, `back`, or `iso` and the cube's corners) and `offset`, `auxiliary` with `from`, `normal_to` and `offset`), and `fields`. Keys are a letter and the next number. Positions are sheet millimetres, written as quantities. Annotations are 9.C's. Each part loads its drawings (`Part::drawings`, each file's error in its place); they count in its revision, tagged after the explodes, and with them the bytes of each project template they name, so parts without drawings hash as before.
- **Templates.** Built-in ones (`drawing::template::compiled`) are made from the paper: `asme-a` to `asme-e` (10 mm margins on A and B, 12.7 mm on C to E; zones about 2 in on B and larger, numbered from the right, lettered from the bottom; a 177.8 mm title block of TITLE, DRAWN, DATE, MATERIAL, SIZE, DWG NO, REV, SCALE, STANDARD, SHEET) and `iso-a4` to `iso-a0` (ISO 5457's 20 mm filing margin and 10 mm elsewhere, zones about 50 mm but on A4; an ISO 7200 block 180 mm wide). A project adds its own at `templates/<id>.json` (kind `ryucad.drawing_template`, `doc::drawing_template`): `area`, `keep_out`, thick `lines`, `thin` lines and `texts` with `{field}`s. `templates/` is a root folder: sessions track it, Git revisions read it, and `.gitattributes` gains `templates/*.json merge=ryucad diff=ryucad text eol=lf` (the `diff` and `scale` goldens moved). A field reads the part's PDM properties, then `name` (the drawing's), `number` (else the part ID), `revision` (the latest official release's label, `*` while it is modified), `date` (that release's official commit's day; empty in work, no clock), `scale`, `sheet`, `sheets`, `size`, `standard`, and the drawing's `fields` last. A name with no value is empty.
- **The project's standard.** `ryucad.json`'s `drawing_standard` (`asme` written as nothing: ASME unless it says ISO, question 337), manifest format 4 only when written; `config drawing.standard`.
- **Releases.** ryupdm's content hash gains a seventh piece: when the part has drawings, `ryucad.drawings`, each drawing's ID and bytes in ID order, then each project template they name (`templates`, its ID, its bytes or `missing`). Parts without drawings hash as before; editing a released part's drawing makes it `modified` (question 336).
- **Git.** Textconv gives each sheet and view a line (`views.v2 = projected from v1, top, offset 70 mm, sheet s1`). The merge driver merges per member and field; a view whose `type` changes on either side, or one both sides added differently, is one value, as are a view's `orient` and `rev`; two sides moving one view is one conflict at its `at`. `diff` gives a part whose drawings differ a `drawings` block by pointer, and the project's `templates`; `diff --model` adds `drawings.model` by drawing: the views whose built drawing (place, box and items, `ViewBuilt::fingerprint`) differs, `added`, `removed`, `changed`. An `id_collision`'s renumbering rewrites theirs' references in drawings (only references). Doctor reports a drawing or template that doesn't read as `invalid_document`, one not canonical `non_canonical`, anything else in `drawings/` or `templates/` `unknown_file`.
- **Building** (`ryucad::drawing::build`). Each view's frame: a base view's from its `orient` (`view::StandardView::axes`, a named view's eye and target, or `server::normal_to_face`); a projected one's a quarter turn of its parent's (top looks along the parent's up with the parent's towards as down; a diagonal by the cube's direction in the parent's frame); an auxiliary one's along the face's normal, refused `invalid_param` with `reason: foreshortened` unless its parent sees the face edge-on. Every view of a family keeps the middle of its source's box at its centre's place, so projected views line up with their parent exactly. Sources are the model's occurrences (`Session::model`) at `working` or a label. Projections come from the session (`Session::projections`, at most 256), the disk cache (kind `projections`, kept on this machine only: the shared cache's protocol names the other three) or `ryukei::project::view_with` side by side under the request's cancel (phase `project`, unit `views`); a cancel inside the kernel keeps nothing of that view.
- **Pieces to items** (`drawing::curves`). Visible pieces are thick continuous lines, hidden ones thin dashes only where the view asks, smooth edges by `tangent`, uncertain ones visible in `drawing::WARNING` with a `drawing_view_uncertain` finding. A piece is drawn only where it is the nearest of those it overlaps, and a source's consecutive pieces of one kind are joined (lines running on along a line made one). Images stay exact: lines, arcs (`Arc` by the frame's turn and sense), ellipses, and a NURBS as its Bézier pieces (interior knots raised to the degree).
- **Layout.** A sheet with no scale takes the largest of `SCALES` (10:1 to 1:1000) at which every view's box, grown by `DIM_ROOM` (12 mm), lies in the template's area clear of its keep-out boxes and each other (`FIT_SLACK`, a micrometre, for places written to a thousandth). A base view with no `at` goes with its family (the views placed along it) at the area's upper left, the next family to its right; projected views go along their row or column (third angle: top above, right to the right; first angle mirrored) just clear of their parent, each grown by its room; an isometric with no `at` at the upper right. Every place ryucad chose, and a scale it chose, is written back by the next write (`drawing.create`, `edit`, `layout`), so the file is what is drawn. A view the kernel refuses is `drawing_view_failed` (`reason`, `kernel`): its box crossed out, the sheet still built.
- **Requests and CLI.** `drawing.list`, `create` (`--views standard`: a base front, its top and right and an isometric), `edit` (`ops`: `add` a sheet or view, `remove`, `set` fields, `move` a view's `at` or `offset`, all or nothing, built in a proposed session first so what a build refuses writes nothing; CLI shortcuts `drawing view add`, `drawing move`, `drawing remove`), `delete`, `rename` (a new ID), `layout` (every place and scale chosen anew; `--dry-run`): each a logged, undoable change guarded by the part's revision and proposable (`annotation::propose::PROPOSABLE`). `get` (`schema: 1`, `--items` every item's exact geometry), `describe` (each sheet in words), `render` (PNG at 4 px/mm by default, or SVG; the bytes after the answer) and `export` (PDF of every sheet, a page each with bookmarks by sheet and view, or one sheet's SVG; the daemon writes `out` as `export` does, never over a project file) take `rev`. Long requests: `get`, `describe`, `render`, `export`, `layout`. New codes: `drawing_not_found`, `drawing_view_failed`, `drawing_view_uncertain`, `drawing_reference_unresolved`, `drawing_reference_not_shown`, `template_not_found`.
- **Writers.** `drawing::output`: SVG on white (`ryu_draw::svg`), PNG by resvg over it with the bundled Inter only (ryucad gains `resvg`, question 338), PDF through `ryu_draw::pdf`. The same drawing gives the same bytes on every platform and thread count.
- **Check.** `check` builds every drawing of the parts it checks (`source: drawing`): a view that fails is an error, uncertain pieces a warning; a drawing that doesn't read is doctor's.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`ryu-draw`'s writers and styles, `doc::drawing`'s and `doc::drawing_template`'s round trips and refusals, templates fitting their paper, curves keeping their ends and kinds, a block's three views and isometric against its edges' lengths, hidden bores dashed only when asked, an auxiliary view's true size and its foreshortened refusal, first angle's placement on an ISO sheet, doctor, merge, renumbering, the content hash, `diff --model`); `tests/cli.rs`'s `drawings_from_the_cli` both ways; the `drawings` golden section (the showcase's standard views and an ISO drawing: `drawing.get` with items, PDF, SVG and PNG, one thread and four).
- **Limits.** Sections, details, flat and exploded views and DXF are 9.B's; dimensions, annotation and the lint 9.C's; the app, corpus drawings, property test, fuzzing and bars 9.D's. Layout fills from the upper left and never moves a view the file places.
