## Geometric references and datums (decided in ryucad phase 13)

The design is document 297 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 13.A (phases 1 to 4).

- **References.** `doc::geometry::GeomRef` names anything that stands for a plane, an axis, a point or a curve: in files `"xy"`/`"yz"`/`"zx"`, `"x"`/`"y"`/`"z"`, `"origin"`, `{feature, name}` (a solid's face, edge or vertex, byte for byte as references are written), `{feature}` (a datum, or a sketch as its plane) and `{feature, entity}` (a sketch's point, line, arc or circle); in text the same words, `<part>/<founder>/<32 hex>`, `<part>/<feature>` and `<part>/<sketch>/<entity>`, and a bare feature ID taken in the part. The origin's words win, so a feature named `x` is `<part>/x`, and a bare word that isn't one (`direction w`, sketch plane `xz`) is a feature of the part, `not_found` when it has none. `Feature::geometry()` lists every geometric reference with its role, `Feature::references()` still the entity references alone, and `Feature::geometry_reads()` the features they read (what `graph::inputs` takes, so deleting or moving past what a datum reads is `has_dependents` or `reference_forward`). A feature is format 7 when it is a datum or holds a new form (`{feature}`, `{feature, entity}`, a plane as a pattern's direction, a word as an up-to target); every other file keeps its format.
- **The resolver.** `ryucad::geometry::resolve(at, role, ref, wants)` gives the first of the kinds a field wants (`Want::Plane`, `Axis`, `Point`, `Curve`) in the reading feature's view, by design 297's table; anything else is `reference_wrong_kind` with `wanted`, `got` (and `kind` for an entity), and what doesn't resolve fails as references do. A plane is its point and unit normal as the source gives them (a face's surface origin and outward normal) and the frame a sketch on it sees (a face sketch's rule); an axis (`geometry::Line`) its point, its unit direction as the source has it (a circle's or a surface's axis bit for bit) and a second point (a straight edge's or sketch line's end, else one unit along). Each use computes from those exactly what it computed from the entity before, so no existing result moved. `geometry::locate` finds a datum a request names, of a part (`<part>/<datum>`) or of an occurrence (`<asm>/<instance>/…/<datum>`, placed by its pose): a step names an instance while the part before it is an assembly and a feature once it is a part.
- **Datums.** Feature types `plane`, `axis` and `point` (`doc::datum`), each `by` a construction with its fields (design 297's tables); quantities are expressions, `flip` turns a plane's normal or an axis's direction. They found no solid, act on none, are built as `Output::Datum` (`ryucad::datum`: the geometry and a hash of its bits), kept in the part's cache and never on disk. Offset and parallel planes carry their base's frame origin and `x` across, angle planes turn them about their axis (the base brought onto the axis first), the rest take a face sketch's rule; `origin` and `x` replace it, projected. Refusals at rebuild: `datum_degenerate`, `datum_no_intersection`, `datum_ambiguous` (`count`), and `invalid_param` for a distance past a curve's length (`length_m`) or a fraction outside `[0, 1]` (that one when read). A point along a curve is exact on lines and circles and bisected on the parameter to resolution against `measure::curve_length` elsewhere; a curve and a plane meet through `intersect::curve_surface`, exactly once.
- **Uses.** A sketch's `plane` takes `{feature}` (`Plane::Datum`: a datum plane or another sketch, its frame as it has it); a face sketch's `origin` and `x` take any point and axis; `projected` takes any point for a point entity (a datum point, a sketch point, the origin), curves still edges. An extrude's and a hole's up-to target any plane or point; a revolve's object `axis` any axis in the sketch's plane (a bare ID stays a line of its sketch, so an origin axis goes through a datum axis `by of`). A pattern's `direction`, `direction2`, `axis` and a mirror's `plane` any geometric reference of their kind (a direction may be a plane's normal). A connector's `at` takes `{feature}`, a datum of the part (`<asm>/<path>/<datum>` in text): a plane's frame, an axis with z along it from the part origin's foot, x by the part's rule, a point with the part's axes; an occurrence carries its part's datums, hashed into its model only when there are any. A sketch's plane reaches a connector through a datum plane offset 0 from it (so no model hash moved for parts with sketches). Sections and `camera --normal-to` take datum planes (and axes, for the camera) of a part or an occurrence; a part's datums are leaves of the hidden set.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `by`, `from`, `about`, `through`, `curve`, `at`, `between` (a midplane's and a planes axis's planes, and an intersection's `of`), `coordinates` (a point's file `at`), `in`, `fraction`, `onto`, `point` and `points`, `origin`, `x`, `flip` with the existing `distance`, `angle`, `axis` and `plane`; references as text. A new `by` keeps only the fields it takes. The CLI's `--origin` and `--x` are a placement's with commas and a datum plane's frame references without. Rebuild reports give each datum `{kind, origin_m, normal | direction, x}`; `inspect` of a datum gives it, of the model lists them (`datums`, only when there are any); `feature.show` gives references as text.
- **Renders and measure.** `render` draws the shown part's datums (`datums`, CLI `--no-datums`): a plane a translucent square in `render::DATUM` as wide as the part's box diagonal projected onto it (10 mm at least), an axis a dashed line across the box, a point a cross, each labelled with its reference; a highlight paints one (`Target::Datum` resolves `<part>/<datum>`). `measure` of one datum gives its geometry; of two references where either is a datum, the distance and angle in closed form (points, lines and planes: a datum, a vertex, a straight edge, a planar face); anything else against a datum is `invalid_param` with `reason: datum_against_body`.
- **Testing.** `doc::geometry` and `doc::datum` round trips and refusals; `geometry::tests` the table row by row; `datum::tests` every construction and refusal; `mate::tests::a_connector_at_a_datum_takes_its_frame`; the server's `datums_are_cut_along_looked_at_hidden_and_inspected`, `datums_are_measured_in_closed_form` and `renders_draw_datums_unless_told_not_to`; `tests/datums.rs` (sketches on offset, angled and three-point planes, a revolve about a datum axis, a mirror in a midplane, a bolt circle about a two-point axis, up to a datum plane, a datum following its face renaming nothing, a mate to a datum, refusals); `tests/recompute.rs` with datums among its edits; `tests/cli.rs`'s `datums_from_the_cli` both ways; the `datums` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt`; the `feature` fuzz target's datum seeds and the `merge` target's `0xf7` mode.

## Paths and helices (decided in ryucad phase 13)

The design is document 297 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 13.B (phases 5 and 6).

- **Files.** Feature types `path` and `helix` (`doc::path::PathFeature`, feature format 7). A path's `segments` are, in order, an edge `{feature, name}` (with `propagate: true` beside it for its tangent chain), a sketch's line, arc or circle `{feature, entity}`, or a path or helix `{feature}`; words are `invalid_document`, as is `propagate` on anything but an edge; `reverse: true` turns the whole. A helix has `axis` (any axis), `radius` or `start` (a point), exactly two of `pitch`, `turns` and `height` (lengths and a number, more than nothing), `hand: left` (right is written as nothing), `taper` (an angle under 90°, none written as nothing); at most 10,000 turns (`MAX_TURNS`). A size, radius or taper out of range is `helix_invalid` with the field's `pointer`. Every quantity is an expression an equations table lists.
- **Building** (`ryucad::path`). Each listed segment is a run of pieces: an edge (with `propagate`, `blend::tangent_chain`'s edges in order, each turned to meet the next), a sketch curve as the resolver gives it, or a path's or helix's pieces as built. Runs are turned to meet: the first so it ends where the second starts or ends, each other so it starts where the one before ends (within `LINEAR_RESOLUTION`); then `reverse`, then `sweep::Path::check`. Output is `Output::Path(Arc<path::Made>)`: pieces (key, curve, range, source, reversed, tolerance), `closed`, `length` (a certified `measure::Value`, the pieces' `curve_length` at 1e-9 summed), `tolerance` (the largest edge tolerance, else resolution), a helix's numbers, and a hash of every bit. Kept in the part's cache, never on disk.
- **Keys.** An edge's segment key is SipHash of `ryucad.path\0<part>\0edge\0<name>`, a sketch curve's its sketch's (`profile::entity_key`), a helix's `ryucad.helix\0<part>\0<helix>`; a path's pieces keep theirs, so editing what a path follows renames nothing (`path::edge_key`, `helix_key`, `Made::kernel` for phase 14's sweeps).
- **Refusals.** `path_disconnected` (`segments`, `pieces`, `gap_m`), `path_not_tangent` (`segments`, `pieces`, `angle_rad`), `path_segment_twice` (`segments`, `piece`: one key twice), `path_branches` (`segment`, `edges`: more than one edge going on tangentially at an open end of a propagated chain, within 1e-6 rad, where the kernel's chain stops), `path_invalid` (the kernel's `BadPathSegment`, or a length that can't be certified), `helix_invalid` (a start on the axis, a taper narrowing the radius to nothing, a form ryukei can't make). Writes check that a path's segments are curves (an edge, a sketch's curve, a path or helix: `reference_wrong_kind` for a face, a sketch, a sketch's point, a datum) and a helix's axis and start their kinds; meeting is rebuild's.
- **Helices.** The axis's point is the base and the radius's start is at the default rule's `x` (the part's x across the axis, else its y); a start point gives the radius (its distance from the axis), the base (its foot) and the starting angle. The NURBS is `geom::Helix::to_nurbs` at `HELIX_TOLERANCE` (1e-7 m), its bound `deviation_m`.
- **As geometry.** A path or helix resolves as a curve (`geometry::Chain`, which every curve now is: one piece for an edge or a sketch curve); a helix also as its axis, but only where a field takes an axis and no curve, so an intersection meets the helix itself. Datums go `along` a chain (by length, across its pieces), `normal_to` it (at either end or a point's nearest foot on any piece) and meet it (each piece's crossings, one at a joint).
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `segments` (each text or `{curve, propagate}`, replacing them all), `reverse`, `axis`, `radius`, `start` (they replace each other), `pitch`, `turns`, `height` (given ones kept, the others as they were in that order until two; three is `invalid_param`), `hand`, `taper` (`""` for none). CLI `--segment` repeated with `--propagate` right after the segment it grows (read from the command line as written; before every segment is `usage`), `--reverse`, `--start`, `--pitch`, `--turns`, `--height`, `--hand`, `--taper`, with `--axis` and `--radius`. Rebuild reports and `inspect --feature` give `path` or `helix`: `{segments: [{from, reversed, start_m, end_m}], closed, length_m, length_bound_m?, tolerance_m, helix?: {axis, radius_m, pitch_m, turns, height_m, hand, taper_deg, deviation_m}}`; `inspect` of a part lists them as `paths` (only when there are any); `feature.show` gives references as text.
- **Uses.** Paths and helices are leaves of the hidden set, resolve as `Target::Datum("path" | "helix")` for highlights and selections, and draw in renders with datums (`render.datums`): a polyline in `render::PATH` cut to normal display quality's chord and angle, labelled at its start, a highlight's colour wider. `measure` of one gives its length (certified), whether it closes, its segments, ends and a helix's numbers; with another reference it is `invalid_param` with `reason: path_against_entity`.
- **Testing.** `doc::path` round trips and refusals; `path::tests` (sketch curves turned to meet, datums along, normal to and through a path, a rounded rim's chain, helices, keys across edits, every refusal, a fork from Euler-built wire); the server's `paths_are_hidden_highlighted_inspected_and_measured` and `renders_draw_paths_and_helices_unless_told_not_to`; `tests/paths.rs` (a rim following its box's resize, sketch curves across two sketches, a helix's closed forms, refusals); `tests/recompute.rs` with a helix and a path along it among its edits (a point along the helix was tried and taken out: bisecting against a certified length on every rebuild ran past 45 minutes in a debug build); `tests/cli.rs`'s `paths_from_the_cli` both ways; the `paths` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt`; the `feature` fuzz target's path and helix seeds and the `merge` target's `0xf6` mode (in `fuzz.yml` since 13.B, with `merge` in its matrix).
