## Sheets and surface sweeps (decided in ryucad phase 18)

The design is document 388 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 18.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **Sheets.** A sheet is a body beside solids: founded by a feature, named by its founder, listed in rebuild reports' `solids` (the name stays) with `kind` (`solid` or `sheet`, from `ryukei`'s `Body::kind`, never stored), and for a sheet `boundaries` (rings of laminar edges, `ryucad::kind::boundaries`) and `closed`. A feature's multi-body entries say the same. `inspect` gives each body `kind` and each edge `laminar: true` where it has one face; `diff --model` gives each side of a solid's change its `kind`. A result of solid regions and sheets together (`Mixed`) is refused in `rebuild::finish` (`wrong_body_kind`, `reason: mixed`).
- **`wrong_body_kind`** (new code, exit 1): `feature`, `role` (`targets`, `target`, `tools`, `solid`), `solid` (the founder), `wanted`, `got`. `rebuild::target_as` refuses a sheet wherever a feature acts on a body (every caller of `rebuild::target`, a boolean's target and tools, a shell's, offset's and split's target, a mirror's source), and `kind::check` raises the same at write from the current rebuild (`server::check_references`, which now rebuilds the part for any feature that reads bodies), so nothing is written. In 18.A every acting feature refuses sheets (question 254); 18.C lifts it feature by feature. Exports refuse a scene holding a sheet in every format, naming it (question 255).
- **Measure, mass, interference, check.** `measure` of a sheet gives its area (several bodies: their areas summed, `volume` only when one is a solid, and `sheets`). `mass` leaves sheets out with a `mass_sheet_skipped` warning each; asked of sheets alone it is `wrong_body_kind` (`role: of`). Interference pairs solids only; clearance keeps sheets. `check` judges a sheet as any body: no finding for a laminar edge.
- **Renders.** `render::Geometry::sheets`: a sheet's triangles drawn from both sides, the back lit by its flipped normal and tinted towards `render::SHEET_BACK` by `SHEET_TINT` (0.6), neither side ever a section's cap (`Facing::{Front, Cut, Back}` in `Band::fill`; solids draw as `Cut`, what `true` was). A translucent sheet draws its front only. No existing pixel moved.
- **Chains.** `ryusketch::Report::paths` (`ryusketch::Path`), parallel to `open`: each open chain walked end to end, or `Err` with the points where it branches; ryucad's `sketch::Report::paths` in the sketch's IDs (question 256). No ryusketch golden moved.
- **Surface profiles** (`profile::surface`): a sketch's closed loops (swept without caps) and open chains, every one or those holding a chosen entity. A chain runs end to end the way its least curve (natural order) is drawn and is keyed over that curve (`Keys::chains`), so extending it at either end keeps its key and renames only the old end's lateral and vertices; a branching chain is `profile_invalid` with `reason: branches` and `points`. `profile::of_face` takes `sheet: bool` for a face section's loops as a surface. `profile::origins` names chains as `ryukei::sweep::sheets` does: sides from their segment's stand-in, laterals and vertices from the pair either side (`chain_end` 0 or 1 at the ends, by `ChainEnd` stand-ins), rails as cap edges of the chain's own caps (`chain`, its least curve). `along::origins` adds the path segments; `loft::origins` names a chain's sides from every section's curve.
- **Features.** `surface: true` on `extrude`, `revolve`, `sweep` and `loft` (written only when true): it takes a surface profile and founds the kernel's sheet. Its operation is `new` only (`invalid_param` at `/operation`: joining sheets is sewing), and a surface extrude is blind or symmetric (`/extent/type`). `planar` (`doc::sweep::Planar`, `{profile}`: a `Section`) is a sheet of a profile's closed loops by `ryukei::sweep::planar`, named as an extrude's start cap; open curves are `profile_invalid` with `reason: open_profile`, refused when written. `Feature::surface()` says which found sheets. A loft's sections may name chains (a curve or a point on one); a section with no `loops` takes its one loop or chain; chains are matched by place, counted (`loop_mismatch`, `segment_count_mismatch`), and each later one runs from the end nearer where the section before's starts once their centroids are brought together, which the loft's report gives as `sections[i].chains: [{chain, entity|edge, reversed}]`. `feature_acts` now reads sweeps, lofts and planars from files that don't load.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `surface` (CLI `--surface [BOOL]`); `planar` takes `sketch` or `face` and `loops` (CLI `feature add <part> planar --sketch S [--loop E]`). `rebuild::sweep_error` names a chain along a revolve's axis (`ChainOnAxis`).
- **Format.** Feature format 12 (`doc::sweep::SURFACE_FORMAT`): a file is format 12 only when it is a `planar` or has `surface: true`; ryucad 16.A's sheet metal keeps 11. `FEATURE_FORMAT` is `SURFACE_FORMAT`; when 16.A lands, the larger constant.
- **Git.** Textconv gives a planar's profile one line as a sweep's (`profile = sketch s6 (loops l2)`), and `surface = true` its own; the merge driver takes a planar's profile as one value and `surface` per key; renumbering follows a planar's sketch and loops.
- **Testing.** `kind`, `render` (`a_sheet_shows_its_back_tinted_and_is_never_capped`), `profile` (keys, walks, branches, origins), `sketch`, `server` (reports and inspect, `feature.add` of surfaces and planars), `merge`, `renumber`, `diff` (textconv, `kind` in `--model`) unit tests; ryusketch's `a_chain_is_walked_end_to_end_and_a_branch_names_its_point`; ryucad-doc's surface and planar round trips and refusals; `tests/surfaces.rs` (an extruded arc, a dome, a chain swept along a bend and two lofted, a planar face, a face swept as a surface, against certified areas; names across edits; every acting feature refused at write and a hand-written cut at rebuild; measure, mass, export, interference, inspect); `tests/cli.rs`'s `surfaces_from_the_cli` both ways; `tests/recompute.rs` with a surface wall and a planar face among its edits; the `surfaces` golden section (bodies, and renders from in front, behind and cut); the `feature` fuzz target's surface seeds and the `merge` target's `0xf1` mode.
- **Limits.** A chain swept along a path, or lofted, pays for the kernel's self-intersection check. Sheets are written by no export format until 18.C.
