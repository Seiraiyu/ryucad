## Sheets and surface sweeps (decided in ryucad phase 18)

The design is document 388 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 18.A (phases 1 to 3).

- **Sheets.** A sheet is a body beside solids: founded by a feature, named by its founder, listed in rebuild reports' `solids` (the name stays) with `kind` (`solid` or `sheet`, from `ryukei`'s `Body::kind`, never stored), and for a sheet `boundaries` (rings of laminar edges, `ryucad::kind::boundaries`) and `closed`. A feature's multi-body entries say the same. `inspect` gives each body `kind` and each edge `laminar: true` where it has one face; `diff --model` gives each side of a solid's change its `kind`. A result of solid regions and sheets together (`Mixed`) is refused in `rebuild::finish` (`wrong_body_kind`, `reason: mixed`).
- **`wrong_body_kind`** (new code, exit 1): `feature`, `role` (`targets`, `target`, `tools`, `solid`), `solid` (the founder), `wanted`, `got`. `rebuild::target_as` refuses a sheet wherever a feature acts on a body (every caller of `rebuild::target`, a boolean's target and tools, a shell's, offset's and split's target, a mirror's source), and `kind::check` raises the same at write from the current rebuild (`server::check_references`, which now rebuilds the part for any feature that reads bodies), so nothing is written. In 18.A every acting feature refuses sheets (question 254); 18.C lifts it feature by feature. Exports refuse a scene holding a sheet in every format, naming it (question 255).
- **Measure, mass, interference, check.** `measure` of a sheet gives its area (several bodies: their areas summed, `volume` only when one is a solid, and `sheets`). `mass` leaves sheets out with a `mass_sheet_skipped` warning each; asked of sheets alone it is `wrong_body_kind` (`role: of`). Interference pairs solids only; clearance keeps sheets. `check` judges a sheet as any body: no finding for a laminar edge.
- **Renders.** `render::Geometry::sheets`: a sheet's triangles drawn from both sides, the back lit by its flipped normal and tinted towards `render::SHEET_BACK` by `SHEET_TINT` (0.6), neither side ever a section's cap (`Facing::{Front, Cut, Back}` in `Band::fill`; solids draw as `Cut`, what `true` was). A translucent sheet draws its front only. No existing pixel moved.
- **Chains.** `ryusketch::Report::paths` (`ryusketch::Path`), parallel to `open`: each open chain walked end to end, or `Err` with the points where it branches; ryucad's `sketch::Report::paths` in the sketch's IDs (question 256). No ryusketch golden moved.
- **Surface profiles** (`profile::surface`): a sketch's closed loops (swept without caps) and open chains, every one or those holding a chosen entity. A chain runs end to end the way its least curve (natural order) is drawn and is keyed over that curve (`Keys::chains`), so extending it at either end keeps its key and renames only the old end's lateral and vertices; a branching chain is `profile_invalid` with `reason: branches` and `points`. `profile::of_face` takes `sheet: bool` for a face section's loops as a surface. `profile::origins` names chains as `ryukei::sweep::sheets` does: sides from their segment's stand-in, laterals and vertices from the pair either side (`chain_end` 0 or 1 at the ends, by `ChainEnd` stand-ins), rails as cap edges of the chain's own caps (`chain`, its least curve). `along::origins` adds the path segments; `loft::origins` names a chain's sides from every section's curve.
- **Features.** `surface: true` on `extrude`, `revolve`, `sweep` and `loft` (written only when true): it takes a surface profile and founds the kernel's sheet. Its operation is `new` only (`invalid_param` at `/operation`: joining sheets is sewing), and a surface extrude is blind or symmetric (`/extent/type`). `planar` (`doc::sweep::Planar`, `{profile}`: a `Section`) is a sheet of a profile's closed loops by `ryukei::sweep::planar`, named as an extrude's start cap; open curves are `profile_invalid` with `reason: open_profile`, refused when written. `Feature::surface()` says which found sheets. A loft's sections may name chains (a curve or a point on one); a section with no `loops` takes its one loop or chain; chains are matched by place, counted (`loop_mismatch`, `segment_count_mismatch`), and each later one runs from the end nearer where the section before's starts once their centroids are brought together, which the loft's report gives as `sections[i].chains: [{chain, entity|edge, reversed}]`. `feature_acts` now reads sweeps, lofts and planars from files that don't load.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `surface` (CLI `--surface [BOOL]`); `planar` takes `sketch` or `face` and `loops` (CLI `feature add <part> planar --sketch S [--loop E]`). `rebuild::sweep_error` names a chain along a revolve's axis (`ChainOnAxis`).
- **Format.** Feature format 12 (`doc::sweep::SURFACE_FORMAT`): a file is format 12 only when it is a `planar` or has `surface: true`; ryucad 16.A's base flanges and flanges took 11 and merged first, so `FEATURE_FORMAT` is `SURFACE_FORMAT`, the larger.
- **Git.** Textconv gives a planar's profile one line as a sweep's (`profile = sketch s6 (loops l2)`), and `surface = true` its own; the merge driver takes a planar's profile as one value and `surface` per key; renumbering follows a planar's sketch and loops.
- **Testing.** `kind`, `render` (`a_sheet_shows_its_back_tinted_and_is_never_capped`), `profile` (keys, walks, branches, origins), `sketch`, `server` (reports and inspect, `feature.add` of surfaces and planars), `merge`, `renumber`, `diff` (textconv, `kind` in `--model`) unit tests; ryusketch's `a_chain_is_walked_end_to_end_and_a_branch_names_its_point`; ryucad-doc's surface and planar round trips and refusals; `tests/surfaces.rs` (an extruded arc, a dome, a chain swept along a bend and two lofted, a planar face, a face swept as a surface, against certified areas; names across edits; every acting feature refused at write and a hand-written cut at rebuild; measure, mass, export, interference, inspect); `tests/cli.rs`'s `surfaces_from_the_cli` both ways; `tests/recompute.rs` with a surface wall and a planar face among its edits; the `surfaces` golden section (bodies, and renders from in front, behind and cut); the `feature` fuzz target's surface seeds and the `merge` target's `0xef` mode (`0xf1` and `0xf0` are ryucad 19.A's and 16.A's).
- **Limits.** A chain swept along a path, or lofted, pays for the kernel's self-intersection check. Sheets are written by no export format until 18.C.

## Fills, extracts, sews and thickens (decided in ryucad phase 18)

The design is document 388 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 18.B (phases 4 to 6).

- **Files.** Feature format 15 (`doc::surface::FORMAT`; `FEATURE_FORMAT` is the largest of every stage's constant): a file is format 15 only when it is a `fill`, `extract`, `sew` or `thicken`, so no other file moved. A fill's `sides` (each `{edge: {feature, name}, tangent?}` or `{curve: <geometric reference>}`, `tangent` written only when true) with `tolerance` (0.001 mm) and `angle` (1 deg, in `(0, 90 deg)`) always written; an extract's `faces`; a sew's `sheets` (two or more founders), `tolerance` (0.01 mm) and `solid` (true) always written; a thicken's `from` (`{sheet}` or `{faces}`), `front` and `back` (zero or more, not both zero; `back` 0 when left out), `faces` groups `{front, back, faces}` (each face among `from`'s, or of its sheet, once), and `operation` always written, `targets` with a join, cut or intersect. Tolerances are from `LINEAR_RESOLUTION` to the cap (`TOLERANCE_RANGE`). Fills, extracts and sews refuse `operation` and `targets` (`invalid_param` at `/operation`). Expression fields: `tolerance`, `angle`, `front`, `back` and groups' `front` and `back`.
- **Fills** (`ryucad::surface::fill`). Each side resolves in the fill's view: an edge by stable name (`reference_wrong_kind` for anything else), a curve through the geometric resolver (a path's or helix's pieces each a kernel side, in order, ranges increasing), refused at write as `check_fill` finds them. One `fill::fill_with` under the feature's `OpKey` and the request's cancel. Origins: faces `{feature, fill: plane | patch}`, boundary edges `{feature, side, piece?}` (the kernel's `FillRole::Side` and `Half` names, derived again), the rest the feature's. The kernel's report (each side's `deviation_m`, a tangent side's `angle_deg`, the inner edges' `inner_deg`) is kept with the result in `Made::report` (renamed from `import`, the same bytes in the disk cache) and is the rebuild report's `fill`; `feature.show` and `inspect --feature` give the fields. `fill_failed`: `reason` (`too_few_sides`, `not_laminar` with a hint, `not_closed` with `gap_m`, `degenerate`, `angle_too_large` with `angle_deg`, `uncertain`), `kernel`, and the side by the file's index (`side`, `piece`, `source`).
- **Extracts** (`surface::extract`). Faces of one body as the extract sees it (`targets_mixed` for faces of two), copied by `sheet::extract` under the feature's key: every entity keeps its source's name, its origin `{feature, extract_of: <name>}`; the body is left as it was. `extract_failed`: `no_faces`, `non_manifold_vertex` (the vertex's reference and a hint).
- **Consumes.** `Feature::consumes()`: a boolean's tools, a sew's sheets, a thicken's sheet; `Feature::targets` lists them too (a sew's sheets, a thicken's targets then its sheet), and `Solids::of_order` ends their chains for any feature (`consumed_by`, `consumer`), files that don't load through `Acts::consumes`. `body_consumed` and references' and entities' messages name any consumer. Rebuild reports' `consumes` and `operation` come from the same (a new sew or thicken says `new`).
- **Bodies by kind.** `rebuild::body_as(part, solids, id, t, Need { role, want, changes }, before)`: a body refused where consumed, not yet founded, failed, a reference import's body where the feature changes it, or not of `want` (`None` for either); `target_as` is it wanting a solid that the feature changes. `kind::bodies_read` gives each body a feature names with its role and the kind it wants: a sew's sheets `Sheet` in `sheets`, a thicken's sheet `Sheet` in `from`, everything else `Solid`. `Feature::may_make_sheet` (a surface sweep, a planar face, a fill, an extract, a sew that isn't solid) is what makes `server::check_references` rebuild to judge kinds.
- **Sews** (`surface::sew`). Each sheet as the sew sees it, one `heal::sew_with` (new in ryukei: `sew` under `Work`, `sew` unchanged) under the feature's key and the cancel. More than one piece is `sew_failed` `pieces` (each piece's sheets and laminar edges, a hint); `solid: true` with a body that doesn't close is `sew_failed` `open` (its laminar edges as `<part>/<sheet>/<name>`); a kernel refusal `sew_failed` with the healing refusal's variant in snake case. Origins: a sewn edge or joined vertex `{feature, sewn: [<names>]}`, a split copy its parent's, the rest the sheets' own.
- **Thickens** (`surface::thicken`). From a sheet (consumed, wanted a sheet) or from faces of one body (extracted under `ryucad.thicken.extract\0<part>\0<feature>`, the body left alone), one `offset::thicken_with` under the feature's key: its own body, through `rebuild::build_body`, so `new` founds a solid and `join`, `cut` and `intersect` are an extrude's, with its tool cache (`tool_hash` reads the consumed sheet). Origins: an entity a moved side took is `{feature, front_of | back_of: <name>}` (each sheet entity's `ThickenRole::Front` and `Back` names derived directly, since a side at zero journals nothing), a wall `{feature, wall_of: [<edges>]}`, an entity a side at zero kept its own. A side at zero keeps the sheet's names, so taking a side from zero to a thickness renames that side's entities; a new thickness of a side already thick renames nothing. `thicken_failed` through `body::kernel_error`, as `offset_failed`. A thicken is never a pattern's seed.
- **Requests and CLI.** A fill takes `sides` whole (else `edges` as edge sides, or one `curve`), `tolerance`, `angle`; the CLI reads `--edge`, `--curve` and `--tangent` in the order written (`cli::fill_sides`; `--tangent` goes right after its `--edge`, else `usage`), a lone `--curve` still a datum's. An extract takes `faces` (or `face`/`face_list`: `--face` is repeatable, one is `face`, two or more `face_list`). A sew takes `sheets` (`--sheet` repeated), `tolerance`, and `make_solid` (`--no-solid`, `--make-solid`; the file's key is `solid`, which a mirror's request field already is). A thicken takes `sheet` (or one `--sheet`) or `from_faces` (or `--face`), `front`, `back`, `face_sides` whole (`--face-sides REF=FRONT,BACK` repeated, equal pairs one group; `--no-face-sides`), `operation`, `targets`. Groups naming a face the thicken doesn't take are `invalid_param` when written.
- **Git.** Textconv gives a fill's sides a line each (`side 1 = wall/<hex> (tangent)`), a thicken's source one line (`from = sheet skin`, `from = faces base/<hex>`) and each group one (`faces[0] = 3 mm, 0 mm: skin/<hex>`). The merge driver merges a thicken per key with `from` one value (a conflict at `/from`, never inside it); a fill's sides, a sew's sheets and an extract's faces are arrays, one value each. Renumbering follows a sew's `/sheets` and a thicken's `/from/sheet`; references inside (`from.faces`, edge sides) follow as all references do.
- **Fixed on the way.** `sketch.edit` of a planar face's sketch reached an `unreachable!` (18.A put planars in `sweeps_of` but not in the check of the loops they name).
- **Testing.** Unit tests (`doc::surface`, `files`, `kind`, `rebuild` (`body_as`, an extract beside its box, a box's faces sewn back or refused, a face thickened and joined, a sheet thickened and consumed), `solid` (consumers, a sew that doesn't load), `surface` (fill origins, extract origins, variant names), `server` (fills and extracts, sews and thickens refused at write), `cli` (`fill_sides`, a thicken's and sew's flags), `diff`, `merge`, `renumber`, `validate` (`merge_broke` through a thickened sheet)); ryukei's `a_sew_stops_at_a_cancel_and_otherwise_sews_a_block_back`; `tests/fills.rs` (a pentagon's area, a quad tangent to a quarter cylinder sheet within its angle, `not_laminar` and `not_closed`, a box's top and all its faces extracted); `tests/sew_thicken.rs` (a box sewn back to its volume and as a closed sheet, a gap as pieces, a square thickened forward and back against closed forms, a box's top thickened and joined); `tests/recompute.rs` with a fill and a thicken among its edits; `tests/cli.rs`'s `fills_from_the_cli` and `sew_and_thicken_from_the_cli` both ways; the `fills`, `sews` and `thickens` golden sections; the `feature` fuzz target's six surface seeds and the `merge` target's `0xed` mode (27: a thicken or a fill, nothing conflicted inside `from` or `sides`).
- **Limits.** The kernel's: a tangent side only on a laminar edge, G1 only; a thicken's boundary vertices of three faces or more (`LaminarVertex`); a sew's several pieces are refused, never bodies of their own. No feature but a sew and a thicken acts on a sheet until 18.C.
