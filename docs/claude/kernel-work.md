## Bounded work (decided in ryukei phase 15)

The design is document 296 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 1, plan 15.A.

- **API.** `ryukei::work::Work` is an optional cancel flag (`with_cancel(&AtomicBool)`) and an optional budget in work units (`with_budget`); `Work::unlimited()` is the plain entry points'. The long entry points have `_with` variants taking `&Work`: `boolean_with`, `blend_with`, `heal_with`, `offset_body_with`, `shell_with`, `sweep::along_with`, `sweep::loft_with`, `check_intersections_with`, `tessellate_with`, `measure::mass_with` and `distance_with`. Refusals are `Error::Cancelled` and `Error::BudgetExceeded { spent, budget }`, and nothing is built. `Work::spent` adds up what the calls given it spent.
- **Units and meters.** A unit is one charge at an inner loop: an enclosure, cell or cell pair visited, a search box, a step of a march, a span certified, a sample whose foot is found, a triangle tested in refinement or integrated, a bisection step of an edge, a candidate pair a heal compares. Leaf calls their own caps bound (`project`, `point_in_face`, root isolation) are charged one unit by their caller. Each sequential task carries a `Meter`, passed explicitly as `&Meter` (never a thread-local), and parallel loops go through `Meter::par_map`, each task's limit the whole budget, their counts added in order. A halt is sticky and travels as `Failure::Uncertain`; `work::run` replaces whatever the call returned with `BudgetExceeded` when its count passed the budget, else `Cancelled`. So a budget is exceeded, and `spent` reported, alike on every platform and thread count (the `work` golden section); a cancel is checked at every charge and isn't deterministic. `Meter::poll` checks a cancel without charging, for debug builds' input checks, so units are the same in every build. Never charge inside a lazily built shared value. Every `Meter::free()` says why (`// unmetered:`), which `tests/work.rs` checks. Not metered: convexity in blends and edge classes in offsets (fixed piece counts), the boolean's build and merge (linear in what they build, after every certificate); the distance's tree walk is charged per piece, not per node pair.
- **Caps.** `Meter::capped(Scope)` bounds one trace (its retries included), curve–surface search, fit or `beside`: past the scope's units, charges answer `Uncertain` for that scope. The caps are 16 times the largest scope seen across the release suites and the pinned STEP corpus, rounded up to a power of two, so no pinned result moved. On cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, 8 threads, shared) the largest were: trace 2,866,615 (`heal_props`), search 65,515 (`general_boolean_props`), fit 1,005,613 (`intersect_props`) and beside 191,434 (`spline_offsets`). That gives caps of `1 << 26`, `1 << 21`, `1 << 24` and `1 << 23`. Search and beside are one power above the rule: 16 times search's largest came within 0.03% of `1 << 20`, and `spline_offsets` ran out of time with two tests unfinished. 14.A's tolerant paths stay well under the caps (`tolerant_boolean_props`: trace 577, search 2,083, fit 333,010). The `work-stats` feature records each scope's largest (`RYUKEI_WORK_STATS` names a file to gather them in, written a whole line at a time so processes don't interleave). #260's two sweep finds and the boolean whose trace's clusters ran away end under them with accepted outcomes: on cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, loaded at 14 to 19), the two sweep finds in 69.1 and 167.8 s and the boolean in 275.6 s (`fuzz_regressions.rs`'s `runaway_finds_end_under_the_caps`, release only). Their seconds are printed and held only under the sweep target's 600 s timeout, since a unit's cost varies by machine and load. `blend::meet` still sends a pipe on an analytic anchor against a NURBS to `pipe_surface`; 15.D routes it to `surface_surface`, under the Trace cap. Plan 15.C weighted charges (see Weighted charges below) and calibrated the caps again by the same rule on cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, 2026-11-04): largest trace 2,866,615 (`heal_props`), search 467,324 (`general_boolean_props`), fit 1,467,251 (`intersect_props`) and beside 577,683 (`spline_offsets`, whose offsets' enclosures now cost 8), so caps of `1 << 26`, `1 << 23`, `1 << 25` and `1 << 24`; the pinned corpus stays far under them (trace 17,603, search 9,184, fit 48,181, beside 32,768).
- **Fuzzing.** The blend, sweep and offset targets run each case through `ryukei_testkit::fuzz_case`. The calls a case makes (the operation, its self-intersection check and its mesh) share one `FUZZ_BUDGET`, each call getting what the earlier ones left, and a watchdog cancels them at `FUZZ_DEADLINE` (450 s). `BudgetExceeded` and `Cancelled` are typed refusals, so a runaway case ends inside the targets' timeouts (600 s for sweep and offset, which `fuzz.yml` passes, and libFuzzer's 1,200 s for blend). The cancel isn't deterministic, but a fuzz case needn't be. `RYUKEI_FUZZ_UNITS=1` prints each case's units. The budget is `1 << 27`, 8 times the largest case measured since plan 15.C's weighted charges, with the budget and the deadline lifted, on cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, shared at a load of 6 to 15, 2026-11-04): sweep's 717 seeds at most 13,808,926 units, and 900 s from empty corpora blend's 1,984,559 and offset's 2,848,083 (15.A's: 6,228,952 on davids-m4, 213,336 and 3,925,012, under `1 << 26`). The deadline is needed because a unit's cost varies about 275 times between cases: without ASAN, 626,000 units a second on one seed and 2,270 on a lofted spline whose four-parameter trace encloses high-degree patches (a sweep surface's enclosures are as dear). Weighting charges by enclosure cost, so that budgets follow seconds, is #353: see Weighted charges below.
- **Weighted charges** (plan 15.C, #353). An enclosure is charged its sheet's cost (`Sheet::cost`): a NURBS `(p + 1)(q + 1) / 2 + 1` (a bicubic 9), a sweep, an offset or a bent surface 8, anything else 1, in the four-parameter tracer and `apart`, `sheet_trace`, the sheet curve–surface search, `beside`'s field and the fit's sheet spans. Units a second of `check_intersections` on one thread (cox-worker-2, Ryzen 7 9700X, shared, load 6 to 10), unweighted to weighted: #260's first sweep find 103,000 to about 1,000,000; ftc_07 436,000 to about 650,000; analytic bodies (ctc_04, ftc_06) 1,240,000 to 3,100,000 either way; a drilled block against its NURBS twin 4.2 times to 1.7. ctc_05 (260,000 to 320,000) spends its time in leaves the kernel charges once by rule (`point_in_face`'s edge projections, `intersect2`'s systems). `tests/work.rs`' `units_follow_time_across_surfaces` holds the block, its twin and the sweep within 8 times (unweighted the sweep is 8.7). The work golden didn't move: its cases enclose no NURBS or sweep. Each operation also shares one table of NURBS enclosures (`Meter::enclosures`; see The check's speed in `kernel-general-intersections.md`).
- **Testing.** `work.rs`'s unit tests (charges, the per-task rule, sums on 1, 2 and 8 threads, caps, cancels), each intersector's halting test, `tests/work.rs` (every entry point: charges, the same on one thread and eight, a budget of what it spends enough, one unit short `BudgetExceeded` alike on both, a cancel `Cancelled`; and the rule on free meters), the `work` golden section.

## Cancels inside unmetered searches (decided in release 8, 0.8.1)

- **Polled, not charged** (#485). `geom::intersect2::crossings_in` takes the caller's meter and polls its cancel (`Meter::poll`) before each pair of pieces, each cell of a pair's subdivision and each cell grouped into clusters, charging nothing, so work units, budgets and golden hashes don't move; `crossings` is it with a free meter. Coincident faces' rims (`intersect::face::coincident`, which healing's overlap check and booleans reach) take it. NIST CTC 05's solid spent about 20 of its 23 s of healing there, on one pair of coincident spline faces, and a cancel waited for it all: a daemon cancelling a cold import's rebuild stayed busy about 28 s. Each piece's hull is now found once rather than for every pair, which halves that heal (22.8 to 10.4 s), with the same bits. On cox-worker-3 (Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus, three rayon threads, load about 2) the worst of 38 cancels stopped 0.13 s later, against 20 s before. `ryustep`'s `tests/cancel.rs` (release only) holds it within 2 s; `cargo run --release -p ryustep --example cancel-probe -- <file>` cancels each body at moments through its heal and times the stop.
- **Left.** `boolean::table`'s `crossings_of` (coincident edges in booleans) still calls `crossings` unmetered: it takes no meter yet.
