## Bounded work (decided in ryukei phase 15)

The design is document 296 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 1, plan 15.A.

- **API.** `ryukei::work::Work` is an optional cancel flag (`with_cancel(&AtomicBool)`) and an optional budget in work units (`with_budget`); `Work::unlimited()` is the plain entry points'. The long entry points have `_with` variants taking `&Work`: `boolean_with`, `blend_with`, `heal_with`, `offset_body_with`, `shell_with`, `sweep::along_with`, `sweep::loft_with`, `check_intersections_with`, `tessellate_with`, `measure::mass_with` and `distance_with`. Refusals are `Error::Cancelled` and `Error::BudgetExceeded { spent, budget }`, and nothing is built. `Work::spent` adds up what the calls given it spent.
- **Units and meters.** A unit is one charge at an inner loop: an enclosure, cell or cell pair visited, a search box, a step of a march, a span certified, a sample whose foot is found, a triangle tested in refinement or integrated, a bisection step of an edge, a candidate pair a heal compares. Leaf calls their own caps bound (`project`, `point_in_face`, root isolation) are charged one unit by their caller. Each sequential task carries a `Meter`, passed explicitly as `&Meter` (never a thread-local), and parallel loops go through `Meter::par_map`, each task's limit the whole budget, their counts added in order. A halt is sticky and travels as `Failure::Uncertain`; `work::run` replaces whatever the call returned with `BudgetExceeded` when its count passed the budget, else `Cancelled`. So a budget is exceeded, and `spent` reported, alike on every platform and thread count (the `work` golden section); a cancel is checked at every charge and isn't deterministic. `Meter::poll` checks a cancel without charging, for debug builds' input checks, so units are the same in every build. Never charge inside a lazily built shared value. Every `Meter::free()` says why (`// unmetered:`), which `tests/work.rs` checks. Not metered: convexity in blends and edge classes in offsets (fixed piece counts), the boolean's build and merge (linear in what they build, after every certificate); the distance's tree walk is charged per piece, not per node pair.
- **Caps.** `Meter::capped(Scope)` bounds one trace (its retries included), curve–surface search, fit or `beside`: past the scope's units, charges answer `Uncertain` for that scope. The caps are 16 times the largest scope seen across the release suites and the pinned STEP corpus, rounded up to a power of two, so no pinned result moved. On cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, 8 threads, shared) the largest were: trace 2,866,615 (`heal_props`), search 65,515 (`general_boolean_props`), fit 1,005,613 (`intersect_props`) and beside 191,434 (`spline_offsets`). That gives caps of `1 << 26`, `1 << 21`, `1 << 24` and `1 << 23`. Search and beside are one power above the rule: 16 times search's largest came within 0.03% of `1 << 20`, and `spline_offsets` ran out of time with two tests unfinished. 14.A's tolerant paths stay well under the caps (`tolerant_boolean_props`: trace 577, search 2,083, fit 333,010). The `work-stats` feature records each scope's largest (`RYUKEI_WORK_STATS` names a file to gather them in, written a whole line at a time so processes don't interleave). #260's two sweep finds and the boolean whose trace's clusters ran away end under them with accepted outcomes: on cox-worker-4 (AMD Opteron 6380, loaded at 14 to 19), the two sweep finds in 69.1 and 167.8 s and the boolean in 275.6 s (`fuzz_regressions.rs`'s `runaway_finds_end_under_the_caps`, release only). Their seconds are printed and held only under the sweep target's 600 s timeout, since a unit's cost varies by machine and load. `blend::meet` still sends a pipe on an analytic anchor against a NURBS to `pipe_surface`; 15.D routes it to `surface_surface`, under the Trace cap.
- **Fuzzing.** The blend, sweep and offset targets run each case under one `ryukei_testkit::FUZZ_BUDGET`, the operation, its self-intersection check and its mesh included, and take `BudgetExceeded` as a typed refusal; `RYUKEI_FUZZ_UNITS=1` prints each case's units. The budget is `1 << 26`, 8 times the largest case measured: sweep's seed corpus at most 6,228,952 units (davids-m4, an Apple M4), blend's 213,336 and offset's 3,925,012 from empty corpora on cox-worker-4. It bounds runaways in units, not seconds. A unit's cost varies about 275 times between cases: without ASAN, 626,000 units a second on one seed and 2,270 on a lofted spline whose four-parameter trace encloses high-degree patches. So some sweep seeds still pass the target's 600 s timeout (#353, weighting charges by enclosure cost, for a later plan).
- **Testing.** `work.rs`'s unit tests (charges, the per-task rule, sums on 1, 2 and 8 threads, caps, cancels), each intersector's halting test, `tests/work.rs` (every entry point: charges, the same on one thread and eight, a budget of what it spends enough, one unit short `BudgetExceeded` alike on both, a cancel `Cancelled`; and the rule on free meters), the `work` golden section.
