## Views and silhouettes (decided in phase 10)

The design is document 326 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 10.A (phases 1 and 2).

- **Views.** `project::View::new(towards, up)`: orthographic only (design 326, question 11); `towards` from the model to the viewer, `x = up × towards` right, `y = towards × x` up, depth along `towards`. A direction or `up` that isn't finite, has no length, or lies within `ANGULAR_RESOLUTION` of `±towards` is `Error::InvalidView(ViewError::BadView)`.
- **Projected curves.** `project::project_curve(curve, range, view)` gives `ImageCurve { image, range }` pieces, each one to one onto its image: a line's affine `Line2` (an end-on line a `Image::Point`), a conic's `Circle2`/`Ellipse2` with the identity map facing the view, an `Ellipse2` from its conjugate image axes (`ImageMap::Affine([−t₀, 1])`) obliquely, and edge-on (its plane holding the view, or its image's minor axis under resolution) a `Line2` with `ImageMap::Cosine { amplitude, phase }` cut at `phase + kπ`; a NURBS's projected control points with its own knots and weights, cut at its folds: roots of its hodograph's numerator along the image's principal axis, in Bernstein form, unless the other axis's is certified non-zero (a stretch running along the view is `Uncertain`).
- **Silhouettes.** `project::silhouettes(body, view)` and `silhouettes_with(…, &Work)`: per face, in parallel, gathered by face. Closed forms (`project::closed`): planes none (edge-on by rule), cylinders and cones their rulings, spheres a great circle (meridians or the equator along their axis; else a fitted pcurve), tori along their axis two circles. Every other surface (NURBS, extrusions, revolutions, pipes, sweeps, offsets, bent surfaces, tori askew) is traced by `intersect::sheet_trace::trace_contour`: the tracer's field has a `Form::Contour(towards)` beside `Form::Implicit`, `F = n · towards` with `n` from `Sheet::normal_jets_at`/`normal_jet_box` (`S_u × S_v` over third-order jets; a pipe's unit normal; a torus's closed form; an offset's base's). Branches are fitted by `intersect::contour::fit_contour`: a Hermite pcurve through the traced parameters (`Traced::params`, new), lifted by `fit_along`, and certified per piece against `F = 0` within `10 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION`: to second order by Taylor's formula on `G = F ∘ q` about each end of the piece (`G''` from the pcurve's hodographs' hulls and `F`'s Hessian over the hull), with the first-order box bound (a partial of one sign over the box grown by `ε/g`, or the box's extent where it holds a traced point) kept for what that can't settle. A face with no loops (a whole sphere or torus) is its surface's whole domain and isn't trimmed; any other branch is trimmed in its face's parameter plane (`intersect2::crossings` with every fin, `point_in_face` per piece); a stretch along an edge marks the edge (`Silhouettes::on_edges`). Faces certified edge-on are listed (`edge_on`). Stretches are numbered per face by their least point in its parameters. Any face that can't be certified refuses the call (`ViewError::Uncertain { at }`): no partial outlines.
- **Departures from the design** (each decided on plan 328; the PR repeats them). Pipes and NURBS go through the one tracer rather than `θ(s)` in closed form and `ss_trace`'s Bernstein patches (`ss_trace` solves a patch boundary from the boundary curve alone, which `F`, reading cross-derivatives, can't be); offsets are traced on themselves (their parameters and normal are their base's); a NURBS face with an interior crease is `Uncertain`; at a collapsed row the contour's zone reaches `2 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION`. The fit's bound is second order (Taylor about each piece's ends), not the plan's first-order box bound alone, which could only certify pieces about the resolution long (a torus fit ran 100 s a round and stalled at 5.9e-6).
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`project::{view, curve, folds, closed, edge_on, trim, silhouette}`, `geom::sheet`'s normal jets, `intersect::pipe`'s contour field, `sheet_trace`'s contours and parameters, `intersect::contour`), `tests/silhouettes.rs` (every surface kind; traced cases release only, their own `full.yml` step), `tests/work.rs`'s `silhouettes_are_bounded`, and the `silhouettes` golden section. No other golden hash moved.
- **Limits.** Silhouettes are curves on single bodies; hidden lines across placed bodies are plan 10.B's, sections and the OCCT race 10.C's.

## Hidden lines (decided in phase 10)

The design is document 326 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 10.B (phase 3).

- **API.** `project::view(&[Placed { body, place }], &View, &ViewOptions)` and `view_with(…, &Work)` give a `Projection`: `pieces` (by placed body; in each, edges by ID, then silhouettes by face and ordinal; along each, by parameter) and each body's `silhouettes` in the placed frame. A `Piece` has its `source` (`Edge { body, edge, name }` or `Silhouette { body, face, face_name, ordinal }`, `body` the index in the slice given), its exact `image` (an `ImageCurve`: the image and the 3D curve's stretch), an enclosure of its `depth`, its `visibility` (`Visible`, `Hidden(n)` behind `n` faces, `Uncertain`), the pieces it lies on (`overlaps`, nearest first), its `class` (`Sharp`, `Smooth`, `Boundary`, `Silhouette`, `Uncertain`) and `on_silhouette`. `ViewOptions::silhouettes` (true by default) turns tracing off. Refusals: `ViewError::Uncertain` (a face's silhouette), `ViewError::Image` (an edge's image, new), `Body::transformed`'s, `Cancelled`, `BudgetExceeded`.
- **Events.** Every pair of image pieces whose boxes overlap (`intersect::broad` over image boxes) is met by `intersect2::crossings_in`; crossings, touches and overlap ends cut both pieces (`ImageCurve::param_of` takes image parameters back to the 3D curve's). A pair it can't certify is halved each way up to three times; stretches still uncertain are cut at their ends and flagged. Cuts within four tolerances of each other are one.
- **Piercings.** Placed bodies may pass through each other (a press fit, which ryucad's `interference` takes to be meant), and a curve's visibility then changes where it pierces another body's face, which needn't lie on any image curve (the PR review's pin through a plate: its silhouette ruling crossed the plate's images only beside its faces). So each image piece's 3D curve is also met with every face of every other placed body whose model-space box its own meets (`project::pierce`: `curve_surface_in` under `Scope::Search`, each hit placed by `point_in_face`), and cut at every hit not certified outside the face (a cut that changes nothing costs nothing: each side is judged alone). A stretch lying along a face is cut at its ends and flagged, as is the whole piece where a search can't be certified. A body's own faces are never met: a valid body's faces meet only at its edges.
- **Rays.** Each piece is judged by rays towards the viewer from its middle, then a third, two thirds, a quarter and three quarters along: every face whose image box holds the point and reaches in front (a box tree in image coordinates and depth) is met by `curve_surface_in` under `Scope::Search`, each hit placed by `point_in_face`; crossings inside faces count. Hits within the touch reach are the piece's own place, and on a silhouette's own face within the tangent wedge `sqrt(2 × size × reach)`. A boundary hit, a tangent touch or a stretch along a face doesn't hide a piece that lies on others (a box's back edges seen face on are `Visible` and name the front edges they lie behind), and otherwise leaves the point unsure. A flagged piece needs two agreeing counts.
- **Folds.** An edge-on circle is cut at `phase + kπ`: a cylinder's rim seen side on, its frame's `x` the view's, is two halves, not three.
- **Classes.** From `blend::convex::bend`: convex, concave or changing is `Sharp`, tangent `Smooth`, a laminar edge `Boundary`, unsettled `Uncertain`.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`project::{curve, items, events, pieces, pierce, rays, hidden}`), `tests/hidden_lines.rs` (a block from 26 directions, a cylinder, a sphere, a drilled plate, a fillet's rails, placed blocks, a sheet in front, a pin through a plate against its rulings' closed-form lengths, one thread and eight), `tests/work.rs`' `views_are_bounded`, and the `hidden_lines` golden section. No other golden hash moved. `views_are_bounded` (a block and a ball, one view) spends 8,845 units (cox-worker-1, Intel Xeon E5-2650, debug build; units are the same on every machine).
- **Limits.** Sections, the OCCT comparison, the race against `HLRBRep_Algo`, the mesh-oracle property test, the fuzz target and the bars are plan 10.C's.

## Sections and references (decided in phase 10)

The design is document 326 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 10.C (phases 4 and 5).

- **Sections.** `project::section(key, &Body, &Plane)` and `section_with(…, &Work)` give `Section { body, journal, faces }`. The body (a solid or a sheet) loses what lies in front of the plane: a block standing on the plane along its frame's `z` (the side cut away, as ryucad's section planes point), a hundredth of the body's diagonal past its box every other way, subtracted through `boolean_in` under the call's meter.
  - Every certificate of the boolean holds, and refusals are its own (`Error::InvalidBoolean`).
  - `faces` are the cap a drawing hatches: the block's base followed through the journal (`Placed` under `SECTION_TOOL`, `Split`, `Merged`, to a fixed point), in `FaceId` order. A sheet's section has none.
  - A plane the body's box doesn't reach past by more than resolution gives the body as it is and an empty journal; a body wholly in front of it gives the empty body.
  - A section view is a `view` of the cut body from the side cut away.
- **A piece inside a circle** (ryucad plan 9.B). `project::inside_circle(image, centre, radius)` gives the stretches of an image piece's 3D curve parameter whose images lie inside a circle in the view's plane: cut at the certified crossings `geom::intersect2::crossings` gives (an overlap cut at both ends), each stretch kept by its middle; `Uncertain` where a crossing isn't certified. A drawing's detail view clips its parent's pieces with it. Unit tests in `project::clip`; no golden moved.
- **What the property test found** (fixed in `project`, each held by `tests/hidden_lines.rs`):
  - A sphere's askew silhouette circle cut by its face's loops into a stretch across the circle's start ran past the `[0, 2π]` its pcurve was fitted over. Such a stretch is fitted again over its own range (`silhouette::covering`), and trimming reads a wrapped middle a period back.
  - An edge's visibility changes where a silhouette of its face ends on it (race body 15: a torus's contour starting on a cone's rim), with no image crossing there. The edge is now cut at the silhouette's end, unless a cut or an end already lies within the touch rule's tangent wedge of it (a cylinder's rulings end where its rim already touches them, so `hidden_lines` didn't move).
  - A contour's image cusps where its tangent turns along the view (race body 18's torus, a sweep), and visibility changes there by one. A silhouette is now cut at local maxima of its tangent's turn towards the view past a squared cosine of 0.9975 (`CUSP_COSINE`). Cutting more than needed only splits a piece.
  - A section ball's cap rim, which `events` had cut at only one of the two points where the sphere's silhouette ends on it, is now cut at both.
- **The mesh oracle.** `ryukei_testkit::projection` gives each piece's curve and image lengths by class (sharp and boundary, smooth, outline; an overlap counted once, as its nearest piece's).
  - Its `Oracle` meshes the placed bodies and counts the triangles a ray from a point towards the viewer crosses. Crossings within four chords are the point's own faces. A crossing through a mesh edge, within fifty chords, or a silhouette's grazing crossing within its tangent wedge is unsure.
  - `judge` samples each piece at a quarter, a half and three quarters and compares visible against hidden only. Pieces lying on others, shorter than eighty chords in the image, and samples within twenty chords of another piece's image aren't judged.
  - `silhouette_excess` holds silhouettes square to the view within twice their tolerance times the gradient of `n · towards`.
- **References.** `ryukei-bench`'s OCCT shim runs `HLRBRep_Algo` and `HLRBRep_HLRToShape` (`Solid::hlr`, linking `TKHLR`): the image lengths of its visible and hidden sharp, `Rg1` and outline compounds, and its time.
  - Lengths are summed edge by edge, a missing 3D curve built from the edge's pcurve: `BRepGProp::LinearProperties` crashed on the curveless edges HLR gives a filleted box.
  - Closed forms (a drilled plate from a corner, a filleted box from a tilted corner, both ways; a cylinder side on, visible only) compare totals over classes, since OCCT reads no regularity from STEP and draws a cylinder's seam ruling as a sharp line. The length of pieces ryukei draws once, lying behind another, is the slack: OCCT draws them again, hidden. Seen exactly from a corner, each fillet's rails lie on one image line, so the filleted box is seen off it.
  - Seeded race bodies and the pinned corpus are judged only where OCCT and the mesh agree and ryukei doesn't, every row printed: 148 views, none judged wrong; 18 refused `Uncertain` by ryukei's silhouettes; 702 s on cox-worker-2 (AMD Ryzen 7 9700X, devcontainer, one test thread).
- **The race, a departure from design 326** (issue #608). The design asks ryukei to beat `HLRBRep_Algo` on one thread; plan 10.C ships slower, as the coxswain decided. Measured on cox-worker-2 (AMD Ryzen 7 9700X, 8 threads), in the devcontainer with OCCT 7.8.1, release, one thread, load average about 1.1, 2026-11-08:
  - the 1,000-hole plate's isometric view: ryukei 6.51 s, OCCT 1.83 s (3.6 times), their lengths within 6e-6 relative;
  - the race bodies' isometric views: 351 drawn by both, ryukei 424 s, OCCT 2.24 s (about 190 times).

  The tests print the ratio and assert only regression guards: `PLATE_BAR` (5 times) and `RACE_BAR` (300 times). Where the time goes (phase timers, one thread):
  - the plate's `events` take 3.6 s, over 13,456 image pairs, 8,712 of them line × ellipse at about 0.4 ms each in certified crossings;
  - its rays take 2.8 s, about 0.28 ms for each of 10,009 pieces, each placing points in the top face's 1,000 loops;
  - the race bodies' traced silhouettes take 20.6 s and their `events` 18.8 s (the first 120 bodies);
  - two bodies spend 122 s and 54 s before refusing `Uncertain`.
- **Benchmarks.** `cargo bench -p ryukei-bench --bench projection`, one thread, medians on cox-worker-2 (load 0.1 to 2.5, 2026-11-08): a drilled plate's section 2.69 ms; a plate from a corner with 1 hole 6.95 ms, 100 holes 335 ms, 1,000 holes 6.49 s; a sphere 27.4 ms; a torus askew 322 ms.
- **Testing.**
  - Unit tests in `project::section` and `ryukei_testkit::projection`.
  - `tests/sections.rs`: a drilled plate's cap, a ball's disc, a cylinder's rectangle, a sheet trimmed, a section view, one thread and eight.
  - `tests/work.rs`' `sections_are_bounded` (8,290 units).
  - `tests/projection_props.rs`, release only, its own `full.yml` step: seeded bodies from seeded directions against a mesh at a thousandth of their size, silhouettes square, at most a tenth of pieces uncertain. 1,940 samples judged, 1,633 unsure, 9 of 2,955 pieces uncertain, 10 views refused, in 383 s on cox-worker-2 (load 1.5).
  - The `section_views` golden section (`projection` names phase 2's point projection). No other golden moved.
  - The `projection` fuzz target: views the same twice, against the oracle, and sections. It builds; it hasn't been run in this plan (fuzzing is cox-worker-4's).

- **Limits.**

  - A piece lying behind another in the image is reported once, as ryukei's rule says, never hidden by it.

## Views that certify (decided in phase 17)

The design is document 484 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 17.B (phases 3 and 4).

- **Images near cusps** (#643). Where a root of a NURBS image's hodograph along its principal axis can't be resolved over more than `STRETCH` (1e-6, local parameter), `project::folds` halves the bracket until each part is one to one or a point. One to one means some direction makes every Bernstein coefficient of the hodograph positive, rounded outward. A point means within `LINEAR_RESOLUTION` (an `Image::Point`, the rule a whole curve already had). A part that is neither once it is under `STRETCH` refuses `ViewError::Image`, as before. Folds elsewhere are cut as before, so views that drew before are unchanged bit for bit.
- **A torus square to the view** (`|t_z| ≤ ANGULAR_RESOLUTION`) is in closed form: its two meridian circles in the plane of the axis and the view, and its parallels at `v = ±π/2`, each cut where they cross. Its tolerance is `LINEAR_RESOLUTION + 2 (R + r) √|t_z|`: within the resolution of square, the true contour pinches at the crossings. Tilted further, it is traced; tilts of 1e-9 to 1e-3 certified in 0.55 to 2.3 s (cox-worker-4, AMD Opteron, loaded).
- **Revolutions** are in closed form: `n · t = h'(v) s cos(u − ψ) − ρ'(v) t_z`, so the contour is `u = ψ ± acos(k)` over the stretches where `G = (h's)² − (ρ't_z)²` is positive. `G w⁴` is a polynomial on each Bézier piece of the profile, and its roots (the folds) are isolated by `Bernstein::roots`. The branches are sampled from the formula at Chebyshev's spacing, 48 to a stretch, then fitted and certified by `fit_contour` as traced ones are. The face is traced as before when a root isn't certified simple (a view along or square to the axis), the profile has a crease inside the face, or a fit can't certify.

## Hidden lines faster (decided in phase 17)

The design is document 484 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 17.C (phases 5 and 6, with what 17.B left of #643 and #767).

- **Speed that decides the same things, bit for bit.** A pair's leftover cells are grouped by a sweep with a union-find, in the scan's order (by each group's last cell). Pairs of Bézier pieces come from a box tree past 64 pairs, the old gap test deciding, and a pair whose control points are farther apart than `tol` along either chord or its normal is skipped (the fat line). A segment or a conic against a conic is certified apart in closed form: a segment's `Q` (the gauge's quadratic along it) is bounded by its exact least and greatest values (its ends and its vertex), a conic against a conic by their outer and inner discs. `intersect::FaceIndex` prepares a face's boundary once (its loops' chains, the rays' targets, a box tree over the chains in the parameter plane for faces with no period, and the vertices and a box tree over the edges in `on_boundary`'s order); a view's `Scene` builds one per face on its first ray (`OnceLock`, any thread). Silhouette ends meet only their own edge's pieces, through a map. No golden section moved for these. The one change in kind: a pair certified apart by the fat line or the closed form is `Some([])` where exhausting `MEET_CELLS` or `MEET_DEPTH` used to give `None` (uncertain).
- **Line and conic crossings stay on the subdivision.** In closed form their points' bits, and at a tangency their kind (a `Point` where the subdivision may give an `Overlap`), would differ, and design 484 lets no speed change move a result. They are most of what is left of the 1,000-hole plate's view (3.48 of 3.99 s on cox-worker-2), which stays past OCCT; its bar is recorded on #608 (decision 525).
- **Rays through an edge** (#643). A ray that isn't lenient and meets `Place::OnEdge` keeps the hit; once every face is met, each edge's hits settle together: a seam's one crossing counts once, an edge between two faces both crossed near one point counts once when the ray leaves (or enters) through both and not at all when it leaves through one and enters through the other (the wedge, convex or concave). Any other pattern leaves the ray unsure, as before.
- **A spline image on a line or a conic** (#643): where the subdivision returns `None` (not cancelled), each Bézier piece certified within `tol` of the carrier (control points for a line; `Q`'s Bernstein bound over `w_min²` for a conic) makes the pair an overlap where both run. With the edge rule, every view of the committed drawings is within design 484's tenth (`KNOWN_UNCERTAIN` is empty): the crankcase's 656 / 1,753 / 133 uncertain pieces went to 37 / 17 / 51, its twin's to 37 of 1,571, 13 of 1,786 and 49 of 1,061. What is left is mostly edge hits in a pattern the rule leaves unsure (Task 10's tally on #643).
- **Revolutions' branches in parts** (#767): a branch the fit can't certify whole is halved at its middle sample, down to sixteen parts, depth first; race bodies 16, 56 and 229, refused before, draw. Where the parts can't be certified the face is still traced: the census (Task 13) found a face only the trace draws after the formula's fit fails, so the trace stays and its minutes with it (recorded on #767). A fit's time is mostly its lift (`fit_along`, about 78 %, the rest certifying spans); a map of spans' bounds across rounds bought nothing, since each round's lift moves nearly every span (#608).
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `geom::intersect2` (clusters against the scan, pruned pairs against every pair, misses, carriers), `intersect::inface` (`FaceIndex` against `point_in_face_near` on a 100-bore plate), `project::rays` (a ray through a block's edge both ways). `tests/hidden_lines.rs`: the twin's views within the tenth, its front view mostly certifies, and the slow revolutions draw or refuse (`--ignored`, minutes). `ryukei-bench`'s `view-probe` example times a view's parts on one thread.
- **Bars** (cox-worker-2, an AMD Ryzen 7 9700X, release, one thread): the 1,000-hole plate's view 4.02 s (6.76 before 17.C), 2.14 times OCCT's 1.82 s, so design 326's bar of beating OCCT is unmet (#608); the race bodies' views 437.6 s in all (478.8). Task 21's runs fill in the rest.
