## Tolerant booleans (decided in phase 14)

The design is document 299 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 14.A.

- **The tube model.** Surfaces are exact; edges and vertices are tolerant: an entity lies within its tolerance of its true place. `intersect::point_in_body` calls a point on the boundary only in an edge's or vertex's tube (`inface::on_boundary`) or within the resolution of a face's surface inside it, and casts rays at the resolution; `point_in_face` closes each gap between a tolerant loop's pcurves with a straight segment, after the pcurves, so no ray slips through a corner.
- **The table.** Two points of one global vertex, and a vertex on a carrier, are one within that vertex's own tolerance as well as the carrier's (`add_point`, the carriers' dedup). A tolerant edge's pcurve on another operand's coincident face is fitted as healing fits one (`intersect::fit_pcurve_tolerant`). An operand's edge found lying in a face at the face's boundary tolerance and a stretch on that face running along it are one global edge (`ons_together`, identified at that reach), so a tool's micron step inside a tolerant face's tube leaves no sliver. A tolerant edge's cut (tolerance past `TOUCH_TOLERANCE`) splits a branch that passes through its tube, not only one within the branch's reach of the hit, and the stretch end it makes clusters within its distance from the hit (`intersect::Stretch::ends`).
- **Splitting.** At a global vertex whose darts' ends lie more than `TOUCH_TOLERANCE` from it, the darts are ordered by their directions from the vertex where they leave its tube (twice its radius), not by their tangents there: a tolerant rim may start microns off an exact edge and leave turned the other way. Every exact body's vertices keep the old order.
- **Merging.** Edges joined onto a curve more than the resolution from theirs take a tolerance covering both; an edge whose pcurves' images end farther from its vertices than its tolerance and the resolution takes a tolerance covering them (`merge::cover_ends`, which the mesher's `EdgeOffSurface` asks for).
- **The touch rule.** A touch reaches 100 tolerances up to `tol::TOUCH_TOLERANCE` (1e-7, a fitted curve's), and past it only its tube's width (`tol::touch_reach`), in `curve_surface` and the table; the pipe and spline searches keep their own rule, which no case has needed changed.
- **Face pairs.** A trace `face_face` leaves uncertain, after its carried retry, is traced once more in a region grown by the slack and `TOUCH_TOLERANCE`: a tolerant face's box put a sphere's tangency with a cylinder on cells the trace couldn't settle, for one region's exact bits only.
- **The cap.** `tol::TOLERANCE_CAP` (1e-4 m) is healing's default cap, and a boolean whose result would hold a tolerance past it is `BooleanError::ToleranceTooLarge { at, needed }`. Operands moved 3e-5 m already hold tolerances up to 9e-5, and some of their results (clustered vertices, merged edges) pass the cap: those are refused, and the tests and fuzzing count the refusal as one.
- **Testing.** `HealOptions::tighten` (default true) off keeps every gap a tolerance; `ryukei_testkit::tolerant_bytes` makes tolerant twins of bodies and booleans. `tests/tolerant_booleans.rs` (a box and a cylinder 1e-6 to 3e-5 m off, drilled, slotted, united and intersected flush, a cylinder flush and tangent to a block, a micron step, a shallow drill, a fan at a corner, the cap, and the seeded cases the property test found), `tests/tolerant_boolean_props.rs` (96 seeded cases against their exact twins, release only: 89 compared, 1 past the cap), ryustep's `drill.rs` (27 bodies of the committed corpus and OCCT files drilled untightened), `ryukei-bench`'s `pinned_bodies_drill_untightened` (devcontainer), the `tolerant` fuzz target and the `tolerant_booleans` golden section. Exact bodies' results didn't move a bit.
- **Limits.** Design 299's branch-end extension by `t / sin θ` wasn't needed: a drill crossing a tolerant edge at 5° passes without it. The full sliver collapse after selection (healing's `collapse` rules on bodies) wasn't built: the table's narrow rule settles the micron step, and other slivers are 14.B's. Coincident faces' rims aren't cut where they pass through the other face's vertex tubes (work item 4093, deferred): no case needed it.

## Tolerant blends (decided in phase 14)

The design is document 299 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 14.B.

- **What blends needed.** A probe found fillets and chamfers on tolerant twins (edges 1e-7 to 3e-5 m off) mostly succeed as on the exact bodies: spines come from the faces' offsets, rails from the faces, ends cross a tolerant edge within `end::crossing`'s reach, and every committed STEP body that fillets tightened fillets untightened. What failed were chamfers on tolerant circles and smooth joins, below. Every rule here applies only past `tol::TOUCH_TOLERANCE`, so exact bodies' results didn't move a bit.
- **Convexity.** `convex::bend` encloses a face's normal over a stretch of a tolerant edge from its pcurve's spline pieces cut to the stretch (`NurbsCurve2::clipped_bounds`; `BezierCurve::clipped` no longer keeps a whole piece a range only touches at a knot), and judges a tolerant edge smooth where its faces' normals at the pcurves' feet are within `ANGULAR_RESOLUTION` plus twice its tolerance times their curvatures at every sample (a slot's laterals between a plane and a half cylinder).
- **Chamfers.** A tolerant circle off its faces is chamfered along the circle its faces meet in (`chamfer::tube_carrier`; parameter 0 at a closed edge's vertex, opposite an arc's middle so no seam meets its faces), a cone between coaxial faces at any distance and angle, or along the faces' branch where one is a spline or a swept surface. Through a smooth join at a tolerant vertex the segments' rails meet within twice the vertex's tolerance (`chamfer::joint`), and the section there, which ends at that vertex, is fitted on the next chamfer at its gap (`intersect::fit_pcurve_tolerant` in `build`).
- **Overflow.** `overflow::in_strip` takes, for a strip along tolerant edges, the section through the point: the rail's nearest point and the edges' nearest to that (a cone's apex was judged across the strip).
- **Issue #345.** `split`'s tube order and `point_in_face`'s loop closing apply only to darts and loops on edges past `TOUCH_TOLERANCE`, so exact bodies take neither by construction.
- **Testing.** `ryukei_testkit::tolerant_bytes::tolerant_blend_case` (and `twin_blends`, the twin's names for an exact case's edges and faces); `tests/tolerant_blends.rs` (the probe's fourteen cases at four moves, the cone rims, a slot's rim filleted and chamfered through its smooth joins, a hole beside a strip, the cap, the seeds the property test found); `tests/tolerant_blend_props.rs` (96 seeded cases, release only: 41 compared, in 781 s on cox-worker-1); ryustep's `drill.rs` (17 committed bodies filleted untightened, 456 s); `ryukei-bench`'s `pinned_bodies_fillet_untightened` (devcontainer); the `tolerant` fuzz target's blends (an odd first byte); the `tolerant_blends` golden section.
- **Limits.** Design 299's strips grown by their edges' tolerances and `BlendError::ToleranceTooLarge` weren't needed: a hole reaching 1e-5 into a fillet's strip overflows on the tolerant twin as on the exact one, and no tolerant blend made a tolerance past the cap (results held at most the bodies' own, 9.3e-5 at the 3e-5 move). At the 3e-5 move a slot's arcs heal past the cap, so it has no twin there.
