## Tolerant booleans (decided in phase 14)

The design is document 299 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 14.A.

- **The tube model.** Surfaces are exact; edges and vertices are tolerant: an entity lies within its tolerance of its true place. `intersect::point_in_body` calls a point on the boundary only in an edge's or vertex's tube (`inface::on_boundary`) or within the resolution of a face's surface inside it, and casts rays at the resolution; `point_in_face` closes each gap between a tolerant loop's pcurves with a straight segment, after the pcurves, so no ray slips through a corner.
- **The table.** Two points of one global vertex, and a vertex on a carrier, are one within that vertex's own tolerance as well as the carrier's (`add_point`, the carriers' dedup). A tolerant edge's pcurve on another operand's coincident face is fitted as healing fits one (`intersect::fit_pcurve_tolerant`). An operand's edge found lying in a face at the face's boundary tolerance and a stretch on that face running along it are one global edge (`ons_together`, identified at that reach), so a tool's micron step inside a tolerant face's tube leaves no sliver. A tolerant edge's cut (tolerance past `TOUCH_TOLERANCE`) splits a branch that passes through its tube, not only one within the branch's reach of the hit, and the stretch end it makes clusters within its distance from the hit (`intersect::Stretch::ends`).
- **Splitting.** At a global vertex whose darts' ends lie more than `TOUCH_TOLERANCE` from it, the darts are ordered by their directions from the vertex where they leave its tube (twice its radius), not by their tangents there: a tolerant rim may start microns off an exact edge and leave turned the other way. Every exact body's vertices keep the old order.
- **Merging.** Edges joined onto a curve more than the resolution from theirs take a tolerance covering both; an edge whose pcurves' images end farther from its vertices than its tolerance and the resolution takes a tolerance covering them (`merge::cover_ends`, which the mesher's `EdgeOffSurface` asks for).
- **Vertices within resolution along a carrier** (#417, the 0.8.x burn-down). Two global vertices within `LINEAR_RESOLUTION` of each other along a carrier would make a piece shorter than any edge, which no operand has: a tolerant vertex projecting within its tolerance onto an edge of its own body where that edge ends at another of the body's vertices 0.3 µm away, or a stretch of two faces ending at two vertices of one operand 10 µm apart across the face. Clustering never merges two vertices of one operand, and both darts at the vertex then left one way, so splitting tied (`Uncertain`). The one nearer the carrier stays on it, ties to the lower, and the other keeps its own edges (`table::drop_coincident`). Exact bodies never put two vertices of a carrier that close, so no golden hash moved. KiCad's f186 now unites; a950 gets past the tie to a union its solids touching along an edge refuse (`NonManifold`, the file's, #208).
- **The touch rule.** A touch reaches 100 tolerances up to `tol::TOUCH_TOLERANCE` (1e-7, a fitted curve's), and past it only its tube's width (`tol::touch_reach`), in `curve_surface` and the table; the pipe and spline searches keep their own rule, which no case has needed changed.
- **Face pairs.** A trace `face_face` leaves uncertain, after its carried retry, is traced once more in a region grown by the slack and `TOUCH_TOLERANCE`: a tolerant face's box put a sphere's tangency with a cylinder on cells the trace couldn't settle, for one region's exact bits only.
- **The cap.** `tol::TOLERANCE_CAP` (1e-4 m) is healing's default cap, and a boolean whose result would hold a tolerance past it is `BooleanError::ToleranceTooLarge { at, needed }`. Operands moved 3e-5 m already hold tolerances up to 9e-5, and some of their results (clustered vertices, merged edges) pass the cap: those are refused, and the tests and fuzzing count the refusal as one.
- **Testing.** `HealOptions::tighten` (default true) off keeps every gap a tolerance; `ryukei_testkit::tolerant_bytes` makes tolerant twins of bodies and booleans. `tests/tolerant_booleans.rs` (a box and a cylinder 1e-6 to 3e-5 m off, drilled, slotted, united and intersected flush, a cylinder flush and tangent to a block, a micron step, a shallow drill, a fan at a corner, the cap, and the seeded cases the property test found), `tests/tolerant_boolean_props.rs` (96 seeded cases against their exact twins, release only: 89 compared, 1 past the cap), ryustep's `drill.rs` (27 bodies of the committed corpus and OCCT files drilled untightened), `ryukei-bench`'s `pinned_bodies_drill_untightened` (devcontainer), the `tolerant` fuzz target and the `tolerant_booleans` golden section. Exact bodies' results didn't move a bit.
- **Limits.** Design 299's branch-end extension by `t / sin θ` wasn't needed: a drill crossing a tolerant edge at 5° passes without it. The full sliver collapse after selection (healing's `collapse` rules on bodies) wasn't built: the table's narrow rule settles the micron step, and other slivers are 14.B's. Coincident faces' rims aren't cut where they pass through the other face's vertex tubes (work item 4093, deferred): no case needed it.

## Tolerant blends (decided in phase 14)

The design is document 299 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 14.B.

- **What blends needed.** A probe found fillets and chamfers on tolerant twins (edges 1e-7 to 3e-5 m off) mostly succeed as on the exact bodies: spines come from the faces' offsets, rails from the faces, ends cross a tolerant edge within `end::crossing`'s reach, and every committed STEP body that fillets tightened fillets untightened. What failed were chamfers on tolerant circles and smooth joins, below. Every rule here applies only past `tol::TOUCH_TOLERANCE`, so exact bodies' results didn't move a bit.
- **Convexity.** `convex::bend` encloses a face's normal over a stretch of a tolerant edge from its pcurve's spline pieces cut to the stretch (`NurbsCurve2::clipped_bounds`; `BezierCurve::clipped` no longer keeps a whole piece a range only touches at a knot), and judges a tolerant edge smooth where its faces' normals at the pcurves' feet are within `ANGULAR_RESOLUTION` plus twice its tolerance times their curvatures at every sample (a slot's laterals between a plane and a half cylinder).
- **Chamfers.** A tolerant circle off its faces is chamfered along the circle its faces meet in (`chamfer::tube_carrier`; parameter 0 at a closed edge's vertex, opposite an arc's middle so no seam meets its faces), a cone between coaxial faces at any distance and angle, or along the faces' branch where one is a spline or a swept surface. Through a smooth join at a tolerant vertex the segments' rails meet within twice the vertex's tolerance (`chamfer::joint`), and the section there, which ends at that vertex, is fitted on the next chamfer at its gap (`intersect::fit_pcurve_tolerant` in `build`).
- **Overflow.** `overflow::in_strip` takes, for a strip along tolerant edges, the section through the point: the rail's nearest point and the edges' nearest to that (a cone's apex was judged across the strip).
- **Issue #345.** `split`'s tube order and `point_in_face`'s loop closing apply only to darts and loops on edges past `TOUCH_TOLERANCE`, so exact bodies take neither by construction.
- **Testing.** `ryukei_testkit::tolerant_bytes::tolerant_blend_case` (and `twin_blends`, the twin's names for an exact case's edges and faces); `tests/tolerant_blends.rs` (the probe's fourteen cases at four moves, the cone rims, a slot's rim filleted and chamfered through its smooth joins, a hole beside a strip, the cap, the seeds the property test found); `tests/tolerant_blend_props.rs` (96 seeded cases, release only: 41 compared, in 781 s on cox-worker-1); ryustep's `drill.rs` (17 committed bodies filleted untightened, 456 s); `ryukei-bench`'s `pinned_bodies_fillet_untightened` (devcontainer); the `tolerant` fuzz target's blends (an odd first byte); the `tolerant_blends` golden section.
- **Limits.** Design 299's strips grown by their edges' tolerances and `BlendError::ToleranceTooLarge` weren't needed: a hole reaching 1e-5 into a fillet's strip overflows on the tolerant twin as on the exact one, and no tolerant blend made a tolerance past the cap (results held at most the bodies' own, 9.3e-5 at the 3e-5 move). At the 3e-5 move a slot's arcs heal past the cap, so it has no twin there.

## Self-union (decided in phase 14)

The design is document 299 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 14.C.

- **API.** `repair::self_union(key, &body)` (and `_with` under `Work`) gives the valid solid of the points where `body`'s winding number is one or more, regularized, and a journal; `body` must be a solid whose topology holds, its faces free to cross and overlap. A body `check_intersections` finds valid comes back as it was, bit for bit, journal empty, so a self-union costs at least the check. Refusals are `Error::InvalidRepair(RepairError)`: `Uncertain { faces }` (a pair the check can't check, or an intersection, split or classification that stays uncertain), `NonManifold { at, faces }` (the region's boundary meets itself at an edge or a point: solids touching along a line), `Empty` (nothing has winding number one), `ToleranceTooLarge { at, needed }`. Nothing is built until every step has succeeded.
- **The table on one body.** The pairs `check_intersections` reports crossing or overlapping are the only pairs met (`boolean::table::Scope::Pairs`); an edge or vertex meets a face it doesn't bound that is listed with one of its faces, a stretch any third face listed with either of its pair. Two of the body's vertices closer than their tolerances' sum are one point, whatever joins them: healing leaves an edge longer than its own tolerance and shorter than its ends' (#322's USB connector), which the self-union collapses. `Scope::Operands` is the boolean's, bit for bit.
- **Selection by winding.** `intersect::winding` is `point_in_body`'s rays counted with signs (`+1` leaving through a face, `-1` entering); `winding_off` counts from a point on a face along rays leaving to its outer side, the hits there on the face and the faces on its surface left out. Pieces are grouped across the body's own edges nothing else touches, one ray per group. A piece is kept when the winding number just outside it is at most 0 and just inside at least 1 (the jump being 1, and ±1 for each face on its surface there by whether it faces the piece's way), and no face facing its way there comes before its own; none is reversed. `boolean::assemble` builds, merges and names the kept pieces as a boolean's target's: faces kept whole keep their names, pieces are `Piece`s named by the faces that cut them (in one body, every other face whose curve bounds the piece), new edges and vertices `IntersectionEdge` and `IntersectionVertex`.
- **Healing.** `HealOptions::repair` (default true): where healing's first build (every connected piece a solid, facing as the file's faces do) is refused `ShellsCross` or `FacesOverlap` naming two faces of one of the file's solids, `self_union` repairs it under a key from the import's (`ImportRole::Repair`), and healing finishes as it would have; `HealReport::repaired` lists the faces the repair cut, split or removed, and the journal ends with the repair's. A repair refused, or finding nothing, leaves healing's refusal as it was, but for one case: a repair refused `NonManifold` (the pieces the overlap glues touch along a line or at a point, as KiCad's MSBR-1 does, #434) is `HealError::PiecesTouch { a, b, at, faces }`, the overlapping (or crossing) pair, where the union meets itself and the faces there, which ryucad reports as `import_failed` with reason `pieces_touch` (decided in the 0.8.x burn-down: uniting such pieces needs non-manifold bodies, so the refusal says why rather than that the faces overlap). Healing still runs no full check: shells crossing with no vertex of either inside the other, and faces crossing within a shell, heal as the file has them (#318), and are `self_union`'s when a caller asks. `heal::sew` keeps `repair` off (decided in the merge with 12.C): a caller sewing sheets who wants the result repaired asks `self_union`.
- **Across a file's solids** see `step.md`'s Real-world files: ryustep unites them.
- **Testing.** Unit tests (`intersect::winding`, `repair`, the table's scope, `RepairError`'s text); `tests/self_union.rs` (crossing shells united, a cavity cut out, inside-out shells, stacked and coincident shells, names, a polyhedron crossing itself against its winding numbers on a grid, a valid body unchanged); `tests/self_union_props.rs` (48 seeded crossing cases against their parts' winding numbers, release only); the `self_union` fuzz target; the `self_union` golden section. `ryukei_testkit::crossing` makes the shapes.
- **Limits.** Solids touching along an edge or at a point (KiCad pins against bodies: 6 of #318's cases, #319's Everlight and Sharp, #322's USB connector; ledgered `file`) are `NonManifold`, which no manifold body holds. A pair the check can't check is `Uncertain` (the barrel jacks of #318), and so are planes tilted by a file's f32 rounding (2.4e-7 rad) lying within resolution of another over an area, which `check_intersections` reports as nothing, so no winding ray settles a piece on them (#318's Ethernet transformer and one more: 15.C's). Three of #318's cases repair to one shell that checks clean, each in under 3 s. A capacitor of #322 (`CP_Radial_D8.0mm_P2.50mm`) took over an hour in its repair in planning; its twin at P5.00 now imports, and its `check_intersections` takes 1,318 s on two threads on davids-m4 with 18 `CannotCheck` pairs (15.C's, #322). The checks of KiCad's QFP packages and of an Eagle board time out or run slow under step-hunt (#353, 15.C's); `known.txt` ledgers them under #353, and the capacitors' imports under #322.

## Global offsets (decided in phase 14)

The design is document 299 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 14.D.

- **When.** `offset_body` and `shell` try 8.E's local build first; where it refuses `FaceCollapses`, `EdgeVanishes`, `SelfIntersecting` or `Unsupported` (`MixedVertex`, `ClosingVertex`, `SharedSection`) and every face moves by one distance, the global offset runs (`offset::global`). Its refusal is the answer; faces at distances of their own keep the local refusal (#396). Every offset the local build makes is unchanged, bit for bit.
- **The envelope.** The local plan in `Mode::Envelope`: every edge between faces at the distance that isn't smooth gets a rolling-ball fill, inverted (the other sense) where the offsets close; a cylinder, sphere or torus whose offset passes its axis, centre or core circle is kept inverted, its own kind on the other side (`offset::inverted`: a cylinder's frame turned, a sphere's antipode `(u + π, -v)` with its sense flipped, a torus's `(u, v + π)`; a pipe's other side of its spine, its angle `θ + π` (17.A); any other face whose offset passes its focal surface whole, a turned or inverted `Offset` on its base's parameters with its sense kept (17.A, #421)); spheres at vertices, one face where the arcs turn one way about it, else a fan of spherical triangles from the apex furthest from flat (`fan_apex`). An inverted fill about a circle no larger than the distance is a spindle torus: two surfaces of revolution (the section's arc from each rail to the point of the axis every section passes through, the inner one mirrored), meeting at a vertex there that both ends' split sections share. Built through the layout with no check.
- **Selection.** `repair::resolve` (14.C's pipeline, shared): `check_intersections`' crossing and overlapping pairs, the `Scope::Pairs` table, `split_all`, groups across untouched edges (inverted faces and fan triangles alone), then by distance: a group is dropped where an inner point has a face's projection inside it, or a point of an edge, nearer than `|d| - 2 reach`; kept where every face whose box comes within `|d| - reach` is certified outside the ball of that radius (`intersect::face_off_ball`, `beside` against a sphere's implicit form) and the point is inside (inwards) or outside (outwards); `reach` is `100 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION` or twice the face's tolerance; four inner points, then `Uncertain`. Inverted faces are dropped untested. Pairs holding an inverted face aren't checked or cut: every point of one lies nearer the body than `|d|`, so no kept piece is bounded there (17.A; a turned spline face against its smooth neighbour ended `CannotCheck` after 57 s). Nothing kept is `OffsetError::Empty`; a tolerance past the cap `ToleranceTooLarge` (`OffsetError` lost `Eq` for it).
- **Shells.** Inwards, each planar open face is carried out by a prism the thickness deep (which must meet the body only there), united with the body, and the extended body is offset at one distance; outwards, only a shell with no open face is global. A non-planar open face keeps the local refusal.
- **Names.** As the envelope names them (faces keep their names; fills, spheres, spindles' inner halves and fan triangles from what they replace), pieces as the resolve does (`Piece`s by their cutters); the journal is the envelope's then the resolve's.
- **Testing.** Unit tests (`offset::inverted`, the envelope of a rounded box and of a tray, `fan_apex`, `resolve`, `face_off_ball`, an L both ways globally against the local closed forms); `tests/global_offsets.rs` (a rounded box shrunk past its radius, bodies shrunk away, a slot grown shut, a saddle grown against the union of its boxes grown, a thin step, filleted holes and a tray shelled past their fillets, names across distances); `tests/global_offset_props.rs` (release: the offset fuzz target's bodies, every vertex at its distance); the `global_offsets` golden section; ryukei-bench's `global_offsets_against_occt_and_smlib`, the shell race and `pinned_bodies_shell_untightened` (devcontainer); the `offset` fuzz target reaches the global path unchanged (an hour on cox-worker-1: 276 runs, no find). Since 17.A: a spline ring shrunk past its corners against its analytic twin and a pipe-rimmed elliptic tube shrunk past its fillets (`global_offsets.rs`), seeded spline rings in `global_offset_props` (`ryukei_testkit::rings`), the spline ring in `global_offsets_against_occt_and_smlib`, the `inverted_offsets` golden section, and the fuzz target's `offset_case_past` (a sixteenth of its inputs, at distances past most bodies' curvature).
- **The race.** Design 299's: a 200 × 200 × 10 mm plate with 100 holes of 8 mm, their top rims filleted at 1 mm, shelled inwards by 2 mm with its bottom open, on one thread (`ryukei-bench`'s `the_shell_race_against_occt_on_one_thread`). On cox-worker-1 (Intel Xeon E5-2650, 8 threads, shared, load 5.5 to 10.5), in the devcontainer, three runs on 2026-11-03: ryukei 0.000132314914 m³ (exact, ± 3.3e-7) in 6.8, 6.8 and 9.1 s; OCCT's `MakeThickSolid` refused it every time (in 1.0 to 1.7 s), so the bar (ryukei within twice OCCT's time) is judged only where OCCT's volume agrees, and wasn't. Against OCCT and SMLib (`global_offsets_against_occt_and_smlib`): the rounded box shrunk past its radius is exact and SMLib agrees, OCCT gives 0; the tray shelled past its fillets is exact, and both refuse it.
- **The corpus.** `pinned_bodies_shell_untightened` tries each body at a fortieth, a hundredth and a four-hundredth of its least side (90 s each): every `PINNED_SET` body but NIST CTC 03 refuses tightened at all three (`PartlySmooth`, the local build's; `FaceCollapses`; `Uncertain`; past 90 s), so `SHELL_SET` adds NIST FTC 11 (AP203) and ryustep's committed OCCT bodies, each of which must be compared: 13 of 18 bodies shell both ways to equal volumes (622 s on cox-worker-1). The OCCT spline revolution's shell checks clean, but its offset face doesn't mesh at the measure's default chord, so its volume is taken at 1e-4 of its size.
- **Limits.** A face whose offset passes its focal surface in part (its focal surface crossing it) is `FaceCollapses` (question 523), and so is one whose factor stays within the certificate's reach of zero over `MAX_CELLS` (a spline tube's whole turn at `−0.07`; a spline sphere's band near its poles at `−0.4`); a fill about a spline edge past its curvature (a pipe that folds) is `Unsupported` `Fill` (#421, a spline-circle cylinder shrunk away); a local offset tracing a closing edge on a spline's offset can take minutes (#408, the property test's seed 3), and one global envelope's self-intersection check about 11 minutes on a shared Xeon E5-2650 before ending `CannotCheck` (#416, seed 14); a spindle fill at a vertex of three runs or more, two at one vertex, or a forward fill about a circle smaller than the distance is `Unsupported` `Fill`; outward shells with open faces and offsets with faces at their own distances are local only.
