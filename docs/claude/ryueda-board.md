## Board files and placement (decided in ryueda 5.A)

The design is document 732 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; these are its phases 1 and 2, plan 5.A.

- **Files.** `boards/<board>/board.json` (kind `ryueda.board`, format 1: `name`, `design`, `preset` `jlc-2l`, `outline`, `cutouts`) and `boards/<board>/placements/<occurrence>.json` (kind `ryueda.placement`: `at`, `rotation_mdeg`), canonical JSON, unknown fields refused. A contour is `{start, edges: [{type: line, to} | {type: arc, mid, to}]}`, closed when the last `to` is `start`. On the command line its coordinates are `coord`'s lengths: a JSON integer is nanometres, a string a quantity (`"2 mm"`, or `"2"` in the project's unit). A placement's file is its occurrence with `/` as `.` (`s1.u1.json`); IDs hold no `.`.
- **Judging.** `board_outline_invalid`: not closed, an edge of no length, an arc through three points in a line, a contour that crosses or touches itself, a cutout outside the outline or meeting another. Straight contours are judged by exact integer tests; a contour with arcs on its ring flattened at 10 nm (the explicit tolerance rule: within 10 nm of crossing is refused). `board_out_of_range` past ±2^40 nm; `board_design_not_found`; an unknown preset is `invalid_document`. Files under `boards/` that aren't a board's are `unknown_file` warnings.
- **Revisions.** Each board's is BLAKE3 over its folder's files (`board/<id>` in `project.changed`); the project's revision doesn't take boards. `board create` guards the project; every other board write guards its board.
- **Session and Git.** Boards are tracked files (undo restores `board.json` before placements), in `.gitattributes` (`boards/**/*.json merge=ryueda diff=ryueda`), read at a revision (`git::FOLDERS`, `project_file`), merged by value per key (a placement's `at` is one value: two moves of one part conflict), flattened by key in textconv, and known to doctor through the tracked files. Boards never move a schematic (`validate::affects`) and are outside PDM (question 1086).
- **Occurrences.** `board::occ`: the design flattened (a design that doesn't flatten refuses with its faults), each occurrence by its reference without the design's prefix (`s1/u1`), its footprint as the netlist chose it, each pad number's net (`sda`, `s1/<net>`, `rail:gnd`). `REF` is an occurrence reference, with or without the prefix, else a designator; one two occurrences share is `board_ref_ambiguous`.
- **Shapes.** `board::shapes::build`, one parallel task per placement, the same bits on any thread count. Pads by `ryu-board`'s symbols (ODB++ corner numbers for chamfers), custom pads as their anchor and primitives (closed primitives filled, as KiCad draws them); drills as `ryu_copper::layout::HoleShape`, plated for through pads. Mask is the pad grown by its margin, else its footprint's, else the preset's; paste by its margin plus its ratio of the smaller side; standard shapes exactly, custom ones through `ryu_copper::poly` at 10 nm. Graphics by `board::graphic` (Béziers as 16 segments, marked approximate). Text in `ryu-board`'s built-in stroke font (`ryu-stroke`, printable ASCII on an 8 × 12 grid, drawn by us), centred on the field's place as KiCad places it, `${REFERENCE}` and `${VALUE}` filled in. An `edge` graphic or a pad on `copper.inner` is `board_footprint_unsupported` and the placement is left out; a missing glyph is `board_text_missing_glyph`; both in `notices`. The session keeps the last four boards' shapes by the board's, the project's, the libraries' and the blocks' revisions.
- **Pads.** `<board>:<refdes>/<number>`, `<number>@<pad id>` when the number repeats, `@<pad id>` for none.
- **The preset.** `board::preset::JLC_2L`: mask expansion 0 (the page's "1:1", pad size to soldermask opening, since its LDI upgrade of June 2025) and paste margin 0 (the page gives none), from JLCPCB's capabilities page read on 2026-11-17 (the source quotes it). Not a file; 5.B adds the rules, the 0.09 mm opening-to-trace clearance among them. `board/preset.rs` is the one file outside the parts connectors that names the fabricator; `tests/connectors.rs` allows it.
- **Commands.** `board create NAME --design D (--rect W,H | --outline OPS) [--id]`, `board edit B [--name] [--outline] [--cutouts]`, `board delete`, `board list`, `board show B [--pads]` (occurrences with their placements, `unplaced`, `orphans`, `notices`), `board place B REF --at X,Y [--rotation A]` or `--ops` (all or nothing), `board unplace B REF` (a placement whose occurrence is gone by its occurrence reference; `--rotation` is degrees, and in `--ops` a string is degrees and a JSON integer millidegrees, as `coord` reads angles), `board render B --out FILE [--layer L…] [--side top|bottom] [--net N] [--window X0,Y0,X1,Y1] [--size PX] [--format]`: PNG or SVG in fixed colours per layer, the outline framed with a millimetre, mirrored from below; `--net` dims copper and highlights that net's pads on each copper side. Renders draw the session's kept shapes (`Session::board_shapes`).
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`board::file`, `outline`, `load`, `occ`, `graphic`, `pad`, `opening`, `text`, `shapes`, `render`, `ryu_board::shape::font`), `tests/cli.rs` (both ways, parity, undo), `tests/git.rs` (two branches placing parts merge, one part moved both ways conflicts), `tests/deps.rs`, and `tests/golden/ryueda.txt`'s `board.breakout.show`, `.top.png` and `.bottom.svg` (one thread and eight alike). The `boards/**` line in `.gitattributes` moved `merge.breakout.clashes.files`, which hashes the merged project's files.

## Routes and DRC (decided in ryueda 5.B)

The design is document 732 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; these are its phases 3 and 4, plan 5.B.

- **Files.** `boards/<board>/tracks/<id>.json` (`ryueda.track`: `net`, `layer` top|bottom, `width_nm`, `points`), `vias/<id>.json` (`ryueda.via`: `net`, `at`, `drill_nm`, `diameter_nm`), `holes/<id>.json` (`ryueda.hole`: `at`, `drill_nm`), format 1, in the board's revision, Git's layout, the merge driver's kinds and textconv. Not positive, or a track without two distinct points: `invalid_document`; past ±2^40 nm: `board_out_of_range`; a net the design doesn't have: `board_net_not_found` (judged with the project, so deleting a net a track names is refused until the track goes).
- **Commands.** `board track add|edit|delete`, `board via add|delete`, `board hole add|delete` (`--ops` for several, all or nothing), `board drc B [--code C…]` (`check_failed` with the report when an error is listed), `board status B`. A path point is `X,Y` or a pad (`R1/1`) or via (`via/<id>`) reference for its centre; the file keeps coordinates. `--path` takes no hyphen values (with several values it swallowed the next flag), so a point with a negative coordinate goes through `--ops` (`track add --ops`, or `track edit --ops` with `{net?, layer?, width?, path?}`; lengths as strings, bare integers nanometres, as `coord` reads them). Defaults: 250 µm tracks, 300/600 µm vias (ryueda's, held at or above the rules by a test).
- **The layout.** `board::layout::Key`: `Pad {occ, pad, layer}`, `Track`, `Via(id, layer)`, `PadDrill`, `ViaDrill`, `Hole`; two copper layers; a netlist point per pad with a net (a through pad's at its drill, through the plating). `board::connect`: `Copper::new` at `BodyOptions::default()`, `netlist::compare`, and each open net's connections (a spanning tree of nearest pairs between its islands).
- **Findings** (`board::drc`): ERC's shape, IDs over `ryueda.board-finding.1`, code, board and sorted references; sorted by code then references. The design's table, plus `net_claim_mismatch` (error), `netlist_point_off_copper` (warning) and `drc_mask_clearance` (error, the page's 0.09 mm). Two islands of one net aren't a clearance finding. DRC never under-reports, so two shorts the compare can't see are found here: copper joining a pad with no net (no netlist point) to a net (`net_short` with `netless`, once per island), and an island no point landed on whose items claim two nets or more (`net_short` with its touching pairs). A pad or via is one reference on both layers, so `drc_clearance` and `copper_contact_uncertain` are one finding per pair of references, the closest gap's `layer` and every `layers` it is under on; a via's bad drill and diameter are one `drc_drill`; and `board::drc::unique` folds any finding repeating an ID into the first's `details.also`, so an answer's IDs are unique (the write's delta and ryueda 7's waivers key on them). Rings: vias by their numbers against the via rule; through pads from the hole to the pad's outline against the PTH rule, exact for regions, flattened at 10 nm otherwise. Hole spacing: two vias' holes by the via rule, any other pair by the pad rule. Drills: plated holes in the PTH range, unplated ones from the NPTH minimum. Uncertain verdicts are findings with `uncertain: true`. A placement left out (`board_footprint_unsupported`) marks opens and shorts `incomplete`. An open courtyard is judged by its graphics' box as drawn (half the pen beyond them).
- **The preset.** `JLC_2L`'s rules, read by the coxswain from JLCPCB's capabilities page on 2026-11-17 for its 1-2 layer, 1.6 mm, 1 oz process (quoted in `preset.rs`): track and clearance 0.10 mm; drills 0.15-6.3 mm plated, 0.50 mm least unplated; via diameter 0.25 mm; rings 0.20 mm round a through pad's hole and 0.05 mm round a via's (the page has no via row: its smallest via's geometry, which one 0.20 mm rule would refuse); hole to hole 0.2 mm between vias, 0.45 mm otherwise; copper to a routed edge 0.2 mm (holes too: the page gives no figure for them); 1.6 mm board, 1 oz copper (35 µm converted: the page gives no µm). Not overridable until ryueda 7.
- **The session** keeps the last four boards' checks by the shapes' key; every board write but `board delete` answers `drc: {added, removed, errors, warnings}` (`{error}` when the design doesn't flatten). Rebuilt per revision: the generated board's track write with its delta took 129 ms through a warm daemon on cox-worker-5 (Intel Core Ultra 7 265KF), under the 250 ms bar, so `ryu-copper`'s quiet edits aren't used (plan task 26 deferred).
- **`check`** has a `boards` section only when the project has boards; board findings count in its totals.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per rule at the rule's edge on boards drawn by hand (`board::testing`); `tests/board_props.rs` (generated serpentine boards clean, each seeded fault exactly its finding, one thread and eight); `tests/cli.rs` both ways; `tests/git.rs` (routes from two branches merge); goldens `board.breakout.drc`, `.layout`, `.islands`; the `board` fuzz target (74,645 runs in 10 minutes, no crash); the bars in `tests/bar.rs` (cox-worker-5, `RYUEDA_BARS=1`: the generated board, 2,000 pads and 1,998 tracks, checked cold in 62 ms against 1 s; its track write 129 ms against 250 ms; the breakout's track write 11.8 ms against 50 ms).

## Fabrication outputs (decided in ryueda 5.C)

The design is document 732 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; these are its phases 5 and 6, plan 5.C.

- **`board export B --out DIR [--allow-errors]`** (`board.export`): refused with `drc_failed` (its report in `details.report`) while the board has a DRC error; `--allow-errors` writes anyway and the manifest records it. The answer holds every file's `text`; the CLI writes them into `--out` atomically and prints the rest, renaming every file into place and then syncing them together (`defer_syncs`, `flush_syncs`) before it answers, so the package waits on the file system's journal once (#270): synced one by one, fourteen files took 44 s on a busy worker. An `--out` holding files but no `manifest.json` is refused (`invalid_param`); one an earlier export wrote has that export's files removed first.
- **The package**, named for the board's ID: `<id>-copper-top.gtl`, `-copper-bottom.gbl`, `-mask-top.gts`, `-mask-bottom.gbs`, `-paste-top.gtp`, `-paste-bottom.gbp`, `-silk-top.gto`, `-silk-bottom.gbo`, `-profile.gm1` (always all nine), `<id>-pth.drl` and `<id>-npth.drl` (when they have holes), `<id>-bom.csv` (`bom`'s CSV), `<id>-pos.csv` (`Designator,Value,Footprint,X,Y,Rotation,Side`: the footprint's origin in mm, counterclockwise degrees), the preset's assembly forms `<id>-bom-<form>.csv` and `<id>-pos-<form>.csv`, and `manifest.json` (`schema`, the board's and project's revisions, the design's netlist hash, preset, slots, DRC counts, `allow_errors`, `left_out`, each file's name, function, BLAKE3 and size, ryueda's version). DNP, `exclude_from_pos`, unplaced and unreadable footprints are left out of pick and place and listed. No dates anywhere; goldens take the version out (`board.breakout.export.*`).
- **Layers** (`board::fab`): copper per side with `.P`/`.N`/`.C` on pads and `.N` on tracks and vias, `.AperFunction` `SMDPad,CuDef`, `ComponentPad`, `Conductor`, `ViaPad`; masks `Negative` (vias tented); the profile drawn with a 0.1 mm pen. Unfilled circles are round-pen strokes (no board shape has a hole); a custom pad's holed opening is cut into hole-free strips (`fab::fracture`). Fab and courtyard layers aren't exported. Protel extensions and X2 attributes both, as JLC's KiCad guide asks ("Use Protel filename extensions", "Use extended X2 format", "Include netlist attributes").
- **Drills**: plated and unplated files (JLC: "KiCad's separate -PTH.drl and -NPTH.drl files are accepted as they are"), `Plated,PTH,ViaDrill`, `Plated,PTH,ComponentDrill`, `NonPlated,NPTH,ComponentDrill`, `NonPlated,NPTH,MechanicalDrill`. Slots are drilled, `G85` (`export::SLOTS`): gerbv 2.10.0 reads them without a warning and its raster matches (0.57 % of ink apart), and refuses routed ones ("Unsupported G00 (rout mode)", `M15`, `G01`, `M16`); JLC's "How to generate Gerber and drill files in KiCad 9" (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-generate-gerber-and-drill-files-in-kicad-9, read by the coxswain on 2026-11-17) asks for "Use alternate drill mode for oval holes" ("Tick to generate accurate slots and oval holes"), KiCad's G85 form, and no JLC page mentions `M15`/`M16`.
- **The assembly form** is the preset's (`AssemblyForm`: name, supplier, BOM and pick and place columns, length suffix, top side), so the fabricator's name stays in `board/preset.rs`. JLC's, read by the coxswain on 2026-11-17: BOM `Comment,Designator,Footprint,LCSC Part #` (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/bill-of-materials-for-pcb-assembly; the part number as JLC's toolkit spells it, its KiCad guide says "JLCPCB Part #"), several designators in one quoted cell (`"R5,R6"`, no ranges); CPL `Designator,Mid X,Mid Y,Layer,Rotation` (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pick-place-file-for-pcb-assembly), lengths in mm written `12.5mm`, layer `Top`, rotation counterclockwise degrees. The supplier's part number is the line's `LCSC` offer, compared ignoring case and spaces.
- **gerbv** 2.10.0 (Debian trixie), in the devcontainer only (`tests/gerbv.rs`, `RYUEDA_REQUIRE_GERBV=1`, full.yml's `ryueda's package read by gerbv`): every file read without a warning, except gerbv's notes that it doesn't know X2's attribute commands (`Unknown RS-274X extension found %TF`/`%TA`/`%TO`/`%TD`: gerbv predates X2 and skips them, which changes nothing it draws). Its PNG export needs no display. Each layer's raster is compared with `ryu-board`'s drawn at exactly gerbv's scale (the shapes' box grown to whole pixels at gerbv's dpi), both cut to their ink and judged at the closest alignment within a pixel: at most 3 % of ink pixels apart, all within 2 pixels of an edge, about 4,000 pixels along the longer side. Worst share 2.67 % (the generated board's `pth.drl`); the breakout's worst 0.79 %. Masks aren't inverted by gerbv. A layer with no aperture (regions only, or empty) still defines one, unused: gerbv takes a file with no `%AD` for RS-274D.
- **Testing.** Unit tests in `board::fab`, `assembly`, `export`; `tests/cli.rs` both ways; goldens `board.breakout.export.*`; the `board` fuzz target exports too (61,453 runs in 10 minutes in fuzz.yml on GitHub's runner, no crash; its sanitizer build of ryucad needs more than the 8 GB members cap themselves to on shared workers); the bar (cox-worker-2, an AMD Ryzen 7 9700X at a load of about 11, `RYUEDA_BARS=1`: the generated board's fabrication files in 3.2 ms against 1 s, the package through a warm daemon in 331 ms against 1 s).

One fresh session, given only the brief and `--help`, laid the board out through the CLI alone: one round, 57 calls, 2 refused (`usage`: a guessed `board route`; `check_failed`: a `board drc` with findings), 5 minutes, 113k tokens. Change it by running a round again.
- **Goldens.** `board.breakout.*` are the round's board: its pads, renders, clean `drc`, layout, islands and every exported file (no `--allow-errors`). They replaced 5.A to 5.C's hand-placed board. `tests/gerbv.rs` reads both packages: the round's, and the hand-placed one (`gerbv_reads_a_partly_routed_breakouts_package`) for `npth.drl` and the via's paths.
- **The breakout's footprints carry a `reference` field** (silk, 1 mm, above the courtyard), as KiCad's do, so its silk layer draws designators in the built-in stroke font. The hand-written footprints had none, and the round's silk came out empty. The round placed parts without them, so some designators sit over pads or past the edge: nothing judges silk this stage (silk over pads is ryudfm 8's DFM; fabricators clip silk at pads and the edge).
- **What the round found, fixed:**
  - Fourteen commands (`board via delete` and `board hole delete` among them) showed `--expect`'s text as their summary: clap takes a flattened group's doc when a variant has none. Each got a doc comment; `cli::tests::no_subcommand_borrows_a_groups_summary` walks the command tree.
  - `board place --ops` read `"rotation": 180` as millidegrees. That is the convention (JSON integers are nanometres and millidegrees, strings lengths and degrees), but no `--ops` help said so. Every board `--ops` help now does (`board_ops_help_says_how_numbers_read`).
  - The frame (x right, y up, a `--rect` board's lower left corner at 0,0) and that a track ending on another track joins it are in `board create`, `place` and `track add`'s help (`board_help_says_the_frame_and_track_joins`).
  - `board export`'s help says pick and place rotations are the placements' own, with no per-package offsets (`board_export_help_says_rotations_carry_no_offsets`).
- **What it found that waits** (ryueda 7, design 732's scope): copper pours (ground hand-routed as tracks and vias); board-level silk text and moving a designator; courtyards in `board render`; a clearance query between two items. Not faults: an edit that opens a net answers `ok` with `net_open` in `drc.added` (the round read it and fixed it next call); vias aren't checked against courtyards (no such rule this stage).
- **The export's folder.** `write_package` writes the manifest last, and removes an earlier export's leftover files only after it. An export that fails partway keeps the earlier manifest, and the folder is taken again (5.C's review).
- **The manifest's golden** leaves out each file's `blake3` and `bytes`: a release stamps its version into every Gerber's header, so those moved and ryueda-release.yml's golden step failed on `aarch64-unknown-linux-musl` (found by 5.D's dry run). Each file keeps its own key, its version written as `VERSION`.

Local gerbv 2.9.6 (Debian bookworm) gave CI's 2.10.0 numbers to the pixel.

## Stackups and sides (decided in ryueda 7.A)

The design is document 924 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; these are its phases 1 and 2, plan 7.A.

- **The stackup.** `board.json`'s optional `stackup: {layers: [...]}`, top to bottom: `{kind: copper, thickness_nm}` and `{kind: dielectric, thickness_nm, material?, er_ppm?, loss_ppm?}` (permittivity and loss are data for stage 8; nothing computes with them). Valid when it starts and ends with copper, alternates, has 2 to 32 copper layers and every thickness positive; else `board_stackup_invalid` at `/stackup`. Without one a board has `Preset::stackup()`: for `jlc-2l`, 35 µm copper round 1.53 mm of FR-4. `board create --layers N` and `board edit --layers N` write `stackup::even` (N of the preset's copper, equal FR-4 filling its thickness); `--layers 2` writes or leaves none; `board edit --stackup JSON` takes one, its thicknesses as `coord` reads lengths.
- **Copper layers are named by position**: `top`, `in1` … `inN`, `bottom` in files and commands, `copper.top`, `copper.in1`, `copper.bottom` in shapes and findings. A copper entry of the stackup carries no name: a stored name could only disagree with the position every other file names the layer by (design 924 had one; the owner agreed to drop it, question 1192). A track or via naming a layer the stackup lacks is `board_layer_not_found` (the file's fault, or the command's refusal, naming the layers the board has). `board show` lists `layers` only for a board with a stackup of its own.
- **Formats.** `board::FORMAT` stays 1 and `file::NEWEST` is 2. A file is written at 2 only when it holds what 1 can't (`conn::format_of`): a board with `stackup`, a placement with `side`, a track on an inner layer, a via with `from` or `to`. Holes stay 1. Answers print a file at its own format (`file::value`), so stage 5's answers still say `"format": 1`, and every `board.breakout.*` golden is what 5.D left. `merge::newest` reads boards, placements, tracks and vias at 2; `/stackup` is one merge atom, so two stackups conflict whole, never interleaved.
- **Vias' spans.** `from` and `to` (absent: top and bottom). `from` must lie above `to` (`invalid_document` at `/from`; `invalid_param` from `board via add`). A via has a disc on every layer of its span and a hole over it. A span short of the whole board is `drc_via_span` (an error) unless the preset makes blind vias; `jlc-2l` doesn't (`blind_vias: false`: JLC's blind and buried vias are HDI, https://jlcpcb.com/pcb-fabrication/hdi-pcb "4–24 layers", read by the coxswain on 2026-11-18).
- **Pads on inner layers.** A through pad, or a pad on `copper.all`, has copper on every layer; `copper.inner` means every inner layer (`pad::copper_sides`). `board_footprint_unsupported` no longer fires for it. A pad's drill spans the whole board.
- **The copper layout** has one layer per copper layer (`Built::layers`, `layer_name`), inner ones `Side::Inner`; through holes span them all, a via its span. DRC's mask rule judges the outer two (`0` and `n - 1`).
- **Bottom-side parts.** A placement's `side: bottom` (written only then). The footprint is mirrored about its own y axis, then turned by `rotation_mdeg` counterclockwise as seen from the top, then moved (`Place {mirror: true}`); its sided layers swap (`silk.top` to `silk.bottom`, mask, paste, fab, courtyard alike) and its pads' copper sides with them. A bottom pad's reported rotation is the placement's less its own. Courtyards only overlap on one side. Regions keep their orientation through the mirror (`Place::contour` reverses them; a test checks the areas).
- **Pick and place.** `<board>-pos.csv`'s `Side` is `top` or `bottom`; X and Y are board coordinates as seen from the top, never negated; the rotation is the placement's. JLC's form (`AssemblyForm::bottom`) writes `Bottom`: its CPL page (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pick-place-file-for-pcb-assembly, read by the coxswain on 2026-11-18) gives Layer as "Top / Bottom", "the board side where the component should be placed". **The bottom rotation convention is unconfirmed**: neither that page nor JLC's KiCad guide (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/how-to-generate-bom-and-centroid-files-from-kicad-8) says whether a bottom part's angle is mirrored; KiBot's position-file notes and its issue #510 say KiCad mirrors it, JLC doesn't, and JLC has changed its convention several times. ryueda writes the placement's rotation; #999 verifies it in JLC's placement viewer before the first bottom-side order. A change would go in `pos_form` only.
- **Fabrication files.** Copper in stack order: `-copper-top.gtl` (`Copper,L1,Top`), `-copper-in<k>.g<k+1>` (`Copper,L<k+1>,Inr`), `-copper-bottom.gbl` (`Copper,L<n>,Bot`), then the other seven as before; a two-layer board's are `fab::LAYERS`' exactly. Drills: `-pth.drl` (`Plated,1,<n>,PTH`), one `-pth-l<a>-l<b>.drl` per other via span in use (`Plated,<a>,<b>,Blind` when it reaches an outer layer, else `Buried`; its holes `Plated,Blind,ViaDrill`), `-npth.drl` (`NonPlated,1,<n>,NPTH`). The manifest gains `copper_layers` and `stackup` only for a board with a stackup of its own. Renders take `copper.in<k>` on boards that have it (`render::layers(n)`), each inner layer its own colour.
- **Hole clearances (#946).** `ryu_copper::Copper::hole_clearance(layer, below)`: pieces on a layer of a hole's span closer than `below` to its wall, where the hole's plating doesn't join them; an unplated hole, and a plated one's back-drilled spans, join nothing. ryueda's `drc_hole_clearance` (an error) applies the preset's `via_hole_clearance_nm`, `pth_hole_clearance_nm` or `npth_hole_clearance_nm` by the hole's kind, each judged at its own distance, once per hole and item (the closest layer, and `layers`); a hole's own pad or via is never a finding, nor, for a plated hole, copper of the hole's own single net (two islands of one net are its `net_open`). JLC's numbers, read by the coxswain from https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities on 2026-11-18: "Via hole to Track: 0.2mm", "PTH to Track: 0.35mm is recommended, minimum 0.28mm" (the preset takes the minimum), "NPTH to Track: 0.2mm". The page's inner-layer rows ("Inner layer via hole to copper clearance": 0.2 mm, "Inner layer PTH pad hole to copper clearance": 0.3 mm) wait for 7.B's 4-layer preset. The breakout and the generated boards stay clean under it.
