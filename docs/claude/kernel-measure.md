## Measurement (decided in ryucad phase 10)

The design is document 198 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 1, plan 10.A.

- **API.** `ryukei::measure` takes bodies, `measure::Entity` (`Vertex`, `Edge`, `Face`, `Body`) and `Rigid`s, nothing else: `properties(&Body, Entity)`, `distance((&Body, Entity, &Rigid), (&Body, Entity, &Rigid), &DistanceOptions)`, `angle(..)` and `mass(&Body, density, &MassOptions)`. Every number is a `measure::Value`: `Exact(x)` (a closed form, exact up to rounding) or `Bounded { value, bound }`, the true value within `bound`; nothing is reported without one or the other. Refusals are `Error::InvalidMeasure(MeasureError)`: `NotAnEntity` (a dead ID, a measure that doesn't apply), `Unsupported` (a face that doesn't mesh, a mesh past its budget), `Uncertain` (an intersection or a bound that stayed uncertain). Work over faces runs on the current rayon pool and is gathered in ID order: one thread and many give the same bits.
- **Properties.** A vertex's point; an edge's end points and length, exact for a line or a circle, else certified (`measure::length`: the range cut until the tangent turns by at most `θ` across each piece by the curve's `CurveBound`, so each piece lies between its chord `c` and `c / cos θ`; the value Gauss–Legendre's, clamped into that range), with a line's direction, an arc's centre, axis, radius and angle, an ellipse's axes; a face's area (from `mass`'s integration) and its surface's shape (plane with outward normal, cylinder, cone with apex and half-angle, sphere, torus); a body's volume and area.
- **Curve length.** `measure::curve_length(curve, range, rel)` is `length` made public (ryucad plan 13.A): exact for a line or a circle, else certified within about `rel` of the length, `None` for a range that doesn't increase or a `rel` that isn't positive. Datum points along curves bisect against it; its golden section is `curve_length`.
- **Mass.** `mass` integrates area, volume, first and second moments by the divergence theorem on the exact surface: collapsed Gauss rules of 4, 8 and 16 points a side (computed by Newton's method from `math::cos` starts, so the same bits everywhere) over the parameter triangles of a coarse mesh (a thousandth of the size, a normal turn of 0.3), raised until two agree within `MassOptions::tolerance` (1e-9) on every moment; along every boundary segment the sliver between the segment and the fin's pcurve is added (so the domain is the face's, not the mesh's polygon), and at poles and apexes the parameter wedges between the triangles' own pole corners, a wedge across a seam carried over by the period (its corner off the pole two corners at one position). The bound comes from a fine certified mesh (`MassOptions::chord`, by default a hundred-thousandth of the size, a normal turn of 0.15 since v0.9.1, #308): the volume within `2 × (chord + tolerances) × curved area` of the mesh's exact polyhedral volume, first moments that times the reach from the box's middle, second moments its square; areas within each triangle's `1 / cos θ - 1` on curved faces, θ the turn the mesher's angle test certifies over it (`mesh::triangle_turn`; 0.15's where unbounded), plus strips two chords wide along curved edges; each bound adds the value's distance from the mesh's figure. Centre of mass, inertia about it (products with their minus sign) and principal moments and axes (cyclic Jacobi, smallest first, right-handed) follow. A box's bounds are rounding; a sphere's volume is within about 1e-9 and its bound about 1e-4 of it: the bound is rigorous, the value far better.
- **Distance.** Closed forms first, exact where the geometry's own distance is attained inside both entities: vertex to vertex, to a line or circle edge, to an analytic face (the surface's projection inside the face); two straight edges; parallel planar faces where a vertex of one projects inside the other. Otherwise both bodies are meshed (a thousandth of the pair's size, then four times finer, four rounds at most, 4M triangles each), a box tree over each entity's pieces finds the nearest pair (`measure::prims`: points, segments, triangles), less both chords and tolerances a lower bound; its points are lifted onto the exact entities (an edge's parameter from the segment, a face's `(u, v)` from the triangle's corners, the nearest corner where that falls outside the face) and refined by alternating Newton projections, an upper bound and the value. `DistanceOptions::tolerance` (1e-4 of the size) is the bound to reach. Where the lower bound reaches zero, `intersect` decides: `face_face`, `curve_surface` with `point_in_face`, and `point_in_body` for a solid around a point of the other; meeting entities are `Exact(0)`.
- **Angle.** Between straight edges, circular edges' and revolved faces' axes, and planar faces' outward normals, in `[0, π]` with its supplement; a line or axis against a plane is the angle to the plane. Exact from the directions.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (`gauss`, `length`, `mass`, `prims`, `tree`, `distance`, `angle`), `tests/measure.rs` (a torus, a cylinder, a holed plate, a lens and a filleted box against closed forms; a cylinder's edges and faces; seeded balls against their gaps; a box and a torus against sampling; a face far from a solid holding another vertex; coincident balls meeting on their surface; a lens whose cap holds both poles; one thread and four), `tests/measure_props.rs` (release only, its own CI step: seeded overlapping primitives, `vol(A) + vol(B) = vol(A ∪ B) + vol(A ∩ B)` and the first moments within the bounds), the `measure` golden section, and the `measure` fuzz target (seeded booleans' targets and tools: a typed refusal or a value whose bound holds against a mesh's volume and every pair of mesh points). `cargo test -p ryukei --release --test measure_props` runs the property test.
- **Limits.** Mass meshes the body twice; a torus a metre across takes about half a second in release. Since ryukei 15.B's planes its fine mesh (a hundred-thousandth of the size, then 0.05 rad) no longer dominates: `nist_ftc_07`'s committed body weighs in 6.5 s on one thread, from 125.5 s on main, both on cox-worker-2 (AMD Ryzen 7 9700X, shared); step-hunt measured 1,325 s on an Opteron at release/0.7. `ryustep`'s `tests/mass.rs` holds every committed body under step-hunt's 30 s (`RYUKEI_BARS=1`; ten times looser otherwise). A distance bound tighter than about 1e-5 of the size needs meshes finer than four rounds give; the value is the refined pair's either way. Curved-face areas are bounded to at most about 1.1% (`1 / cos 0.15 - 1`), in practice far less: each triangle's own certified turn.

## Mass's fine mesh at speed (decided in the 0.8.x burn-down)

Issues #307 and #308. Measured on cox-worker-1 (Intel Xeon E5-2650, 8 threads, shared at a load of 3 to 6), release, one thread, `examples/check-split` with `CHECK_SPLIT_MASS=1`; every value and bound below is bit for bit main's.

- **What it spent its time on.** The fine certified mesh (a hundred-thousandth of the size): each triangle tested encloses its surface's second derivatives over its parameter box. Three costs dominated, one commit each:
  - subnormal interval bounds (see `kernel-numerics.md`'s No subnormal bounds): FTC 07 33.5 → 19.9 s, KiCad's `RV_Disc_D12mm` varistor 74.8 → 26.4 s;
  - an extrusion's or revolution's enclosure taking its curve's third and fourth derivative nets, cut to the triangle's range and thrown away: `Curve::jet2_box` (`NurbsCurve::jet2_box`, `jet2_piece`) gives the point and first two derivatives alone, by the same arithmetic, so the same bits. The varistor 26.4 → 20.2 s;
  - the constrained Delaunay triangulation's `incircle` sent to 2^1074-scaled big integers wherever a point had a coordinate below `2^-100` (a rounding residue at a seam or pole of a scaled parameter plane): see `kernel-numerics.md`'s Tiny coordinates rescaled. KiCad's `SMA_Amphenol_132291` connector 174.9 → 7.7 s.
- **What's left.** NURBS faces' enclosures (FTC 07 19.6 s, from 33.5) are the patches' own de Casteljau cuts, per triangle.
