## Offset bodies and shells (decided in phase 8)

The design is document 94 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 8.E.

- **API.** `offset::offset_body(key, &body, &OffsetSpec { distance, faces })` moves every face of a solid along its outward normal by its distance (the spec's, or a face's own from `faces` by name), positive outwards: a new body and a journal. `offset::shell(key, &body, &ShellSpec { thickness, outward, open, faces })` hollows it: inwards the body less its offset by `-thickness` with the `open` faces at zero (which lie on the body's own, so the boolean takes them away), outwards the offset by `+thickness` less the body; no open face makes a cavity. Refusals are `Error::InvalidOffset(OffsetError)`: `BadDistance` (not finite, under `10 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION` but not zero, or all zero), `NoSuchFace`, `FaceCollapses`, `EdgeVanishes`, `SelfIntersecting { faces }`, `Unsupported { at, case: OffsetCase }`, `Uncertain { at }`, all made before anything is built; a shell's boolean refuses as booleans do.
- **Faces.** Each face's surface is offset keeping its parameters (`Surface::offset`: analytic surfaces and pipes their own kinds; NURBS, extrusions, revolutions and sweeps an `Offset` over the face's box grown by a tenth each way and at least three distances in model space, or the box alone where that folds), so the face's pcurves are its offset's.
- **Edges** (`offset::classify`). An edge between faces at different distances closes; both at zero, it stays (kept); otherwise it is smooth when its faces' normals at 17 samples are within `SMOOTH_GAP / |d|` of each other (`SMOOTH_GAP` = `10 × LINEAR_RESOLUTION`: the offsets then lie within an intersection fit's allowed tolerance of one curve, the gap added to the edge's tolerance), else convex or concave as `blend::convex` certifies, opening where the offsets move apart (convex outwards, concave inwards) and closing where they cross. An edge tangent in places only is `Unsupported` (`PartlySmooth`). A smooth edge and an opening edge's rails are the edge moved along each face's normal: exactly for a line (normal fixed) or a circle (normal fixed against its axis and radius), else `fit_along` its pcurve on the offset. An opening edge's fill is the surface a ball of radius `|d|` sweeps rolling about it: a cylinder about a line (seam opposite the faces' normals' middle), a torus about a circle (refused, `Fill`, unless the circle is larger than `|d|`), else a pipe about the edge's curve over its interval anchored on the first face's offset (on its rail where that isn't analytic); `Forward` outwards, `Reversed` inwards. A closing edge between two analytic offsets is the branch of their intersection nearest where its middle moves, within the larger distance (`EdgeVanishes` past that; a tangent branch is `Uncertain`), exact where the pair has a closed form; a closed clamped branch of a closed edge is restarted where its vertex goes (`intersect::reseamed`). Any other closing edge is traced from the edge (`edges::traced`): in each section across it, the point on both offsets by Newton's method from the fins' own parameters, with its derivative along `n_A × n_B`, fitted on shared knots and certified on both (`fit_on`), over the edge's interval carried on by three distances along a line or a circle, each end stopping where the section's point leaves either offset's domain, by bisection from the edge's middle (a sweep's offset ends with its profile segment, and a cap edge's ends may already lie past it); `EdgeVanishes` where a section has no such point near where the edge moves. That finds the edge's curve, not every curve the offsets share: the self-intersection check on the result rules out another crossing. (Two offsets of splines ending together, a sweep's laterals at its path's ends, are what the four-parameter intersector can't settle.)
- **Vertices** (`offset::vertices`), by their edges' classes round the fan of corners: only kept and smooth edges, one point moved by the faces' common normal (it keeps its name); opening and smooth, one point per run of corners joined by smooth edges (`V + d n`), the fills ending on arcs of the sphere of radius `|d|` about the vertex in the planes square to their edges, which bound a sphere face with three runs or more, are one shared section with two, and with an edge closed at the vertex let its fill wrap round; closing with smooth or kept, of three faces, the point where one curve (a moved one first) meets the third face's offset, or of two faces the curve's point nearest where the vertex moves (it keeps its name); and mixed, three edges of three faces none smooth: one opening edge's fill ends on the third face's offset along their intersection between its rails' crossings with it (an end curve inserted in that face's loop), two opening edges' fills trim each other along their intersection from their rails' common point on the face they share to where the closing edge's curve meets them (the shorter arc of a closed branch). Refused (`Unsupported`, naming the vertex): `MixedVertex` (opening and closing edges at a vertex of four or more edges, or with a smooth or kept edge), `ClosingVertex` (only closing, smooth and kept edges, four faces or more), `SharedSection` (two opening edges ending on one arc where a sphere is needed, a closed opening edge among them), and a face with a lone-vertex loop (`LoneVertex`, a cone's apex).
- **Build** (`offset::build`). Layout vertices are the new points, vertex by vertex; edges each original edge's (or two rails), cut between the parameters its vertices give (`EdgeVanishes` where they come in the wrong order: a closing edge whose ends crossed), then the vertices' sections, ends and trims; faces each face's offset with its loops in the body's order (rails and moved edges carrying the face's own pcurves, closing branches theirs), then fills and spheres. `layout` builds them, each shell of the body one shell, its voids voids; every fin without a pcurve gets one, exact or fitted (the edge's tolerance covering the fit); the result must pass `check::check` and `check_intersections` (crossing or overlapping faces are `SelfIntersecting`, naming them; pairs it can't check are `Uncertain`). Nothing is repaired.
- **Names.** Faces keep their names, and edges and vertices that are still one keep theirs (`Modified`); a fill is `OffsetRole::Edge` from the edge or `OffsetRole::Vertex` from the vertex it replaces (`Replaced`); rails are `Rail` from their fill and face, sections `Section` from the two fills (sorted) and the vertex, end curves `End` from the fill, the face and the vertex, new vertices `Point` from the edges there, sorted (`Created`). A new distance renames nothing. A shell's faces keep their names, the open faces as the walls' rims (each a face with the opening as a hole), and the offset's are the boolean's tool's, `Placed` under the shell's key.
- **Sweep offsets.** A sweep's third derivatives come from its frame's, over third-order jets in one variable (`geom::jet1`), which read the path's fourth derivatives (`Curve::jet4`, `jet4_box`; `NurbsCurve::jet4` and its Bézier enclosures by the quotient rule to fourth order) and the minimal frame's quintic's third. `Offset::new` takes a sweep base; its cells' enclosures are taken in each cell's tangent frame with the sweep moved rather than its frame sampled again (`Sweep::moved`, `Carry::moved`). Blends still refuse sweep faces: a spine along the path can't be carried past its ends without changing its frame, so it ends on the domain's edge, where the intersection is uncertain.
- **NURBS forms of offsets.** `Offset::to_nurbs_within(box, tolerance)` is a bicubic Hermite net on the offset's own parameters (knots of multiplicity three at a grid of its cells' sides, halved), certified patch by patch by Taylor's formula over parts of it, the patch's second derivatives enclosed from its second differences. Its cost grows with how tightly the offset bends. `heal::seamed` writes an offset of a sweep this way within `pipe_tolerance` (a file can describe an offset of any other surface exactly), keeping its pcurves; ryuformats' USD writes every offset face this way.
- **Testing.** Unit tests per module (classes, fills, rails, vertex classes, the L's mixed corners, refusals; sweeps' third derivatives against differences and their enclosures; offsets of sweeps; NURBS forms against their bounds); `tests/offsets.rs` (a box grown by Steiner's formula and shrunk, grown but its top, a cylinder both ways, an L both ways against the parallel set's volume, a rounded box both ways, shells of a box (open, outward, closed into a cavity), a cylinder cup and a rounded tray, refusals, names); `tests/spline_offsets.rs` (release only, its own step in `full.yml`: a NURBS box and an extruded spline cylinder against the analytic results, a square swept along a wavy path grown by Pappus, and shrunk to the refusal below); golden sections `sweep_offsets`, `offset_bodies` and `shells`; the `offset` fuzz target (`ryukei_testkit::offset_bytes`: bodies as the blend target makes them, or blended, offset or shelled on a coarse grid of distances).
- **Limits.** Offsets and shells where faces vanish, global self-intersection repair, and the vertex classes above: phase 9. An open face beside a fillet whose inner offset doesn't reach it (the wall would need the fillet's offset extended) is `EdgeVanishes`. A sweep along an open path shrunk builds, but its neighbouring sides' offsets, whose domains end together at the path's ends, are `CannotCheck` in the self-intersection check, so it is refused as `Uncertain` (issue #223; phase 9, see References, the race and hardening in `kernel-general-intersections.md`); grown, its sides meet fills and it checks clean.
