## Sweeps along paths and lofts (decided in phase 8)

The design is document 94 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 8.B.

- **Frames.** `geom::FrameLaw` is `Minimal` (rotation-minimizing) or `Binormal(d)` (`e₂` the direction made perpendicular to the tangent). A frame along a path (`path_sweep::Carry`) is a closed form of `s`: a binormal one `e₁ = unit(d × c')`; a minimal one from double-reflection frames (Wang et al.) at samples a turn of at most an eighth of a radian apart within each piece of the path, the reference vector between them the quintic Hermite through its value and the derivatives a rotation-minimizing frame has there (one-sided at a path's knots), `e₁ = unit((c' × r) × c')`, so it is `C²` where the path is `C³` and within about `10⁻⁵` rad per unit of spin of minimizing. A twist turns it about the tangent linearly in `s`. Both are certified defined over the range (stretches halved up to 12 times, 16,384 at most, else `Degenerate`); round a closed path a minimal frame comes back turned (`turned`), which the caller twists away.
- **The sweep surface.** `Surface::Sweep(Arc<Sweep>)`: `P(u, s) = c(s) + Σⱼ (aⱼ + σ(s) bⱼ(u)) fⱼ(s)`, a profile curve (any 3D `Curve` over a range) carried in the frame and scaled about the start frame's origin, `σ` linear from 1 to `scale`. `Sweep::new` certifies `P_u × P_s` non-zero over its whole domain (cells halved across their longer side up to 16 times, 16,384 at most, else `Degenerate`): no fold, no stall. Exact by definition; every surface arm (eval to second order over jets, normals, domain and periods, genus 1 for a closed curve round a closed path, bounds, transform, mirror with `ParamFlip::Kept` and the twist reversed, codec tag 9, dump); `offset` is `None` and `to_nurbs` `Degenerate` until 8.D. Its derivative bounds are the worst of those over its parts between profile pieces and frame samples, a part the normal may turn a radian across halved up to four times (64 parts at most). Projection is `geom::sheet::nearest`, for any sheet: subdivision of the parameters with cells dropped by distance, by a one-signed gradient component, or settled by Krawczyk's test and Newton's method, the domain's edges searched in one parameter and its corners taken, cells a quarter of resolution across settled by their middle's foot, capped at 4,096 cells (then local minima). `Sweep::to_nurbs_within` (export) and `Sweep::rail` (a lateral edge) fit the carried control points by cubic Hermite spans in `s`, certified by Taylor's bound about each eighth of a span's middle.
- **Paths.** `sweep::Path` of `PathSegment`s (key, curve, increasing range): each meeting the next within resolution and tangentially within `ANGULAR_RESOLUTION`, closed when the last meets the first so (or one segment is a whole period; one clamped spline closing on itself is made periodic). Refusals are `SweepError::{EmptyPath, BadPathSegment, PathNotG1}`.
- **`sweep::along`.** `along(key, &profile, &path, &AlongOptions { law, twist, scale })`: the profile's plane more than `ANGULAR_RESOLUTION` from the start tangent (`ProfileAlongPath`), scaled about where the path's start meets its plane along its normal, twist and scale spread over the path by length (round a closed path whole turns and no scale, else `NotClosing`). Each path segment is a leg: along a line with no twist or scale it is an extrude's motion, round a circle whose axis lies in the section's plane a revolve's (planes, cylinders, cones, tori, extrusions, revolutions; a profile touching the axis is `PathTooCurved`); otherwise each side is a `Sweep` (`PathTooCurved` where it folds, `FrameUndefined` where the law is) and each lateral its rail. Legs meet at sections, the profile moved rigidly and scaled: exact curves both sides share. An open path builds as a capped sweep in one stretch per leg (`build::capped`, now in `Ring`s; extrude and revolve build as before, bit for bit); a closed one makes each loop a tube, a lump's outer loop its solid's outer shell and each hole a cavity: a full revolve's ring of bands where one way has one piece (`build::turned`), else a grid closed by KFMRH, MEKR and MEF (`build::closed_ring`). The result must pass `check_intersections` (`SelfIntersecting`, `Uncertain`, naming the first pair).
- **`sweep::loft`.** `loft(key, &sections, &LoftOptions { closed, start, end })`: sections matched by loop key and segment order (`TooFewSections`, `LoopMismatch`, `SegmentCountMismatch`, `BadSection` for one in the plane before it or facing back from it, `BadTangent`). Matched segments become exact NURBS made compatible (`nurbs::skin::compatible`: raised to one degree in Bézier form, mapped to `[0, 1]`, each piece Möbius-reparameterized to end weights 1, given each other's breaks), and the skin (`skin::skin`) interpolates each column of homogeneous control points across the sections at centripetal parameters from the vertices' mean steps: degree `min(3, conditions - 1)`, knots averaged (The NURBS Book's 9.8 and 9.21), end tangents `(w D, 0)`, periodic when closed; weights at or below zero are `BadWeights`. Sides share the vertex columns' interpolants exactly. Open lofts build as one stretch with caps, closed ones as rings; a closed loft of a loop of one closed curve builds as one spline face with no loops (since 8.C). Then `check_intersections` as `along`.
- **Names.** `SweepRole` gains `PathSegment` (a path key's stand-in), `Section` (between two legs' sides) and `JointVertex`. Along: a side from its profile segment and path segment, a section from both sides, a lateral from the profile segments either side and its path segment, a joint vertex from those and the path segments either side; caps, cap edges and end vertices as an extrude's. Loft: a side from its loop and its segment in every section; a lateral and its vertices from the sides either side. Editing the path's shape, twist, scale or a section's size renames nothing.
- **STEP.** `heal::seamed` writes a sweep face as a NURBS within `pipe_tolerance` (`Sweep::to_nurbs_within`, periodic along a closed path it wraps), its bound the face's tolerance; lofts are NURBS already.
- **Testing and limits.** Unit tests per module (frames, `nearest`, the sweep, paths, the builder's stretches, skins); `tests/along.rs` and `tests/loft.rs` (Pappus's volumes along bends, splines, twisted and scaled, holes, closed paths and rings; frustums, prisms; refusals; names; the slow ones release only, each file its own CI step); ryustep's `tests/write.rs` round trips (release only, its own CI step); golden sections `sweeps_along` and `lofts`; the `sweep` fuzz target reads its bytes as a sweep along a path or a loft too (`ryukei_testkit::along_bytes`; those have passed the self-intersection check already, and a run can take a minute or two: `-timeout=600`). Limits: a profile reaching near a bend's centre of curvature can't be meshed at a coarse chord (its normal turns too fast for the estimate's cells); `face_box` of a torus face is the whole torus, so corner tori a diameter apart (a rounded square with corners touching) are `Uncertain`; the self-intersection check dominates, pairs of sweep faces going to the four-parameter tracer: a bar along a spline takes seconds, a seven-sided profile along two tilted arcs about 50 s, and a square a centimetre across swept 3 m from the axis of a 0.2 m bend about 150 s, refused as `Uncertain` (its sides, ribbons 30 times longer than wide, take 40 to 140 s a pair; the `sweep` fuzz target's input `[47, 38, 26, 0, 49, 26, 0, 49, 26, 0, 0, 31, 31, 31, 31, 31]`, past `-timeout=600` under instrumentation) (a sweep's enclosure over a small box costs 5 to 9 µs).
