## Assemblies and instances (decided in ryucad phase 5)

The design is document 72 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 5.A.

- **Assembly parts.** An assembly is a part whose `part.json` says `part_kind: assembly` (part format 2; `kind` was already the file kind), made by `part.create` with `kind` (`ryucad part create <id> --assembly`). It holds only `instance` features (feature format 4): an instance in a part, or a solid, sketch or blend in an assembly, is `wrong_part_kind`. Doctor's list repairs keep the kind, and a lost `part.json` whose features are all instances comes back as an assembly.
- **Instances.** `{type: instance, part, rev, pose, fixed?}`: `rev` is `working` or `{label: L}` (1 to 12 capital letters and digits), `pose` the part origin's frame in meters (`origin_m`, `z`, `x`), solver state written as sketch positions are (`-0` as `0`). Requests take `of` (the part placed; `part` names the assembly), `rev`, a `placement` of quantities and expressions, and `fixed`. Writes refuse a missing part, a label that doesn't resolve now, and a placement that would make an assembly place itself; `part.delete` of a part an assembly places at `working` is `has_dependents`.
- **Labels.** A label resolves through its record (`release::pinned`): to the part's files at the commit where the record became official (that part only, loaded once per commit), else to the working files while its proposed record's content hash describes them; no record is `label_not_found`, anything else `label_not_official`; an obsolete label still places, with an `instance_obsolete` warning (a `check` warning too). Inside a part read at a label, an instance at `working` is `instance_not_released`.
- **Rebuild.** `assembly::Rebuild` rebuilds parts and the assemblies over them, a part before every assembly that places it, over a `Store` (each part's rebuild keyed by its revision and, for an assembly, its children's models, plus feature caches, and the same for pinned parts by part and label) that the session keeps and `check` starts empty. Cycles are found from the files before anything is built (Tarjan over parts at every revision the pins reach), so what fails doesn't depend on which assembly is asked for first. A part's *model* is its solids as shown; an assembly's, every occurrence its instances place at any depth, each with its instance path, part, revision, solids (with the feature shown, which sizes its mesh) and pose. Instances are keyed by their part's model hash; subscribers hear of an assembly when what it places changes.
- **References.** `<assembly>/<instance>/…/<solid>/<name>` names an entity of an occurrence, `<assembly>/<instance>/…` the occurrences an instance places; three parts in an assembly read as an occurrence two deep. Selections, highlights, framing and `inspect` (with a full reference) take them.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `rebuild` reports an assembly's `kind`, each instance's part, revision, pin (`official` and `commit`, or `proposed`), pose and count, and `occurrences`. `assembly.move` (`ryucad assembly move`: a placement, then `translate` and a turn of `angle` about `axis` through its origin; `instance_fixed` for a fixed one) is one logged, undoable change; `assembly.tree` gives the instances as a tree. `mesh.get` gives an assembly's bodies once each, labelled `<path>/<solid>` by the first occurrence placing them, with every occurrence's pose (or one occurrence's, by `feature`); `render` draws occurrences where they are. `view.focus` (`ryucad focus <part>`, `--project`) sets what a participant's window shows (named, else the human, else the caller; in `participant.changed`); with no part asked for, renders, framing and the window show the project's model: its one root assembly (one nothing places), else every part that isn't an assembly.
- **Git and releases.** An instance diffs with its pose summarised (`--positions` lists it); `diff --model` gives an assembly's occurrences `added`, `removed`, `moved` and `changed`; textconv writes `rev = label B` and the pose last. An instance merges per key, its revision one value, its pose from the side that moved it (ours if both); `merge_broke` blames an instance on the side that changed what it places. Releasing an assembly needs every instance, at any depth, at an official label that isn't obsolete (`instance_not_released` among `release_not_ready`'s problems).
- **App.** An assembly's solids are its occurrences' (`<path>/<solid>`), each mesh fetched per occurrence and moved where the occurrence is, so drawing, picking (a pick is the occurrence's reference) and framing work unchanged; the window draws the parts it shows (its participant's focus, else the project's model). The tree says what each instance places (`pa (bolt @ label A obsolete, fixed)` when its label's record is obsolete), with a subassembly's instances under it; View > Show sets the focus; the property panel edits an instance's revision, `fixed` and pose.
- **Testing.** `assembly.rs` unit tests (cycles either way round, labels in a real repository, moves and undo, meshes, renders, diff, focus), merge and validate tests, `tests/recompute.rs` (assemblies incremental equals from nothing at 1 and 8 threads), the `assemblies` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt`, a CLI scenario both ways, the `feature` fuzz target building instances in a project, and the app's `tests/assemblies.rs` and a window test.

## Mates and the solver (decided in ryucad phase 5)

The design is document 72 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 5.B.

- **`ryusketch::core`.** The solver apart from sketches, public: dual numbers generic over their slot count (`dual::D<N>`), dense Cholesky and pivoted QR (`linalg`), the Dulmage-Mendelsohn decomposition (`blocks`), damped least-norm Gauss-Newton whole or in pieces (`solve`), and the diagnosis: freedom, null space (a `Part`'s `vars` and `null`) and minimal dependent sets by group (`diagnose`), over any system implementing `Equations` (what each equation reads, its residual, misfit, gradient and group, each variable's step limit and clamp). Sketches solve through it to the same bits as before (golden hashes unchanged).
- **`ryumate`.** An `Assembly` of rigid `Body`s (a `Pose`, fixed or free), `Pattern`s whose copies (`On::Copy { pattern, seed, index }`, never `[0, 0]`) move with their seeds and the bodies their directions are on, and `Mate`s of six `Kind`s between `Connector`s (a frame on a body or a copy): `Fastened`, `Revolute { angle }`, `Slider { distance }`, `Cylindrical`, `Planar { distance }` (default 0), `Ball`. Each free body has six variables, a scaled translation and a rotation vector applied to its stored pose by the exponential map (Rodrigues in `θ²`, series below `θ² = 0.01`), so a solve starts at zero and an assembly that holds comes back with the same bits. Each mate is rows with exactly one zero near where it holds: `(o_b - o_a) · e_k - c` (lengths), twice the tangent of half the angle between z axes, Rodrigues' vector of the frames' relative turn, and the driven angle's half-tangent; frames exactly opposite can't be judged (infinite misfit), so a component starting there turns the mate's last free body by `(0.01, 0.02, 0.03)` first, and one still there conflicts alone. Translations step at most half the assembly's size, rotations a quarter turn. Stage 2 as sketches. The report is read at the solved poses, where the variables are a twist: each body's freedom as translations and rotation axes through points, in a canonical basis (a direction signed, a plane by its normal, else the assembly's axes); body states `well`, `under`, `over`, `failing`; mate states `well`, `redundant`, `conflicting`, `failing` (a component that doesn't converge with no conflict explaining it); minimal redundant and conflicting sets by mate. `copy_poses` places copies. Frames started up to 2 rad off solve (19-link chains, 40 of 40; 36 of 40 at 3 rad).
- **Files.** `mate` (feature format 4): `form` (a feature file's `kind` is its header), connectors `a` and `b`, `angle` (revolute, cylindrical, planar) and `distance` (slider, cylindrical, planar), expressions over the assembly's parameters. A connector is `{instance: [path], at: {feature, name} | "origin", offset?: {origin, rotate, flip}}`: the path from this assembly down, a copy's first step `<pattern>:<i>` (`<pattern>:<i>:<j>` past the first row) followed by its seed; `at` a solid's founder and stable name in the part at the end of the path. `instance_pattern` (format 4): `form` `linear` or `circular` with a part pattern's fields, `instances` (its seeds), directions and axis `x`/`y`/`z` or a connector's z. Connector frames follow design 72's table (`crate::connector`); a torus, NURBS or swept face, an ellipse or a spline edge is `connector_unsupported`. Assemblies hold instances, mates and instance patterns (`assembly::assembles`).
- **Rebuild.** Instances place at their stored poses, then `mate::settle` resolves every connector in the models the instances place, solves with ryumate (instances fixed as written) and gives each instance its solved pose (a file's pose, `Frame::new` and all), each mate `Output::Mate` (state and both frames) and each pattern `Output::Copies`; `PartResult::solve` holds the report. Moved poses make the assembly stale (`assembly_stale`, naming the instances); a component whose mates can't hold keeps its stored poses and its mates fail with `assembly_unsolved` naming the conflicting sets; a connector that doesn't resolve fails its mate alone (`not_found`, `reference_unresolved` with `part` and `rev`, `dependency_failed`). Copies are occurrences under `<pattern>:<i>/<seed>/…` (`Id::copy`), and entity references take them. A subassembly enters as one rigid body.
- **Writes.** `feature.add`/`edit`/`delete`, `param.set` and `sketch.edit` note which assemblies they may reach that are current (stored poses hold, or nothing to solve) and write back the solved poses of those the write left stale, in the same change (`assemblies` in the response). A write to an assembly that would leave mates that hold unable to hold is refused (`assembly_overconstrained`, with the conflicting mates); edits of a placed part or of parameters keep assemblies current and report what fails. Mates and patterns are checked when written: what they name comes before them, connectors resolve in the current rebuild, a pattern's seeds are instances and its directions aren't on copies, and a mate joins two different instances.
- **Protocol and CLI.** `feature.add`/`edit` take `form`, `a`, `b` (entity references `<asm>/<path>/<solid>/<name>`, or `<asm>/<path>` for an occurrence's frame), `a_offset`, `b_offset` (`{}` for none), `angle`, `distance` (`""` lets either go), `instances` and a part pattern's fields (CLI `--a`, `--b`, `--a-offset`, `--b-offset`, `--distance`, `--instance`). `assembly.solve` (`ryucad assembly solve <asm> [<instance> <move flags>]`) solves without writing, with moves held where put for a first solve, else free from there (`held`). `assembly.move` writes every instance the move moves, held or solved free, in one change. `assembly.tree` gives each instance's state and freedom and each pattern's copies; rebuild reports give the solve, each instance's freedom, each mate's state, form, connectors and frames, and each pattern's copies.
- **Git, check and doctor.** `diff --model` adds an assembly's `solve` (`from`/`to`: state, freedom, whether it holds); textconv gives each connector one line (`a = base : slab/<name> offset origin 0 mm, 0 mm, 2 mm rotate 90 deg flip`); a merge takes each connector as one value (`merge::is_connector`), forms per key, poses as solver state; renumbering rewrites connectors' first steps and patterns' `instances`, never a connector's `at` (another part's solid). `merge.status` reports `assembly_stale` (stale in the merge, current on the sides) with the repair that writes the solved poses. `check` reports `assembly_unsolved` once per assembly (not its mates' `feature_failed`); `doctor` reports `assembly_stale` with its repair.
- **App.** The tree gives each instance its freedom (`pin (bolt @ working, 1 dof)`, `fixed`, `failing`), each mate its form, paths and state (`m1 (revolute: base -> pin, redundant)`, connectors in full on hover), each pattern its copies, and the assembly its solve (`frame (assembly, under, 2 dof, stale)`); copies' solids are under their pattern. Choosing a mate paints the faces its connectors are on. The property panel edits a mate's form, angle, distance and offsets (connectors shown, not changed), and an instance pattern as a part pattern, its seeds instances.
- **Testing and limits.** ryumate: unit tests, `tests/kinds.rs` (each kind's freedom and axes, frames far apart and exactly flipped), `tests/report.rs` (redundancy, conflicts, fixed bodies, copies), `tests/props.rs` (random mechanisms from poses that hold, jostled; stage 2 against whole components), golden hashes (`tests/golden/mate.txt`, in the `cpu-levels` job), the `mate` fuzz target, `benches/solve.rs` (1,000 fastened bolts: 35 ms jostled, 16 ms held). ryucad: `mate.rs`'s tests (solving, staleness, conflicts, patterns, the protocol, moves, keeping current, doctor and check, diff), `connector.rs`'s, merge, renumber and textconv tests, `tests/merge.rs`'s merged assembly left stale and repaired, the `mates` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt`, `tests/recompute.rs` with mates at 1 and 8 threads, a CLI scenario both ways, the `feature` fuzz target building mates and patterns after two instances. App: model and tree unit tests, `tests/assemblies.rs`, a window test editing a mate's angle. Limits: a body more than about 2 rad off may not converge.

## Scale and interaction (decided in ryucad phase 5)

The design is document 72 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 5.C.

- **The codec.** `ryukei::codec`, the kernel's one change in phase 5: `encode_body`/`decode_body` (`ryukei.body.1`: every array as stored, tombstones included, floats by their bits, names, recipes, tolerances, pcurves, each geometry an `Arc` shares written once and shared again on decode), `encode_journal`/`decode_journal`, `encode_mesh`/`decode_mesh` (a `BodyMesh`, whose face and edge IDs ryucad can't build) and `encode_rigid`/`decode_rigid` (a `Rigid` bit for bit, for what a hole or join applied). Decoding rebuilds geometry through its own constructors (exact ones where bits must stay: `Frame::from_axes`, `UnitVector3::exact`, crate-private `Frame2::exact`/`Line2::exact`; a pipe or revolution is certified again), checks `check_topology`, and refuses anything else with `Error::InvalidEncoding { what }`, never a panic. `decode(encode(b))` dumps as `b`. Tests: `codec/tests.rs`, `tests/codec.rs` (primitives, seeded sweeps, booleans, blends, mirrors and healed bodies), the `codec` golden section, the `codec` fuzz target.
- **The disk cache.** `ryucad::cache::Disk`, in the OS cache folder (`$XDG_CACHE_HOME/ryucad`, `~/Library/Caches/ryucad`, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ryucad\cache`) or `RYUCAD_CACHE_DIR`, never in a project. An entry (`v1/<kind>/<2 hex>/<key>`) is keyed by a BLAKE3 of the build's identity (versions, commit, target, the executable's size and time: development builds all say 0.0.0), its kind (`features`, `tools`, `meshes`) and the input hash the session already computes, with the part and feature (alike features of different parts make the same shape under other names); it holds a header, a BLAKE3 of its payload and the payload, written to a temporary file and renamed. A read that fails is a miss and the entry goes (one note); a write that fails is one warning. Reads touch an entry's time; trimming removes the least recently read down to nine tenths of `cache.max_bytes` (10 GiB), on a daemon's first request and after writes pass a sixteenth of it, on a thread. The server's sessions open it (`Session::with_disk`); this crate's unit tests get none unless they name a folder, and every test harness sets `RYUCAD_CACHE_DIR`.
- **What it holds.** A body-making feature's solids (`cache::results`: each solid's body, journal, origins and hash, and what a join, cut or hole applied with its places) by the feature's input hash, a tool's own body by its tool hash, and display meshes by body hash and mesh parameters. `rebuild_part_in` reads beneath its memory cache and writes what it builds; `PartResult::disk` lists what came from disk. Sketches, placements, mates and errors are recomputed: milliseconds from what is cached. `check` rebuilds without it, so its `cache_mismatch` checks the disk cache too. `cache.stats` and `cache.clear` (`ryucad cache stats|clear`) answer through the project's session; the app's Preferences show the size and a Clear cache button.
- **Lazy loading.** 5.A's `assembly::Rebuild` already builds only what a request reaches; the window still loads every part for its tree. A cold start is cheap through the disk cache.
- **Visibility.** A participant's hidden set (`live::Participant::hidden`, in `participant.changed`), kept at the leaves: each occurrence (`<asm>/<path>`) or part solid (`<part>/<solid>`) that `crate::visibility::reach` finds under a reference (a part, an instance at any depth, a copy, a solid). `visibility.set` (`hide`, `show`, `isolate`, `all`; `participant`, else the one human, else the caller) applies `all`, then `isolate` (everything else in those parts hidden), then `show`, then `hide`, all or nothing; CLI `ryucad hide|show|isolate <ref>…`, `show --all`, `--of`. `render` leaves out its `hidden` (`--hidden`), by default `participant`'s set, else the one human's (where an agent's `hide` goes), else the caller's. Session state only, never in Git.
- **Drawing at scale.** The app keeps a mesh in its part's frame, shared by every occurrence of its body (asked for once per body), each drawn at its `core::model::Pose`. The scene uploads a body once per level of detail and draws all its occurrences in one instanced draw (an instance buffer of matrices relative to the scene's reference point, the translation worked in f64); painted faces are drawn at their occurrence's matrix with the same positions, so the same depths. `core::lod` draws each occurrence coarse under 64 pixels across, fine over 512, normal between, one level coarser or finer when `display.quality` is coarse or fine, culls boxes outside the view, fetches missing levels nearest first and draws what it has meanwhile. Picking goes through a box tree of occurrences, then the mesh in the occurrence's frame. A move redraws: it fetches nothing.
- **Visibility in the window.** The window follows its participant's hidden set: hidden occurrences aren't drawn or picked. Tab hides the occurrence (or part solid) under the mouse, Shift+Tab shows those hidden under it (by ray, and by what picking would find there), Ctrl+Shift+Tab (or Cmd+Shift+Tab) shows everything; a text field keeps its keys, and Tab over the viewport is taken from the frame's input before egui sees it (`View::take_tabs`, in `raw_input_hook`), so it never moves keyboard focus. The window applies its own `visibility.set`'s answer at once, as well as the notification. Instance and copy rows have a checkbox (named `Show <ref>`), and rows a menu with Isolate and Show everything; solids a Hide/Show and Isolate menu. Every action is `visibility.set` for the window's own participant.
- **Dragging.** A left-drag on an assembly's instance moves it (in the ryucad navigation style, where left-drag orbits, with Ctrl/Cmd): the grabbed point follows the pointer across the plane through it facing the eye, the window sends `assembly.solve` with that translation held and `component` at most every 50 ms (only what the moved instances' mates reach, through free instances, is solved again, from the poses the last rebuild settled (`mate::Solve::asm`, `ryumate::Assembly::component`), with no rebuild; moves in several components solve each; CLI `--component`) and draws the instances where it puts them (`Model::preview`, until the assembly's next report), and the drop sends one `assembly.move` against the revision the window saw, logged and undoable. A fixed instance, one its mates leave no freedom and a pattern's copy don't move, and the status line says why; a hidden one can't be grabbed.
- **Mate frames.** Choosing a mate draws both its connectors' frames as the rebuild report gives them: x, y and z axes in red, green and blue, a tenth of the model's diagonal long, named `a` and `b`.
- **Testing and limits.** ryucad: `cache` unit tests (entries, corruption, stats, trimming, dead writers' files), `cache::results` round trips, `tests/cache.rs` (a warm cache builds no body and gives the same hashes, a tool's own body from disk, corruption, trimming), `mesh` with a disk, `visibility` tests (reach, apply, the live session, renders), CLI scenarios both ways and a daemon scenario with a watching human. App: model, lod, pick (box tree, hidden picking) unit tests, `tests/settings.rs`, the scene test drawing 1,000 occurrences of one mesh, window tests for Preferences' cache, Tab/Shift+Tab/the tree, a drag written once and undone, and a mate's frames. Limits: a drag previews top-level instances (a pattern's copies follow when the move is written); levels of detail follow the user's display quality in steps, not a budget; the disk cache isn't shared between machines (phase 7).

## Interference and hardening (decided in ryucad phase 5)

The design is document 72 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its plan 5.D.

- **Interference.** `ryucad::interference` boxes every solid of every occurrence where its assembly puts it, takes each pair of solids of different occurrences whose boxes overlap (swept along x, in model order), and intersects them with ryukei (`BooleanOp::Intersect`), the second placed in the first's frame (`pose_a⁻¹ ∘ pose_b`), in parallel. An empty result is clear, a `NonManifold` refusal is `touching` (bodies meeting where a mate puts them), a solid result an interference: its pair (`<asm>/<path>/<solid>`), `volume_m3` from its mesh at normal quality (an estimate: facets turning up to 15° hold a little less), its mesh's box in the assembly's frame, and the faces of each side it touches, followed back through the boolean's journal; any other refusal leaves the pair `unchecked` with ryukei's error. Verdicts are kept by the session (`Session::interference`) by both bodies' hashes and the bits of the relative pose, so asking again, or after moving an unrelated instance, intersects nothing. Faces in contact (a pin in a hole its size, a bolt standing on a plate) are clear.
- **Protocol, check and app.** `interference` (`ryucad interference <asm> [--instance <path>…]`, pairs with a side under those paths; `not_found` for a path that places nothing, `wrong_part_kind` for a part). `check` warns `instances_interfere` for each pair (a press fit is meant) and `cannot_check` for pairs it couldn't intersect, from a cache of its own. The app's Tools > Check interference runs it on the shown assembly, lists each pair with its volume in mm³ and a Show button (framing the faces), paints the touched faces in the removed colour, and asks again when the assembly changes; closing the dialog ends the paint, and a compare doesn't show it.
- **Hardening.** The window draws and picks only the shown parts' sketches, so an assembly's view, like its render, shows none (#95); choosing an instance frames everything it places, at any depth (`Model::framed`, #107); poses are written with axis components within four ulps of 0 or ±1 made exact and origin components at their shortest decimal within four ulps (`Pose::snapped`, in moves and solves), and the Placement form shows axes to 12 decimals and origins in the project's default unit (#108); an instance's cached result is keyed by its label's pin and subscribers hear when only the release branch moved (#105); renumbering a collided solid of a placed part rewrites the connectors theirs aimed at it, through `working` instances, names mapped as references are (#1145); a compare paints an assembly's changed occurrences and ghosts each moved, changed or removed one where it was, from `diff --model`'s `occurrences.sides` and `occurrences.bodies` (#109).
- **The bars.** `tests/assembly_bar.rs` (release only, its own CI step): 1,000 pins of 19 sizes on a fixed plate (1,001 occurrences of 20 parts), each held by one of 1,000 mates (fastened, revolute, revolute driven to 30°); the assembly's rebuild solves in 36 ms when its poses hold and 61 ms from poses a degree and a tenth of a millimetre off (bars 0.1 s and 1 s), and a daemon started cold over a warm disk cache answers `rebuild` in 0.18 s, writing nothing (bar 2 s) (on cox-worker-3: an Intel Core Ultra 5 250K Plus, 18 threads, a quiet disk). `tests/frames.rs` (ignored; hardware) draws the same occurrences while orbiting and zooming in: 123 fps, worst frame 13 ms, on davids-m4's Apple M4 (Metal), the bar's record (bar 60 fps, no frame over 50 ms); cox-worker-3's integrated Arrow Lake GPU (Intel Graphics, Mesa's Vulkan driver) manages only 33 fps, worst 41 ms, so integrated Intel graphics under Mesa doesn't meet the bar. With `RYUCAD_BARS=1` the bars are asserted as they are; CI asserts ten times looser. A request that builds nothing costs 13 ms over the 2,021 files (#25 not hit). `cargo bench -p ryucad --bench assembly` builds the same project (`tests/bar_project`) and prints its machine: the assembly's rebuild held and jostled, and interference from nothing over all 1,001 occurrences (1,000 touching pairs, all intersected): 36, 57 and 17 ms on cox-worker-3.
- **Decided by the bars.** The disk cache holds no assembly results: placing and solving from what is cached takes tens of milliseconds. The rebuild report gives no per-solid boxes: a body's first mesh is fetched once per body, not per occurrence, and the cold start is far inside its bar. The level bands (64 and 512 pixels) stay, and the viewport snapshot gains an assembly with a pattern drawn at its levels (`assembly-lavapipe.png`).
- **Testing.** `interference.rs` unit tests (a bolt through a plate, touching and apart, plates overlapping by a block, the cache, narrowing), `tests/interference.rs` (two balls' lens, a pin pressed into a small hole and one that fits, a pin along x, subassemblies and copies paired within and across, one thread and eight), a CLI scenario both ways, `check` warning, the `interference` section of `tests/golden/rebuild.txt`, the `feature` fuzz target finding interference twice (from its cache and from nothing) on every assembly it builds, the `merge` target's instance and mate modes (poses never conflict); app: `core::interference` and model unit tests, `tests/assemblies.rs` and `tests/compare.rs` against real daemons, window tests for the dialog and a subassembly's framing.

## Clearance (decided in ryucad phase 10)

The design is document 198 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 2, plan 10.A.

- **Clearance.** `clearance` (`ryucad clearance <asm> [--instance <path>…] [--below <length>]`, `crate::clearance`): every pair of solids of different occurrences whose boxes come within `below` (a length in the default unit; by default a hundredth of the assembly's diagonal), from interference's sweep along x grown by it, measured by `ryukei::measure::distance` with the second placed in the first's frame; closest first, each with its distance and both nearest points in the assembly's frame. A pair at `Exact(0)` is `contact` (touching or interfering: `interference` says which); a refusal leaves the pair `unchecked` with ryukei's error. Results are kept by the session by both bodies' hashes and the bits of the relative pose (`Session::clearance`, interference's key). `wrong_part_kind` for a part, `not_found` for an instance path that places nothing, `invalid_param` for a negative reach.
- **Assembly mass.** `mass` of an assembly or an occurrence adds every occurrence's solids at its pose, each part at its own material's density (see Measure, mass and materials in `ryucad-session.md`).
- **Testing.** `clearance.rs` unit tests (a bolt standing on a plate at 0, one 10 mm off, the default reach, a longer one, narrowing by instance, the cache), `mass.rs`'s assembly test, `tests/cli.rs`'s interference scenario, the `measure` golden section, and the `feature` fuzz target finding clearances from its cache and from nothing on every assembly it builds, a pair sharing a volume at 0.

## Occurrence appearance (decided in ryucad phase 10)

The design is document 198 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 5, plan 10.C.

- **Overrides.** An assembly's `appearance.json` colours what it places: `occurrences` by instance path (`p1`, `p1/left`, a copy `ring:2/pin`) or an occurrence's solid (`p1/left/shank`), overriding the placed parts' own appearance there and below, the outermost assembly first; faces are coloured only in their parts. `appearance.get` of an assembly gives each occurrence's solid by `<path>/<founder>` and every part it reaches, subassemblies included, with its revision. An explode, mate, interference, clearance, mass or export never reads it.
- **Testing.** `appearance.rs`'s assembly test (a bolt, a pair placing two, a frame placing two pairs: the frame's, the pair's and the bolt's entries resolved field by field) and the golden `appearance` section's assembly.
