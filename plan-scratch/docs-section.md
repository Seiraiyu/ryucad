
## Mockups (decided in ryucad phase 11)

The design is document 199 in the Coxswain planning project `ryucad-ryukei`; this is its phase 1, plan 11.A. It changes no app code: it sets what 11.B to 11.E draw.

- **Where they live.** `design/app/`: one HTML page per surface (`layout`, `palette`, `feedback`, `progress`, `start`, `form`, `tree`, `shortcuts`, `icons`), all drawn by `mock.js` from one stylesheet (`app.css`) and the glyph table (`glyphs.js`), and the PNGs they render to under `png/<page>/<variant>.png` (eight variants: Drafting, Graphite and Studio light and dark, high contrast light and dark), `png/crops/*@2x.png` and `png/looks.png`. They are images and pages, not design documents. The owner reviews and approves them as cox visuals; later plans' window snapshots are compared with these PNGs by eye, and a PR that departs from one says so.
- **Regenerating.** `python3 design/app/fetch-fonts.py` (the fonts, by SHA-256), `sh design/app/model/make.sh <ryucad>` (the bracket the viewport shows, rendered by `ryucad render`), `python3 design/app/render.py setup` once (Playwright's chrome-headless-shell, and on a host without a desktop libnspr4, libnss3 and libasound2 unpacked into `target/mockup-libs`), then `python3 design/app/render.py`. `python3 design/app/contrast.py` checks every palette.
- **Fonts.** `crates/ryucad-app/fonts/` holds what 11.B embeds: Inter 4.1 (`InterVariable.ttf`), JetBrains Mono 2.304 (Regular), Phosphor 2.1.2 Regular and Fill, their licences and `LICENSES.md`.
- **Glyphs.** `design/app/glyphs.js` is the source of ryucad's 15 CAD glyphs: strokes (polylines, ellipses, arcs, arrows, cabinet blocks) on Phosphor's 256-unit grid with its 16-unit stroke, round caps and joins, each inside the grid with its half stroke. 11.B ports the table to `ui::icon::glyphs` stroke for stroke.
- **Palettes.** `app.css` keeps each look's colours from `ui::style` but for two that fail WCAG AA: weak text (`text_weak`) and the light themes' warning colour are darkened (light) or lightened (dark) until 4.5:1 on the app, raised and frame surfaces, and each look gains `ok` (green), `info` (blue, under-defined sketches and proposals) and `focus` colours. 11.B takes these values into `Look::palette`. High contrast is one palette per theme over Drafting's metrics: black on white with a dark blue accent, white on black with a yellow one.
- **Layout.** The window at 1600×1000: the tree 280 px wide and full height, the viewport 1040 px (65% of the width), the right tabs 280 px, the bottom tabs 170 px under the viewport and the right tabs; menu bar 24 px, toolbar 32 px, tab bars 24 px, status line 22 px, tree rows 20 px in every look. Tiles are square in every look; only controls take the look's corners.
